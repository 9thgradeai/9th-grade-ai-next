Section A: Personal, Possessive and Reflexive Pronouns (1–25)
1. Choose the correct pronoun: “Neither Rahim nor Karim completed ___ assignment.”
A. their
B. his
C. our
D. them
Correct Answer: B. his
Explanation: In traditional competitive-exam grammar, neither…nor referring to two singular masculine subjects takes a singular pronoun. The sentence refers to Rahim and Karim individually.
2. Choose the correct option: “This is the student ___ won the scholarship.”
A. whom
B. whose
C. who
D. which
Correct Answer: C. who
Explanation: Who functions as the subject of the verb won. Use whom when the pronoun functions as an object.
3. Select the correct sentence.
A. Me and he went to the bank.
B. He and I went to the bank.
C. Him and I went to the bank.
D. He and me went to the bank.
Correct Answer: B. He and I went to the bank.
Explanation: A subject pronoun is required because the pronouns perform the action of went. He and I are the subject forms.
4. Choose the correct pronoun: “The manager called you and ___.”
A. I
B. myself
C. mine
D. me
Correct Answer: D. me
Explanation: The pronoun is the object of called, so the objective form me is required. A useful test is to remove the other person: “The manager called me.”
5. Fill in the blank: “This pen is ___, not yours.”
A. my
B. me
C. mine
D. myself
Correct Answer: C. mine
Explanation: Mine is a possessive pronoun that stands independently. My is a possessive determiner and must precede a noun, as in “my pen.”
6. Choose the correct pronoun: “She completed the assignment by ___.”
A. her
B. herself
C. hers
D. she
Correct Answer: B. herself
Explanation: Herself is a reflexive pronoun used when the subject and object refer to the same person. The phrase by herself also means alone or without assistance.
7. Identify the correct sentence.
A. Each of the boys brought their book.
B. Each of the boys brought his book.
C. Each of the boys brought them book.
D. Each of the boys brought themselves book.
Correct Answer: B. Each of the boys brought his book.
Explanation: In traditional examination grammar, each is singular and takes a singular pronoun. Singular they is widely accepted in modern English, but the traditional agreement pattern is often tested in competitive exams.
8. Choose the correct option: “The responsibility rests with you and ___.”
A. I
B. mine
C. myself
D. me
Correct Answer: D. me
Explanation: The preposition with requires an objective pronoun. Therefore, me is correct, not I.
9. Fill in the blank: “The two sisters love ___ very much.”
A. each other
B. one another's
C. themselves each
D. theirselves
Correct Answer: A. each other
Explanation: Each other is a reciprocal pronoun phrase expressing mutual action or feeling. It is appropriate when the sisters love one another.
10. Choose the correct pronoun: “The dog wagged ___ tail.”
A. it's
B. its
C. their
D. itself
Correct Answer: B. its
Explanation: Its is the possessive form of it. It's means it is or it has, so the apostrophe changes the meaning.
11. Select the correct sentence.
A. This is her's book.
B. This book is her.
C. This book is hers.
D. This is hers book.
Correct Answer: C. This book is hers.
Explanation: Possessive pronouns such as hers, ours, yours and theirs do not take apostrophes and do not directly precede nouns.
12. Fill in the blank: “Between you and ___, the proposal is impractical.”
A. I
B. myself
C. me
D. mine
Correct Answer: C. me
Explanation: A pronoun following the preposition between must be in the objective case. Thus, between you and me is correct.
13. Choose the correct option: “The children enjoyed ___ at the picnic.”
A. them
B. themselves
C. theirselves
D. theirs
Correct Answer: B. themselves
Explanation: The reflexive pronoun themselves refers back to the plural subject the children. Theirselves is nonstandard in formal English.
14. Choose the correct pronoun: “It was ___ who informed the authorities.”
A. me
B. mine
C. myself
D. I
Correct Answer: D. I
Explanation: In formal grammar, the pronoun following the linking verb was in this cleft construction is traditionally the subject form I. Informal speech may use me, but formal examinations generally prefer I.
15. Fill in the blank: “The principal praised both Mina and ___.”
A. she
B. her
C. hers
D. herself
Correct Answer: B. her
Explanation: The pronoun is the object of praised, so the objective form her is correct. Remove the other name to check: “The principal praised her.”
16. Choose the correct sentence.
A. Myself will attend the meeting.
B. I myself will attend the meeting.
C. Me myself will attend the meeting.
D. Mine will attend the meeting.
Correct Answer: B. I myself will attend the meeting.
Explanation: Myself can emphasize the subject I. A reflexive pronoun should not ordinarily replace a personal pronoun when there is no antecedent.
17. Choose the correct pronoun: “The house ___ roof was damaged has been repaired.”
A. which
B. who
C. whose
D. whom
Correct Answer: C. whose
Explanation: Whose expresses possession and can refer to things as well as people. Here, it links the house with its roof.
18. Fill in the blank: “The teacher asked Karim and ___ to stay after class.”
A. I
B. me
C. mine
D. myself
Correct Answer: B. me
Explanation: The pronoun is the object of asked. The correct objective form is me.
19. Choose the correct option: “She is older than ___.”
A. me am
B. I am
C. myself am
D. mine is
Correct Answer: B. I am
Explanation: The complete comparison is “She is older than I am.” In ordinary informal English, “older than me” is also standard; however, the supplied option with the complete clause is grammatically correct.
20. Identify the correct sentence.
A. Each student must bring their own identity card.
B. Each student must bring his or her own identity card.
C. Each student must bring them own identity card.
D. Each student must bring themselves own identity card.
Correct Answer: B. Each student must bring his or her own identity card.
Explanation: Traditional formal grammar uses his or her to agree with singular each student. Modern English also commonly accepts singular their, as in option A.
21. Fill in the blank: “The committee members congratulated ___ on their success.”
A. them
B. themselves
C. their
D. theirs
Correct Answer: B. themselves
Explanation: The members are both the subject and the recipients of the congratulation in the intended meaning. Therefore, the reflexive pronoun themselves is appropriate.
22. Choose the correct pronoun: “That is the colleague to ___ I entrusted the documents.”
A. who
B. whose
C. whom
D. which
Correct Answer: C. whom
Explanation: The preposition to requires an objective pronoun in formal English. Whom is the object form of who.
23. Select the correct sentence.
A. The decision is between he and I.
B. The decision is between him and me.
C. The decision is between he and me.
D. The decision is between him and I.
Correct Answer: B. The decision is between him and me.
Explanation: Both pronouns follow the preposition between, so both must be in the objective case.
24. Fill in the blank: “The cat cleaned ___ after eating.”
A. it
B. its
C. itself
D. it's
Correct Answer: C. itself
Explanation: Itself is the reflexive pronoun referring back to the cat. Its indicates possession, while it's means it is or it has.
25. Choose the correct option: “The responsibility is ___.”
A. your
B. yours
C. you
D. yourself
Correct Answer: B. yours
Explanation: Yours is an independent possessive pronoun. Your must modify a noun, as in “your responsibility.”
Section B: Relative and Interrogative Pronouns (26–50)
26. Choose the correct pronoun: “The man ___ you met yesterday is my uncle.”
A. which
B. whose
C. whom
D. what
Correct Answer: C. whom
Explanation: The relative pronoun is the object of met. In informal English, who is also commonly used as an object, but whom is the traditional formal answer.
27. Fill in the blank: “___ of these two books do you prefer?”
A. Who
B. Whom
C. Which
D. Whose
Correct Answer: C. Which
Explanation: Which is used to ask someone to choose between a limited number of alternatives. Here, the alternatives are two books.
28. Choose the correct pronoun: “___ is knocking at the door?”
A. Whom
B. Who
C. Whose
D. Which's
Correct Answer: B. Who
Explanation: Who is the subject of the verb is knocking. Use whom when the person is the object of a verb or preposition.
29. Select the correct sentence.
A. This is the boy which won the prize.
B. This is the boy whom won the prize.
C. This is the boy who won the prize.
D. This is the boy whose won the prize.
Correct Answer: C. This is the boy who won the prize.
Explanation: Who refers to a person and functions as the subject of won. Which normally refers to things or animals, while whom is the object form.
30. Fill in the blank: “The woman ___ son is a doctor lives next door.”
A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Correct Answer: C. whose
Explanation: Whose expresses possession or relationship. It connects the woman with her son.
31. Choose the correct option: “To ___ did you send the letter?”
A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which's
Correct Answer: B. whom
Explanation: In formal English, whom follows the preposition to. The less formal alternative is “Who did you send the letter to?”
32. Fill in the blank: “I do not know ___ he wants.”
A. that
B. which
C. what
D. who
Correct Answer: C. what
Explanation: What means “the thing that” in this sentence and introduces an embedded interrogative clause. The sentence asks indirectly about the thing he wants.
33. Choose the correct pronoun: “___ comes first will receive the award.”
A. Whom
B. Whoever
C. Whomever
D. Whose
Correct Answer: B. Whoever
Explanation: Whoever is the subject of the verb comes. A useful test is to identify the role of the pronoun inside its own clause.
34. Choose the correct option: “Give the prize to ___ you think deserves it.”
A. whomever
B. whoever
C. whom
D. whose
Correct Answer: B. whoever
Explanation: Inside the clause “___ deserves it,” the pronoun is the subject of deserves. Therefore, whoever is correct, even though the entire clause follows to.
35. Fill in the blank: “The book ___ I borrowed from you is fascinating.”
A. who
B. whom
C. which
D. whose
Correct Answer: C. which
Explanation: Which introduces a relative clause referring to the book. In this restrictive clause, that would also be acceptable.
36. Choose the correct pronoun: “The person ___ I spoke was very helpful.”
A. which
B. to whom
C. whose
D. who
Correct Answer: B. to whom
Explanation: In formal English, the preposition can appear before whom: “the person to whom I spoke.” A more conversational version is “the person who I spoke to.”
37. Select the correct sentence.
A. Whom do you think will win?
B. Who do you think will win?
C. Whose do you think will win?
D. Which do you think will win him?
Correct Answer: B. Who do you think will win?
Explanation: The pronoun is the subject of will win within the embedded clause. The phrase “do you think” does not change that grammatical role.
38. Fill in the blank: “I will support ___ you choose.”
A. whoever
B. whomever
C. whose
D. who
Correct Answer: B. whomever
Explanation: In the clause “you choose ___,” the pronoun is the object of choose. The objective form whomever is therefore appropriate in formal grammar.
39. Choose the correct pronoun: “The village ___ I was born is now a town.”
A. which
B. where
C. who
D. whose
Correct Answer: B. where
Explanation: Where refers to a place and means “in which” in this context. The sentence could also be written “the village in which I was born.”
40. Fill in the blank: “___ of the candidates has submitted the form?”
A. Whom
B. Which
C. Whose
D. Who's
Correct Answer: B. Which
Explanation: Which asks about a person selected from a known group of candidates. Whose asks about possession, and who's means who is or who has.
41. Choose the correct option: “The reason ___ he resigned remains unclear.”
A. whom
B. whose
C. why
D. who
Correct Answer: C. why
Explanation: Why introduces a clause explaining a reason. “The reason that he resigned” and “the reason he resigned” are also grammatical constructions.
42. Fill in the blank: “This is the best film ___ I have ever seen.”
A. who
B. whom
C. that
D. whose
Correct Answer: C. that
Explanation: After a superlative such as best, that is commonly preferred in restrictive relative clauses. The relative pronoun is the object of seen.
43. Choose the correct pronoun: “The candidate ___ application was rejected appealed to the authority.”
A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Correct Answer: C. whose
Explanation: Whose indicates that the application belongs to the candidate. It can be used with people and, in many contexts, things.
44. Select the correct sentence.
A. The person which called you is here.
B. The person whom called you is here.
C. The person who called you is here.
D. The person whose called you is here.
Correct Answer: C. The person who called you is here.
Explanation: Who is the subject of called. The object form whom cannot serve as the subject of this clause.
45. Fill in the blank: “I cannot understand ___ you mean.”
A. whom
B. whose
C. what
D. which's
Correct Answer: C. what
Explanation: What refers to the meaning or thing being communicated. It introduces an embedded clause functioning as the object of understand.
46. Choose the correct option: “The officer with ___ I discussed the matter was courteous.”
A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Correct Answer: B. whom
Explanation: The preposition with takes the objective relative pronoun whom in formal English. The person is the object of the preposition.
47. Fill in the blank: “Take ___ you need from the cupboard.”
A. whichever
B. whoever
C. whomever
D. whose
Correct Answer: A. whichever
Explanation: Whichever refers to a choice among available items. It is suitable because the object being chosen is understood to be a thing.
48. Choose the correct pronoun: “___ of the two routes is shorter?”
A. Who
B. Whom
C. Which
D. Whose
Correct Answer: C. Which
Explanation: Which is used when selecting between two or more identified alternatives. Who and whom refer to people.
49. Choose the correct option: “The man, ___ lives next door, is a banker.”
A. that
B. which
C. who
D. whom
Correct Answer: C. who
Explanation: A non-defining relative clause adds extra information and is separated by commas. In standard formal English, that is not used in non-defining relative clauses.
50. Fill in the blank: “She is the only person ___ can solve this problem.”
A. whom
B. which
C. whose
D. that
Correct Answer: D. that
Explanation: That is appropriate in this restrictive clause after the only person. Who would also be grammatical, but it is not among the options.
Section C: Indefinite, Demonstrative and Distributive Pronouns (51–75)
51. Choose the correct pronoun: “___ of the two brothers is honest.”
A. Every
B. Either
C. All
D. Many
Correct Answer: B. Either
Explanation: Either refers to one or the other of two people or things. It is singular in this construction and takes a singular verb.
52. Fill in the blank: “___ of the information was useful.”
A. Many
B. Several
C. Much
D. Few
Correct Answer: C. Much
Explanation: Information is an uncountable noun, so much is appropriate. Many and few are normally used with countable plural nouns.
53. Choose the correct option: “___ has its own advantages and disadvantages.”
A. Every
B. Each
C. All
D. Many
Correct Answer: B. Each
Explanation: Each is a distributive pronoun that refers to members individually. It takes a singular verb and can be used with its when the reference is to things.
54. Select the correct sentence.
A. Neither of the answers are correct.
B. Neither of the answers is correct.
C. Neither of the answer are correct.
D. Neither answers is correct.
Correct Answer: B. Neither of the answers is correct.
Explanation: In traditional competitive-exam grammar, neither is treated as singular and takes is. In some informal contexts, plural agreement occurs, but singular agreement is the safer examination choice.
55. Fill in the blank: “There are five applicants; ___ has submitted the required documents.”
A. every
B. each
C. all
D. many
Correct Answer: B. each
Explanation: Each can stand independently as a pronoun and emphasizes individuals separately. Every is a determiner and ordinarily requires a noun after it.
56. Choose the correct pronoun: “Some students passed, but ___ failed.”
A. another
B. the others
C. other
D. each other
Correct Answer: B. the others
Explanation: The others refers to the remaining members of a specific group. Here, it means the students who did not pass.
57. Choose the correct option: “I need ___ to write with.”
A. something
B. someone
C. somewhere
D. somebody
Correct Answer: A. something
Explanation: Something refers to an unspecified thing. Since the speaker needs an object for writing, it is the appropriate indefinite pronoun.
58. Fill in the blank: “___ knows the answer should raise a hand.”
A. Whoever
B. Whomever
C. Whosever
D. Whichever
Correct Answer: A. Whoever
Explanation: Whoever is the subject of knows. It introduces a clause referring to any person who meets the stated condition.
59. Choose the correct pronoun: “I have two pens; you may take ___.”
A. neither
B. either
C. every
D. all
Correct Answer: B. either
Explanation: Either means one or the other of two alternatives. Neither means not one and not the other.
60. Select the correct sentence.
A. Everyone have their own opinion.
B. Everyone has their own opinion.
C. Everyone have his own opinions.
D. Everyone are entitled to opinion.
Correct Answer: B. Everyone has their own opinion.
Explanation: Everyone is grammatically singular and takes has. Singular their is widely accepted in contemporary English and avoids specifying gender.
61. Fill in the blank: “Few people attended the meeting, and ___ stayed until the end.”
A. little
B. much
C. none
D. every
Correct Answer: C. none
Explanation: None means not one or not any of a group. It can be used with plural countable nouns or uncountable nouns.
62. Choose the correct pronoun: “___ of the two proposals is acceptable.”
A. Neither
B. All
C. Several
D. Many
Correct Answer: A. Neither
Explanation: Neither means not either of two alternatives. It is conventionally followed by a singular verb in formal examination grammar.
63. Fill in the blank: “The two teams congratulated ___ after the match.”
A. themselves
B. each other
C. itself
D. one
Correct Answer: B. each other
Explanation: Each other expresses a reciprocal relationship between the teams. Themselves would suggest that each team acted upon itself.
64. Choose the correct option: “___ is responsible for this decision?”
A. Whom
B. Whose
C. Who
D. Which's
Correct Answer: C. Who
Explanation: Who is the subject complement in this question after the linking verb is. The objective form whom is not appropriate here.
65. Choose the correct pronoun: “I have read several reports, but ___ of them was convincing.”
A. none
B. no one
C. neither
D. every
Correct Answer: A. none
Explanation: None refers to zero members of a group and can take a singular verb when the emphasis is on each item or a singular interpretation. The sentence uses the singular was.
66. Fill in the blank: “One should always keep ___ promises.”
A. his or her
B. one's
C. theirselves
D. oneself's
Correct Answer: B. one's
Explanation: One's is the possessive form of the indefinite pronoun one. Formal writing generally maintains agreement between one and one's.
67. Choose the correct option: “___ of the candidates were selected for the final interview.”
A. Each
B. Every
C. Several
D. Either
Correct Answer: C. Several
Explanation: Several means more than two but not many, and it takes a plural verb. Each and every are singular.
68. Identify the correct sentence.
A. This are my books.
B. These is my book.
C. These are my books.
D. Those is my books.
Correct Answer: C. These are my books.
Explanation: These is the plural demonstrative pronoun corresponding to this. It takes a plural verb and refers to things near the speaker.
69. Fill in the blank: “___ is the road to the railway station.”
A. Those
B. These
C. This
D. Them
Correct Answer: C. This
Explanation: This is a singular demonstrative pronoun used for something near the speaker. Its plural form is these.
70. Choose the correct pronoun: “Of all the applicants, ___ performed best.”
A. whoever
B. whichever
C. the one
D. whom
Correct Answer: C. the one
Explanation: The one can refer to a particular individual from a group. It is the only option that fits the sentence naturally without changing its intended structure.
71. Choose the correct option: “Not everyone agreed, but ___ supported the proposal.”
A. some
B. much
C. every
D. each of
Correct Answer: A. some
Explanation: Some is an indefinite pronoun referring to an unspecified number of people. It contrasts naturally with not everyone.
72. Fill in the blank: “There is ___ in the room; everyone has left.”
A. somebody
B. anybody
C. nobody
D. everybody
Correct Answer: C. nobody
Explanation: Nobody means no person. The second clause confirms that the room is empty of people.
73. Choose the correct pronoun: “You may choose ___ of the three available seats.”
A. either
B. neither
C. any
D. every
Correct Answer: C. any
Explanation: Any is appropriate when choosing one item from a group of three or more. Either traditionally refers to a choice between two alternatives.
74. Select the correct sentence.
A. Each of the players received a medal.
B. Each of the players received medals each.
C. Every of the players received a medal.
D. Each players received a medal.
Correct Answer: A. Each of the players received a medal.
Explanation: Each of + plural noun takes a singular verb. Every cannot normally replace each directly before of.
75. Fill in the blank: “The two alternatives are good; ___ will serve our purpose.”
A. neither
B. either
C. none
D. no one
Correct Answer: B. either
Explanation: Either indicates that one or the other alternative is suitable. Neither would mean that both alternatives are unsuitable.
Section D: Pronoun Agreement and Antecedent Reference (76–100)
76. Choose the correct pronoun: “The jury delivered ___ verdict.”
A. their
B. its
C. them
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. its
Explanation: When a collective noun is treated as a single unit, it takes a singular pronoun. Here, the jury acts as one body delivering one verdict.
77. Fill in the blank: “Neither the teacher nor the students brought ___ books.”
A. his
B. her
C. their
D. its
Correct Answer: C. their
Explanation: With neither…nor, traditional agreement often follows the nearer subject. Because students is plural and nearest to the blank, their is appropriate.
78. Choose the correct pronoun: “Every boy and every girl must bring ___ own lunch.”
A. their
B. his or her
C. them
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. his or her
Explanation: In traditional formal grammar, every boy and every girl is treated distributively, so his or her is the expected pronoun. Contemporary English may use singular their for inclusivity.
79. Identify the correct sentence.
A. The team celebrated their victory as a single unit.
B. The team celebrated its victory as a single unit.
C. The team celebrated them victory as a single unit.
D. The team celebrated themselves victory as a single unit.
Correct Answer: B. The team celebrated its victory as a single unit.
Explanation: A collective noun takes a singular pronoun when its members are considered as one whole. British English sometimes uses plural agreement when emphasizing individual members.
80. Choose the correct option: “Neither of the girls has completed ___ homework.”
A. theirselves
B. her
C. them
D. hers
Correct Answer: B. her
Explanation: In traditional examination grammar, neither is singular and the feminine antecedent girls supports her when the individuals are considered separately. Modern English also permits singular their.
81. Fill in the blank: “The committee has submitted ___ report.”
A. their
B. its
C. them
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. its
Explanation: The committee is treated as a single unit, so the singular possessive determiner its is appropriate. The sentence emphasizes one collective report.
82. Choose the correct pronoun: “The number of applicants has increased, and ___ is encouraging.”
A. they
B. them
C. it
D. their
Correct Answer: C. it
Explanation: The antecedent is the increase in the number of applicants, understood as a situation or fact. The singular pronoun it refers to that situation.
83. Choose the correct sentence.
A. Every one of the employees must submit their form.
B. Every one of the employees must submits his form.
C. Every one of the employees must submit his or her form.
D. Every one of the employees must submitted their form.
Correct Answer: C. Every one of the employees must submit his or her form.
Explanation: Every one is singular and is followed by the base verb after the modal must. The traditional formal pronoun is his or her, though singular their is also common today.
84. Fill in the blank: “The police have arrested the suspect, but ___ have not released further details.”
A. it
B. he
C. they
D. she
Correct Answer: C. they
Explanation: Police is a plural-form noun in standard English and generally takes a plural pronoun. It refers to the officers collectively.
85. Choose the correct pronoun: “The United States has changed ___ policy.”
A. their
B. its
C. them
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. its
Explanation: The name of a country is normally treated as singular when it represents the state as a political entity. Therefore, its policy is correct.
86. Choose the correct option: “Neither of the two machines is functioning; ___ needs repair.”
A. they
B. them
C. each
D. it
Correct Answer: D. it
Explanation: The clause treats each machine as a singular item, and it refers to either machine in the singular. The sentence does not require a plural pronoun.
87. Fill in the blank: “The captain, along with the players, gave ___ opinion.”
A. their
B. his
C. them
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. his
Explanation: The main subject is the captain; the phrase along with the players does not change the subject's number. In traditional grammar, the singular subject takes a singular pronoun.
88. Choose the correct pronoun: “A person should be careful about what ___ says online.”
A. they
B. them
C. theirselves
D. theirs
Correct Answer: A. they
Explanation: Singular they is standard contemporary English for a person whose gender is unspecified. Traditional exam questions may instead expect he or she, but they is widely accepted today.
89. Select the correct sentence.
A. The furniture has lost their shine.
B. The furniture has lost its shine.
C. The furniture have lost its shine.
D. The furniture has lost them shine.
Correct Answer: B. The furniture has lost its shine.
Explanation: Furniture is an uncountable singular noun. It takes the singular verb has and the possessive determiner its.
90. Fill in the blank: “The audience took ___ seats before the performance.”
A. its
B. their
C. it
D. itself
Correct Answer: B. their
Explanation: The audience members are considered individually as people taking their respective seats. A plural possessive pronoun is therefore natural, particularly in British English.
91. Choose the correct option: “Each of the books has ___ own value.”
A. their
B. its
C. them
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. its
Explanation: Each is singular, so the corresponding possessive determiner is its. The sentence considers the books individually.
92. Identify the correct sentence.
A. The scissors has lost its sharpness.
B. The scissors have lost their sharpness.
C. The scissors has lost their sharpness.
D. The scissors have lost its sharpness.
Correct Answer: B. The scissors have lost their sharpness.
Explanation: Scissors normally takes a plural verb when referring to the tool. Accordingly, the plural pronoun their is appropriate.
93. Fill in the blank: “The book and the pen are on the table; ___ belong to me.”
A. it
B. they
C. each
D. itself
Correct Answer: B. they
Explanation: The antecedent contains two coordinated nouns, making it plural. The pronoun they correctly refers to both objects.
94. Choose the correct pronoun: “The principal and secretary gave ___ separate reports.”
A. his
B. her
C. their
D. its
Correct Answer: C. their
Explanation: The phrase the principal and secretary ordinarily denotes two people when the roles are coordinated in this way. The plural pronoun their refers to both.
95. Choose the correct option: “One of the girls has forgotten ___ umbrella.”
A. theirselves
B. her
C. them
D. hers
Correct Answer: B. her
Explanation: The head of the phrase is one, which is singular, and the antecedent is identified as one of the girls. In traditional exam grammar, her is the expected choice.
96. Fill in the blank: “The company announced that ___ would open a new branch.”
A. they
B. them
C. it
D. their
Correct Answer: C. it
Explanation: A company is treated as a single organization in standard American English and formal international business English. Therefore, it is appropriate.
97. Choose the correct pronoun: “The rich should help ___ who are in need.”
A. them
B. those
C. their
D. themselves
Correct Answer: B. those
Explanation: Those is a demonstrative pronoun referring to people. The phrase “those who are in need” is a standard construction.
98. Select the correct sentence.
A. One must do their duty.
B. One must do one's duty.

D. Myself and the manager attended the conference.
Correct Answer: B. The manager and I attended the conference.
Explanation: The pronouns form the subject of attended, so the subject form I is required. Myself should not be used as a substitute for I in this context.
192. Fill in the blank: “The girl ___ father is a professor won the scholarship.”
A. who
B. whom
C. whose
D. which
Correct Answer: C. whose
Explanation: Whose indicates possession or family relationship. It links the girl to her father.
193. Choose the correct option: “They looked at one another and smiled, ___?”
A. did they
B. didn't they
C. hadn't they
D. weren't they
Correct Answer: B. didn't they
Explanation: The main clause is affirmative and uses the past simple verb looked, so the negative question tag is didn't they. The reciprocal phrase one another does not affect the tag.
194. Identify the correct sentence.
A. Let you and I resolve the issue.
B. Let you and me resolve the issue.
C. Let yourself and I resolve the issue.
D. Let yours and me resolve the issue.
Correct Answer: B. Let you and me resolve the issue.
Explanation: In the construction let + object, the pronoun takes the objective case. Therefore, me is correct rather than I.
195. Fill in the blank: “The person ___ the award was presented thanked the committee.”
A. who
B. whom
C. to whom
D. whose
Correct Answer: C. to whom
Explanation: The award was presented to the recipient. In formal English, the preposition can precede the objective relative pronoun: to whom.
196. Choose the correct option: “Neither of the brothers has brought ___ passport.”
A. theirselves
B. his
C. them
D. theirs
Correct Answer: B. his
Explanation: Traditional competitive-exam grammar treats neither as singular and expects his when referring to the two brothers. Modern English may use singular their for gender-neutral agreement.
197. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The book which cover is torn belongs to me.
B. The book whose cover is torn belongs to me.
C. The book whom cover is torn belongs to me.
D. The book who's cover is torn belongs to me.
Correct Answer: B. The book whose cover is torn belongs to me.
Explanation: Whose is the possessive relative pronoun and can refer to things as well as people. Who's means who is or who has, not possession.
198. Fill in the blank: “The two parties must settle the dispute between ___.”
A. them
B. themselves
C. their
D. theirs
Correct Answer: A. them
Explanation: The preposition between takes the objective pronoun them. The phrase between themselves is also possible in other contexts, but here the direct object of the preposition is the parties.
199. Choose the correct option: “It was ___ who first proposed the idea.”
A. them
B. their
C. they
D. theirs
Correct Answer: C. they
Explanation: In formal grammar, the pronoun in this cleft construction is traditionally the subject form they. The sentence emphasizes that they were the ones who proposed the idea.
200. Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
A. Whom do you think is the most suitable candidate?
B. Who do you think is the most suitable candidate?
C. Whose do you think is the most suitable candidate?
D. Which whom do you think is the most suitable candidate?
Correct Answer: B. Who do you think is the most suitable candidate?
Explanation: Who is the subject complement associated with is the most suitable candidate in the embedded clause. The words do you think do not make who an object; this is a common advanced pronoun-case trap.
