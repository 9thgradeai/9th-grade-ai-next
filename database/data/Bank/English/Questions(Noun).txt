Section A: Types and Classification of Nouns (1–15)
1. Which of the following is an abstract noun?
A. Chair
B. Honesty
C. Garden
D. Soldier
Correct Answer: B. Honesty
Explanation: An abstract noun names a quality, state, idea, or feeling that cannot be physically touched. Honesty, wisdom, courage, and freedom are abstract nouns.
2. Which of the following is a collective noun?
A. Member
B. Player
C. Committee
D. Citizen
Correct Answer: C. Committee
Explanation: A collective noun refers to a group considered as a unit. Committee, team, jury, and flock are collective nouns.
3. Identify the proper noun in the following sentence: “The Padma flows through Bangladesh.”
A. Flows
B. River
C. Bangladesh
D. Through
Correct Answer: C. Bangladesh
Explanation: A proper noun is the specific name of a person, place, institution, or thing. Bangladesh is a proper noun; river is a common noun.
4. Which of the following is a material noun?
A. Kindness
B. Gold
C. Crowd
D. Village
Correct Answer: B. Gold
Explanation: A material noun names a substance from which things may be made. Gold, silver, iron, and cotton are examples.
5. Which word is a common noun?
A. Dhaka
B. Monday
C. Teacher
D. Shakespeare
Correct Answer: C. Teacher
Explanation: A common noun names a general class of person, place, or thing. Teacher is a common noun, whereas Dhaka and Shakespeare are proper nouns.
6. In the sentence “Wisdom is better than strength,” which words are abstract nouns?
A. Better and than
B. Wisdom and strength
C. Is and better
D. Than and strength
Correct Answer: B. Wisdom and strength
Explanation: Both words name qualities rather than physical objects. Abstract nouns can function as subjects or complements in sentences.
7. Which of the following is a concrete noun?
A. Poverty
B. Beauty
C. Childhood
D. Bicycle
Correct Answer: D. Bicycle
Explanation: A concrete noun denotes something that can be experienced through the senses. A bicycle is a physical object, whereas poverty, beauty, and childhood are abstract concepts or states.
8. Which noun represents a group of cattle?
A. Herd
B. Fleet
C. Pack
D. Bouquet
Correct Answer: A. Herd
Explanation: Herd is the collective noun commonly used for cattle and certain other animals. A fleet refers to ships or vehicles, a pack to animals such as wolves, and a bouquet to flowers.
9. Which of the following is an uncountable noun in standard English?
A. Chair
B. Suggestion
C. Furniture
D. Coin
Correct Answer: C. Furniture
Explanation: Furniture is generally uncountable in standard English. We say some furniture or three pieces of furniture, not three furnitures.
10. Which of the following is a compound noun?
A. Happiness
B. Toothbrush
C. Beautiful
D. Quickly
Correct Answer: B. Toothbrush
Explanation: A compound noun consists of two or more elements functioning as one noun. Toothbrush combines tooth and brush.
11. Which word is an abstract noun formed from an adjective?
A. Darkness
B. Darken
C. Darkly
D. Dark
Correct Answer: A. Darkness
Explanation: Darkness is the noun formed from the adjective dark. The suffix -ness frequently converts adjectives into abstract nouns, as in kindness and weakness.
12. Which of the following is a noun of multitude?
A. Army
B. Courage
C. Copper
D. Intelligence
Correct Answer: A. Army
Explanation: Army denotes a large organized body of people and functions as a collective noun. The other choices denote abstract qualities or a material.
13. Which word is a verbal noun in the sentence “Swimming is good exercise”?
A. Is
B. Good
C. Swimming
D. Exercise
Correct Answer: C. Swimming
Explanation: Swimming functions as a noun because it names an activity and serves as the subject. The same -ing form can function differently depending on its use in a sentence.
14. Which of the following is a countable noun?
A. Advice
B. Information
C. Luggage
D. Recommendation
Correct Answer: D. Recommendation
Explanation: A recommendation can be counted: one recommendation, two recommendations. Advice, information, and luggage are generally uncountable in standard English.
15. In “The rich should help the poor,” what does “the rich” mean?
A. A rich person only
B. Rich people as a group
C. A rich object
D. A rich quality
Correct Answer: B. Rich people as a group
Explanation: The definite article the followed by an adjective can represent a whole class of people. Such expressions normally take plural verb agreement: The rich are not always happy.
Section B: Singular and Plural Nouns (16–35)
16. What is the correct plural form of “criterion”?
A. Criterions
B. Criterias
C. Criteria
D. Criteriones
Correct Answer: C. Criteria
Explanation: Criterion has the irregular plural criteria. Other Greek-derived examples include phenomenon → phenomena and bacterium → bacteria.
17. Choose the correct plural of “phenomenon.”
A. Phenomenons
B. Phenomena
C. Phenomenas
D. Phenomenae
Correct Answer: B. Phenomena
Explanation: The standard plural of phenomenon is phenomena. In formal examination English, remember the pattern -on → -a for these words.
18. What is the plural of “analysis”?
A. Analysises
B. Analysiseses
C. Analysae
D. Analyses
Correct Answer: D. Analyses
Explanation: Analysis becomes analyses in the plural. Similar patterns include crisis → crises and thesis → theses.
19. Choose the correct plural form of “mouse” when referring to the animal.
A. Mouses
B. Mice
C. Meese
D. Mouse
Correct Answer: B. Mice
Explanation: The irregular plural of the animal noun mouse is mice. In computing, mice and mouses are both encountered, but mice is the traditional standard plural.
20. What is the correct plural of “ox”?
A. Oxes
B. Oxens
C. Oxen
D. Oxies
Correct Answer: C. Oxen
Explanation: Oxen is the established plural of ox. It is an irregular form that does not follow the usual addition of -s or -es.
21. Choose the correct plural of “thief.”
A. Thiefs
B. Thieves
C. Thievs
D. Thiefes
Correct Answer: B. Thieves
Explanation: Many nouns ending in consonant + y change y to ies, while certain nouns ending in -f or -fe change to -ves. However, not every -f noun follows this pattern; for example, roofs.
22. What is the plural of “chief”?
A. Chiefs
B. Chieves
C. Chiefes
D. Chieveses
Correct Answer: A. Chiefs
Explanation: Chief forms its plural by adding -s: chiefs. Do not apply the -ves rule mechanically to all nouns ending in -f.
23. Which is the correct plural form of “passer-by”?
A. Passers-bys
B. Passer-bys
C. Passers-by
D. Passer-bies
Correct Answer: C. Passers-by
Explanation: In many compound nouns, the principal noun takes the plural ending. In passer-by, passer is the principal noun, so the plural is passers-by.
24. What is the plural of “mother-in-law”?
A. Mother-in-laws
B. Mothers-in-law
C. Mothers-in-laws
D. Mother-ins-law
Correct Answer: B. Mothers-in-law
Explanation: In compounds containing a noun followed by a preposition, the main noun normally takes the plural ending. Thus, mother-in-law becomes mothers-in-law.
25. Choose the correct plural of “commander-in-chief.”
A. Commander-in-chiefs
B. Commander-ins-chief
C. Commanders-in-chiefs
D. Commanders-in-chief
Correct Answer: D. Commanders-in-chief
Explanation: The principal noun commander is pluralized, while in-chief remains unchanged. The same principle applies to many titles and compound nouns.
26. Which is the correct plural of “passer-by” when describing several people passing a place?
A. Passers-by
B. Passer-bys
C. Passers-bies
D. Passer-by's
Correct Answer: A. Passers-by
Explanation: The plural ending belongs to passer, the head noun. An apostrophe is not used to form ordinary plurals.
27. What is the correct plural of “deer”?
A. Deers
B. Deeres
C. Deer
D. Deeren
Correct Answer: C. Deer
Explanation: Deer has the same singular and plural forms in standard usage. Other examples of unchanged plural forms include sheep and species.
28. Choose the correct plural of “species.”
A. Specieses
B. Species
C. Specie
D. Speciess
Correct Answer: B. Species
Explanation: Species remains unchanged in the plural. Context and verb agreement determine whether it refers to one species or several.
29. What is the plural of “tooth”?
A. Tooths
B. Toothes
C. Toothies
D. Teeth
Correct Answer: D. Teeth
Explanation: Tooth has the irregular plural teeth. Other common irregular pairs include foot → feet and goose → geese.
30. Choose the correct plural of “datum.”
A. Datums only
B. Data
C. Datumes
D. Datas
Correct Answer: B. Data
Explanation: Data is the traditional plural of datum. In modern English, data is also widely used as a mass noun with singular agreement, especially in general and technical usage.
31. Which sentence uses the plural noun correctly?
A. The childs are playing.
B. The childrens are playing.
C. The children are playing.
D. The childrens' are playing.
Correct Answer: C. The children are playing.
Explanation: The plural of child is children, not childs. Since children is already plural, it does not take another plural ending.
32. Choose the correct plural of “lady.”
A. Ladys
B. Ladies
C. Ladyes
D. Ladie
Correct Answer: B. Ladies
Explanation: A noun ending in consonant + y normally changes y to ies. Therefore, lady → ladies and city → cities.
33. What is the plural of “roof”?
A. Roofs
B. Rooves
C. Roofes
D. Roofies
Correct Answer: A. Roofs
Explanation: Roof normally forms its plural as roofs. The spelling pattern -f → -ves is not universal, so individual nouns must be learned.
34. Which is the correct plural of “belief”?
A. Believes
B. Beliefes
C. Beliefs
D. Believeses
Correct Answer: C. Beliefs
Explanation: Belief becomes beliefs in the plural. Compare the verb believes, which is a third-person singular present-tense form and not the plural of the noun.
35. What is the plural of “index” in the general sense of alphabetical lists or indicators?
A. Indexes only
B. Indices only
C. Indexen
D. Indexes or indices
Correct Answer: D. Indexes or indices
Explanation: Both indexes and indices are accepted plurals, though their use may vary by context. In mathematics, indices is particularly common, while indexes is common for books and databases.
Section C: Countable and Uncountable Nouns (36–55)
36. Choose the grammatically correct sentence.
A. She gave me many advice.
B. She gave me an advice.
C. She gave me a piece of advice.
D. She gave me several advices.
Correct Answer: C. She gave me a piece of advice.
Explanation: Advice is uncountable in standard English. Use a piece of advice or some advice, rather than an advice or advices.
37. Which expression is correct?
A. Many informations
B. Much information
C. An information
D. Several information
Correct Answer: B. Much information
Explanation: Information is an uncountable noun and takes much in standard quantity expressions. To count individual items, say pieces of information.
38. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The furnitures are expensive.
B. We bought three furnitures.
C. We bought some furniture.
D. We bought a furniture.
Correct Answer: C. We bought some furniture.
Explanation: Furniture is uncountable and normally does not take a plural ending. Use some furniture or three pieces of furniture.
39. Which quantifier correctly completes the sentence: “There is ___ milk in the refrigerator”?
A. Many
B. Few
C. A few
D. Some
Correct Answer: D. Some
Explanation: Milk is uncountable, so some is suitable in an affirmative statement. Many and a few normally modify plural countable nouns.
40. Choose the correct expression.
A. A loaf of bread
B. A bread
C. Many bread
D. Three breads
Correct Answer: A. A loaf of bread
Explanation: Bread is usually uncountable when referring to the food generally. To count it, use units such as a loaf of bread or two slices of bread.
41. Which sentence is correct?
A. The news are encouraging.
B. The news is encouraging.
C. The news have encouraging.
D. The news were an encouraging.
Correct Answer: B. The news is encouraging.
Explanation: News ends in -s but is grammatically singular. It takes a singular verb: The news is good.
42. Choose the correct word: “How ___ money do you need?”
A. Many
B. Few
C. Much
D. Several
Correct Answer: C. Much
Explanation: Money is uncountable when used generally, so much is appropriate. Use many with countable plural nouns, such as coins or banknotes.
43. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A. There are fewer traffic today.
B. There is less traffic today.
C. There are less traffic today.
D. There is fewer traffic today.
Correct Answer: B. There is less traffic today.
Explanation: Traffic is uncountable, so less is normally used. Fewer is used with plural countable nouns, as in fewer vehicles.
44. Choose the correct expression.
A. A piece of luggage
B. A luggage
C. Many luggage
D. Two luggages
Correct Answer: A. A piece of luggage
Explanation: Luggage is uncountable in standard English. Use a piece of luggage or two pieces of luggage.
45. Which sentence is correct?
A. She has many work to do.
B. She has much works to do.
C. She has a lot of work to do.
D. She has several work to do.
Correct Answer: C. She has a lot of work to do.
Explanation: Work is generally uncountable when it means tasks or effort collectively. In this sense, use much work or a lot of work; works has other meanings, such as literary or artistic creations.
46. Choose the correct sentence.
A. I need a few sugar.
B. I need a little sugar.
C. I need many sugar.
D. I need few sugar.
Correct Answer: B. I need a little sugar.
Explanation: Sugar is uncountable, so a little is appropriate. A few and few normally modify plural countable nouns.
47. Which word correctly completes the sentence: “There are ___ students in the classroom”?
A. Much
B. Little
C. A little
D. Several
Correct Answer: D. Several
Explanation: Students is a plural countable noun, so several is appropriate. Much and a little are generally used with uncountable nouns.
48. Choose the correct expression.
A. Two pieces of equipment
B. Two equipments
C. An equipment
D. Many equipment
Correct Answer: A. Two pieces of equipment
Explanation: Equipment is uncountable in standard English. To refer to individual items, use pieces of equipment or items of equipment.
49. Which sentence is correct?
A. The police is investigating the case.
B. The police has investigated the case.
C. The police are investigating the case.
D. A police are investigating the case.
Correct Answer: C. The police are investigating the case.
Explanation: Police normally takes plural agreement when referring to the police force or its members. For one person, use a police officer.
50. Choose the correct word: “Only ___ people attended the meeting.”
A. Little
B. A few
C. Much
D. A little
Correct Answer: B. A few
Explanation: People is plural and countable, so a few is correct. A few suggests a small but positive number, whereas few emphasizes scarcity.
51. Which sentence correctly uses “experience” to mean accumulated knowledge or skill?
A. She has many experiences in banking.
B. She has much experience in banking.
C. She has an experience in banking.
D. She has several experience in banking.
Correct Answer: B. She has much experience in banking.
Explanation: Experience is uncountable when it means knowledge or skill gained over time. It can be countable when referring to particular events: She described several unusual experiences.
52. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The scenery are beautiful.
B. The sceneries are beautiful.
C. The scenery is beautiful.
D. A scenery is beautiful.
Correct Answer: C. The scenery is beautiful.
Explanation: Scenery is an uncountable noun in standard English. Use a beautiful scene or several beautiful scenes when referring to countable views or individual sights.
53. Which expression is correct?
A. A pair of scissors
B. A scissor
C. Two scissor
D. A scissors
Correct Answer: A. A pair of scissors
Explanation: Scissors is a plural-form noun for the cutting instrument. One instrument is commonly described as a pair of scissors.
54. Choose the correct sentence.
A. These trousers is new.
B. This trousers are new.
C. These trousers are new.
D. This trouser are new.
Correct Answer: C. These trousers are new.
Explanation: Trousers is normally plural in form and takes a plural verb. For one garment, use a pair of trousers.
55. Which word correctly completes the sentence: “We have ___ time left, so hurry up”?
A. Few
B. A few
C. Many
D. Little
Correct Answer: D. Little
Explanation: Time is uncountable in this context, and little emphasizes that there is not enough. A little time would suggest that some time remains, with a more positive emphasis.
Section D: Possessive Nouns and Apostrophes (56–70)
56. Which is the correct possessive form of “the girl”?
A. The girls book
B. The girl's book
C. The girls' book
D. The girl book's
Correct Answer: B. The girl's book
Explanation: For a singular noun, add apostrophe + s to show possession. The girl's book means the book belonging to one girl.
57. Which expression indicates that a book belongs to several girls?
A. The girl's book
B. The girls book
C. The girls' book
D. The girls's book
Correct Answer: C. The girls' book
Explanation: For a regular plural noun ending in -s, add only an apostrophe to show possession. Thus, girls' book means a book belonging to multiple girls.
58. Choose the correct possessive form of “children.”
A. Childrens' toys
B. Childrens toys
C. Childrens's toys
D. Children's toys
Correct Answer: D. Children's toys
Explanation: Children is an irregular plural that does not end in -s. Therefore, its possessive form is children's.
59. Which sentence correctly expresses possession by one man?
A. The man's car is new.
B. The mans' car is new.
C. The mans car is new.
D. The man car's is new.
Correct Answer: A. The man's car is new.
Explanation: The possessive of the singular noun man is man's. The apostrophe precedes the s because the owner is singular.
60. Choose the correct form for a room used by several teachers.
A. The teacher's room
B. The teachers room
C. The teachers' room
D. The teachers's room
Correct Answer: C. The teachers' room
Explanation: Teachers' indicates possession by multiple teachers because the regular plural already ends in -s. Teacher's room would indicate a room belonging to one teacher.
61. Which sentence uses the apostrophe correctly?
A. Its raining heavily.
B. It's raining heavily.
C. Its' raining heavily.
D. Its's raining heavily.
Correct Answer: B. It's raining heavily.
Explanation: It's is the contraction of it is or it has. Its is the possessive determiner, as in The company changed its policy.
62. Choose the correct possessive form of “women.”
A. Womens' rights
B. Womens rights
C. Women's rights
D. Womens's rights
Correct Answer: C. Women's rights
Explanation: Women is an irregular plural not ending in -s, so its possessive form is women's. The same pattern applies to men's clothing and children's books.
63. Which expression correctly shows a journey lasting three days?
A. Three days journey
B. Three day's journey
C. Three days's journey
D. Three days' journey
Correct Answer: D. Three days' journey
Explanation: The plural time expression three days takes an apostrophe after the final s in the possessive construction. Compare a day's journey for a singular time expression.
64. Choose the correct phrase.
A. At arm's length
B. At arms length
C. At arms' length
D. At arm length's
Correct Answer: A. At arm's length
Explanation: The conventional expression is at arm's length. It refers literally to the distance of an extended arm and figuratively to keeping someone or something at a distance.
65. Which sentence correctly expresses joint ownership by two brothers?
A. Rahim's and Karim's shop is nearby.
B. Rahim and Karim's shop is nearby.
C. Rahim and Karim shop's is nearby.
D. Rahim's and Karim shop is nearby.
Correct Answer: B. Rahim and Karim's shop is nearby.
Explanation: When two people jointly own one thing, the possessive marker normally appears on the final name. If each brother owned a separate shop, Rahim's and Karim's shops would be appropriate.
66. Which phrase correctly indicates separate ownership by two sisters?
A. The sister's books
B. The sisters book's
C. The sisters' books
D. The sister books'
Correct Answer: C. The sisters' books
Explanation: The plural possessive sisters' indicates that the books belong to the sisters. The form sister's would refer to one sister.
67. Choose the correct expression.
A. A week's holiday
B. A weeks holiday
C. A weeks' holiday
D. A week's holidays'
Correct Answer: A. A week's holiday
Explanation: A singular time expression takes apostrophe + s: a week's holiday. The same pattern appears in a month's salary and a year's experience.
68. Which sentence is correctly punctuated?
A. The company's profits increased.
B. The companies profit's increased.
C. The companys' profits increased.
D. The company's profit's increased.
Correct Answer: A. The company's profits increased.
Explanation: Company's is the possessive of the singular noun company. Profits is a regular plural noun and does not need an apostrophe.
69. Which expression is correct for a school attended by girls?
A. Girls school
B. Girl's school
C. Girls's school
D. Girls' school
Correct Answer: D. Girls' school
Explanation: Girls' is the plural possessive of girls. The expression indicates a school associated with or intended for girls.
70. Choose the correct form: “This is ___ decision, not mine.”
A. somebody else's
B. somebody elses'
C. somebodys else's
D. somebody else
Correct Answer: A. somebody else's
Explanation: In the expression somebody else's, the possessive marker attaches to else following the indefinite pronoun. It indicates that the decision belongs to another person.
Section E: Noun Forms and Word Formation (71–85)
71. What is the noun form of “decide”?
A. Decisive
B. Decision
C. Decidingly
D. Decided
Correct Answer: B. Decision
Explanation: Decision is the noun corresponding to the verb decide. Other useful formations include conclude → conclusion and permit → permission.
72. Choose the noun form of “strong.”
A. Strongly
B. Strengthen
C. Strength
D. Stronger
Correct Answer: C. Strength
Explanation: Strength is the noun derived from the adjective strong. Strengthen is a verb, while strongly is an adverb.
73. What is the noun form of “accurate”?
A. Accurately
B. Accurateness only
C. Accurize
D. Accuracy
Correct Answer: D. Accuracy
Explanation: Accuracy is the standard noun form of accurate. It denotes correctness or freedom from error.
74. Which word is the noun form of “perform”?
A. Performance
B. Performing
C. Performed
D. Performative
Correct Answer: A. Performance
Explanation: Performance names the act or result of performing. It is frequently used in formal and professional contexts, such as job performance.
75. Choose the noun form of “poor.”
A. Poorly
B. Poverty
C. Poorer
D. Poorish
Correct Answer: B. Poverty
Explanation: Poverty is the noun that denotes the state of being poor. Competitive examinations often test irregular or non-obvious word formations such as poor → poverty.
76. Which is the correct noun form of “high”?
A. Highly
B. Heighten
C. Height
D. Higher
Correct Answer: C. Height
Explanation: Height is the noun corresponding to high. Notice the spelling change: high becomes height, not highness in the standard expression for physical measurement.
77. What is the noun form of “know”?
A. Known
B. Knowing
C. Knowable
D. Knowledge
Correct Answer: D. Knowledge
Explanation: Knowledge is the standard noun form associated with know. It refers to information, understanding, or familiarity acquired through learning or experience.
78. Choose the noun form of “strongly” in the sentence “She argued strongly.”
A. Strength
B. Strong
C. Strengthen
D. Stronger
Correct Answer: A. Strength
Explanation: Strength is the related noun, while strongly is an adverb modifying the verb argued. Recognizing a word's part of speech helps avoid confusing related forms.
79. Which noun is derived from the adjective “wise”?
A. Wisely
B. Wisdom
C. Wiser
D. Wisen
Correct Answer: B. Wisdom
Explanation: Wisdom is the noun denoting the quality of being wise. The ending and spelling change, so the noun cannot be formed simply by adding -ness.
80. Choose the correct noun form of “wide.”
A. Widely
B. Widen
C. Width
D. Wider
Correct Answer: C. Width
Explanation: Width is the noun referring to the measurement or extent from side to side. It is an irregular formation, similar to long → length.
81. What is the noun form of “deep”?
A. Deeply
B. Deepen
C. Deeper
D. Depth
Correct Answer: D. Depth
Explanation: Depth is the noun corresponding to deep. Like width and length, it has a distinct form rather than simply adding -ness.
82. Which is the noun form of “choose”?
A. Choice
B. Chosen
C. Choosingly
D. Chooserly
Correct Answer: A. Choice
Explanation: Choice is the noun corresponding to choose. The verb and noun have different spellings and pronunciations: choose and choice.
83. Choose the noun form of “explain.”
A. Explanatory
B. Explanation
C. Explained
D. Explanatorily
Correct Answer: B. Explanation
Explanation: Explanation names the act or result of explaining. It is a common noun formation in academic and professional English.
84. Which word is the noun form of “strongly recommend”?
A. Recommendable
B. Recommending
C. Recommendation
D. Recommended
Correct Answer: C. Recommendation
Explanation: Recommendation is the noun derived from recommend. It refers to advice or a proposal that someone or something should be chosen or considered.
85. What is the noun form of “grow”?
A. Grown
B. Growing
C. Growerly
D. Growth
Correct Answer: D. Growth
Explanation: Growth names the process or result of growing. In economic contexts, it appears in expressions such as economic growth and population growth.
Section F: Noun Number, Agreement and Usage (86–105)
86. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The committee has submitted its report.
B. The committee have submitted its report.
C. The committee has submitted their report.
D. The committee are submitted its report.
Correct Answer: A. The committee has submitted its report.
Explanation: When a collective noun is treated as a single unit, it takes singular agreement in standard formal usage. British English can use plural agreement when emphasizing individual members, but the pronoun and verb must remain consistent.
87. Which sentence is correct?
A. Mathematics are difficult for him.
B. Mathematics is difficult for him.
C. Mathematics were difficult for him.
D. Mathematics have difficult for him.
Correct Answer: B. Mathematics is difficult for him.
Explanation: Mathematics is singular when it refers to the academic subject. Nouns ending in -s, such as physics and economics, can also take singular agreement when naming fields of study.
88. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The cattle is grazing.
B. The cattles are grazing.
C. The cattle are grazing.
D. A cattle are grazing.
Correct Answer: C. The cattle are grazing.
Explanation: Cattle is a plural noun and normally takes a plural verb. It does not ordinarily have the form cattles in standard English.
89. Which sentence is correct?
A. The scissors is on the table.
B. The scissor are on the table.
C. The scissors has been repaired.
D. The scissors are on the table.
Correct Answer: D. The scissors are on the table.
Explanation: Scissors is plural in form and generally takes a plural verb. To emphasize one instrument, use a pair of scissors is on the table.
90. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The number of applicants has increased.
B. The number of applicants have increased.
C. A number of applicant has increased.
D. The number of applicants are increased.
Correct Answer: A. The number of applicants has increased.
Explanation: The number of takes a singular verb because the grammatical subject is number. By contrast, a number of applicants have increased uses plural agreement.
91. Which sentence correctly uses “a number of”?
A. A number of students is absent.
B. A number of students are absent.
C. A number of student are absent.
D. A number of students has absent.
Correct Answer: B. A number of students are absent.
Explanation: A number of means several and takes a plural verb. This contrasts with the number of, which takes a singular verb.
92. Choose the correct sentence.
A. Every student have a book.
B. Every students has a book.
C. Every student has a book.
D. Every student are having a book.
Correct Answer: C. Every student has a book.
Explanation: Every takes a singular countable noun and normally requires a singular verb. The correct pattern is every student has, not every students have.
93. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A. Each of the boys have a pen.
B. Each of the boys are ready.
C. Each of the boys were ready.
