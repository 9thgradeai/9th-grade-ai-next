4. Choose the correctly spelt word.
A. Neccessary B. Necesary C. Neccesary D. Necessary

5. Choose the correctly spelt word.
A. Maintainance B. Maintenance C. Maintenence D. Maintanance

6. Choose the correctly spelt word.

7. Choose the correctly spelt word.

Correct Answer: C. Questionnaire Explanation: Questionnaire ends in -naire and contains double n. It means a set of questions used to collect information.
8. Choose the correctly spelt word.

9. Choose the correctly spelt word.

Section C: Appropriate Words in Context (81–120)
81. Choose the appropriate word to complete the sentence.
The bank will not _____ any responsibility for losses caused by unauthorized transactions.
A. except B. accept C. expect D. aspect
Correct Answer: B. accept Explanation: Accept means to receive or agree to take responsibility. Except means excluding, while expect means to anticipate.
82. Choose the appropriate word.
The new policy will _____ all branches of the bank.
A. effect B. affect C. infect D. defect
Correct Answer: B. affect Explanation: Affect is usually a verb meaning to influence. Effect is usually a noun meaning a result, although it can also be a verb meaning to bring about.
83. Choose the appropriate word.
The manager gave me useful _____ about preparing for the examination.
A. advise B. advices C. advice D. advising
Correct Answer: C. advice Explanation: Advice is an uncountable noun. Advise is the corresponding verb: “The manager advised me to prepare carefully.”
84. Choose the appropriate word.
The committee must _____ whether the proposal is financially viable.
A. decide B. decision C. decisive D. decidedly
Correct Answer: A. decide Explanation: Decide is the verb required after the modal must. Decision is a noun, as in “make a decision.”
85. Choose the appropriate word.
The company has appointed an _____ to examine its financial statements.
A. auditor B. author C. editor D. advisor
Correct Answer: A. auditor Explanation: An auditor examines financial records and statements. An author writes books, while an editor revises or prepares written material.
86. Choose the appropriate word.
The government intends to _____ inflation through appropriate monetary measures.
A. curb B. curve C. carve D. curve up
Correct Answer: A. curb Explanation: Curb means to control or limit something undesirable. Curve refers to a bent line or shape.
87. Choose the appropriate word.
The witness gave a _____ account of the incident.
A. credible B. creditable C. credulous D. credulousness
Correct Answer: A. credible Explanation: Credible means believable or worthy of trust. Creditable means deserving praise or recognition.
88. Choose the appropriate word.
The bank introduced strict measures to prevent financial _____.
A. fraud B. fraudulence C. fraudulent D. fraudulently
Correct Answer: A. fraud Explanation: Fraud is a noun referring to intentional deception for unlawful gain. Fraudulent is the adjective, as in “fraudulent transactions.”
89. Choose the appropriate word.
The officer was praised for his _____ in handling confidential information.
A. discreet B. discrete C. discretion D. discreetly
Correct Answer: C. discretion Explanation: Discretion is a noun meaning careful judgment, especially when dealing with sensitive information. Discreet is the adjective describing a person or action that avoids attracting attention.
90. Choose the appropriate word.
The new regulations will _____ the existing guidelines.
A. complement B. compliment C. complimentary D. complimentarily
Correct Answer: A. complement Explanation: Complement means to complete or enhance something. Compliment means to express praise.
91. Choose the appropriate word.
The board reached a _____ decision after lengthy discussion.
A. unanimous B. anonymous C. autonomous D. unanimousness
Correct Answer: A. unanimous Explanation: Unanimous means agreed upon by everyone involved. Anonymous means having an unknown name or identity.
92. Choose the appropriate word.
The officer's explanation was clear and _____.
A. concise B. precise C. excise D. incision
Correct Answer: A. concise Explanation: Concise means brief but clear. Precise means exact or accurate; both can describe writing, but brevity is emphasized by concise.
93. Choose the appropriate word.
The company must _____ with the central bank's regulations.
A. comply B. compile C. compel D. complain
Correct Answer: A. comply Explanation: Comply with means to act according to a rule or requirement. Compile means to collect information into a single work.
94. Choose the appropriate word.
The chairman refused to _____ the allegations without evidence.
A. confirm B. conform C. confine D. confuse
Correct Answer: A. confirm Explanation: Confirm means to establish that something is true. Conform means to follow a rule, standard, or convention.
95. Choose the appropriate word.
The candidate's qualifications are _____ for the position.
A. adequate B. addicted C. adjacent D. adamant
Correct Answer: A. adequate Explanation: Adequate means sufficient for a particular purpose. It indicates that the qualifications meet the necessary standard.
96. Choose the appropriate word.
The bank decided to _____ its operations to rural areas.
A. extend B. extent C. extensive D. extension
Correct Answer: A. extend Explanation: Extend is a verb meaning to make longer or increase the reach of something. Extent is a noun indicating size, range, or degree.
97. Choose the appropriate word.
The financial report must be _____ before publication.
A. scrutinize B. scrutinized C. scrutiny D. scrutinizingly
Correct Answer: B. scrutinized Explanation: The passive construction must be scrutinized requires a past participle. Scrutinize means to examine carefully.
98. Choose the appropriate word.

118. Choose the appropriate word.

164. Choose the appropriate word.

165. Choose the appropriate word.
The manager's instructions were _____, leaving no room for misunderstanding.

167. Choose the appropriate word.

168. Choose the appropriate word.

169. Choose the appropriate word.

170. Choose the appropriate word.

171. Choose the appropriate word.

172. Choose the appropriate word.

173. Choose the appropriate word.
The manager's statement seemed to _____ that the existing procedure was inefficient.
