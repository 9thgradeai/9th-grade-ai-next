Section A: Subject–Verb Agreement (1–100)
Basic rules and singular/plural subjects
1. Each of the candidates _____ required to submit an application.
A. are B. is C. were D. have been
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: Each is singular and takes a singular verb. The phrase of the candidates does not change the number of the subject.
2. Neither Rahim nor his friends _____ willing to accept the proposal.
A. is B. was C. are D. has
Correct Answer: C. are
Explanation: With neither…nor, the verb generally agrees with the nearer subject. Here, friends is plural, so are is correct.
3. Neither the students nor the teacher _____ present yesterday.
A. were B. are C. have been D. was
Correct Answer: D. was
Explanation: The nearer subject, teacher, is singular. The past-tense singular verb was is therefore appropriate.
4. The quality of these products _____ improved considerably.
A. have B. has C. are D. were
Correct Answer: B. has
Explanation: The subject is quality, not products. A singular head noun takes a singular verb.
5. A number of applicants _____ waiting outside the examination hall.
A. is B. was C. are D. has
Correct Answer: C. are
Explanation: A number of means several and takes a plural verb. Compare: The number of applicants is increasing.
6. The number of unemployed graduates _____ increasing every year.
A. are B. have C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: The number of refers to one numerical figure and takes a singular verb.
7. Everyone in the offices _____ expected to attend the meeting.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: Indefinite pronouns such as everyone, everybody, someone, and nobody are grammatically singular.
8. One of my closest friends _____ in a commercial bank.
A. work B. have worked C. works D. are working
Correct Answer: C. works
Explanation: The subject is one, which is singular. The plural noun friends appears in a prepositional phrase and does not control the verb.
9. Each boy and each girl _____ given a separate identity card.
A. was B. were C. have been D. are
Correct Answer: A. was
Explanation: When each precedes individual nouns joined by and, the construction is generally treated as singular.
10. Bread and butter _____ my usual breakfast.
A. are B. were C. have been D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: When two nouns joined by and express one combined item or concept, a singular verb is used.
11. The captain, along with the players, _____ arriving shortly.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: Expressions such as along with, together with, and as well as do not make the main subject plural. The subject captain is singular.
12. The principal as well as the teachers _____ responsible for maintaining discipline.
A. are B. have C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: In standard subject–verb agreement, as well as does not operate like and. The singular subject principal determines the verb.
13. Either of the two proposals _____ acceptable to the committee.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: Either meaning one or the other is singular and takes a singular verb.
14. Neither of the explanations _____ convincing.
A. seem B. have seemed C. are seeming D. seems
Correct Answer: D. seems
Explanation: In formal examination English, neither of + plural noun normally takes a singular verb.
15. Somebody _____ left a notebook on the desk.
A. have B. has C. are D. were
Correct Answer: B. has
Explanation: Somebody is a singular indefinite pronoun and takes has in the present perfect tense.
16. Many a student _____ failed to understand this rule.
A. have B. are C. has D. were
Correct Answer: C. has
Explanation: The expression many a + singular noun takes a singular verb despite referring to many individuals.
17. More than one candidate _____ applied for the position.
A. has B. have C. are D. were
Correct Answer: A. has
Explanation: More than one + singular noun normally takes a singular verb in standard exam grammar.
18. One-third of the work _____ already completed.
A. have been B. are C. were D. has been
Correct Answer: D. has been
Explanation: With fractions, the verb generally agrees with the noun after of. Work is uncountable and singular here.
19. One-third of the employees _____ absent today.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: The noun after of is employees, which is plural. Therefore, the plural verb are is required.
20. Ten thousand taka _____ sufficient for the initial payment.
A. are B. have been C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: A sum of money considered as one total amount normally takes a singular verb.
21. Five years _____ a long time to wait for a promotion.
A. are B. have been C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: A period of time considered as one unit takes a singular verb.
22. The scissors _____ on the table.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: Scissors is a plural-form noun and normally takes a plural verb. A pair of scissors is is also correct.
23. This pair of trousers _____ too expensive for me.
A. are B. have C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: The head noun is pair, which is singular. The phrase of trousers does not affect agreement.
24. The news _____ encouraging.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: News is an uncountable singular noun, even though it ends in -s.
25. Mathematics _____ my favourite subject.
A. are B. have been C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: Names of academic subjects ending in -s, such as mathematics, physics, and economics, usually take singular verbs.
26. The police _____ investigating the incident.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: Police is generally treated as a plural noun and takes a plural verb.
27. The cattle _____ grazing in the field.
A. is B. has C. are D. was
Correct Answer: C. are
Explanation: Cattle is a plural noun and takes a plural verb. Its singular equivalent is a cow or an animal.
28. The furniture in these rooms _____ imported.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: Furniture is an uncountable noun and takes a singular verb.
29. No student and no teacher _____ allowed to enter without permission.
A. are B. have been C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: Repeated no before singular nouns joined by and generally produces a singular subject.
30. Not only the manager but also the employees _____ dissatisfied with the decision.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: With not only…but also, the verb generally agrees with the nearer subject. Here, employees is plural.
Compound subjects and special expressions
31. The poet and philosopher _____ dead.
A. are B. have C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: When two titles joined by and refer to the same person, the verb is singular.
32. The poet and the philosopher _____ invited to the ceremony.
A. was B. has been C. is D. were
Correct Answer: D. were
Explanation: The repeated article the indicates two different people. The compound subject is plural.
33. Slow and steady _____ the race.
A. wins B. win C. have won D. are winning
Correct Answer: A. wins
Explanation: When two adjectives joined by and express one combined quality or principle, the expression may take a singular verb.
34. Every man, woman, and child _____ evacuated from the building.
A. were B. was C. have been D. are
Correct Answer: B. was
Explanation: Every makes the compound subject distributively singular, so a singular verb is used.
35. The manager and the accountant _____ submitted their reports.
A. has B. is C. have D. was
Correct Answer: C. have
Explanation: The two nouns refer to separate people, making the subject plural.
36. Either the director or the assistant managers _____ responsible for the error.
A. is B. was C. has D. are
Correct Answer: D. are
Explanation: With either…or, the verb generally agrees with the nearer subject, assistant managers.
37. Either the assistant managers or the director _____ responsible for the error.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: The nearer subject, director, is singular. Therefore, is is appropriate.
38. The United States _____ a major economic power.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: The name of a country treated as one political entity takes a singular verb.
39. The United Nations _____ its headquarters in New York.
A. have B. are having C. has D. were having
Correct Answer: C. has
Explanation: The United Nations is an organization considered a single entity and therefore takes a singular verb.
40. The Arabian Nights _____ an interesting collection of stories.
A. are B. have C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: A title of a book or literary work is generally treated as singular, even when it contains a plural word.
41. The rich _____ not always happy.
A. are B. is C. has D. was
Correct Answer: A. are
Explanation: The + adjective can refer to a whole class of people and takes a plural verb, as in the poor are.
42. The injured _____ taken to hospital after the accident.
A. was B. were C. has been D. is
Correct Answer: B. were
Explanation: The injured refers to injured people collectively and normally takes a plural verb.
43. What he says _____ true.
A. are B. have been C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: A noun clause functioning as the subject normally takes a singular verb.
44. To err _____ human.
A. are B. were C. have been D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: An infinitive phrase used as the subject generally takes a singular verb.
45. Swimming in deep water _____ excellent exercise.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: A gerund phrase functioning as the subject is singular.
46. The committee _____ divided in their opinions.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: A collective noun can take a plural verb when the members are emphasized as individuals, particularly in British English. When the committee is considered one unit, is is appropriate.
47. The committee _____ reached its final decision.
A. have B. are C. has D. were
Correct Answer: C. has
Explanation: Here, the committee acts as a single unit, so the singular verb has is appropriate.
48. The jury _____ unanimous in its verdict.
A. are B. have C. were D. was
Correct Answer: D. was
Explanation: The jury is considered one body reaching a single verdict, so a singular verb is used.
49. The jury _____ arguing among themselves.
A. are B. is C. has D. was
Correct Answer: A. are
Explanation: The plural verb emphasizes individual jurors acting separately. Collective nouns may take singular or plural verbs depending on meaning and dialect.
50. A pair of spectacles _____ lying on the desk.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: The head noun pair is singular. Therefore, the verb is singular even though spectacles is plural in form.
Agreement with intervening phrases and relative clauses
51. The list of selected candidates _____ on the noticeboard.
A. are B. have been C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: The head noun is list, not candidates. A singular head noun takes a singular verb.
52. The effects of inflation on low-income families _____ serious.
A. is B. has been C. was D. are
Correct Answer: D. are
Explanation: The subject effects is plural. The phrase of inflation on low-income families does not control agreement.
53. The introduction of new banking regulations _____ necessary.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: The head of the subject is introduction, a singular noun.
54. The manager, together with two assistants, _____ attending the conference.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: Together with two assistants is an additional phrase. The subject remains the singular manager.
55. The students who _____ regularly usually perform better.
A. studies B. has studied C. study D. is studying
Correct Answer: C. study
Explanation: The relative pronoun who refers to the plural antecedent students. Therefore, the plural verb study is required.
56. She is one of those employees who _____ always punctual.
A. is B. has been C. was D. are
Correct Answer: D. are
Explanation: In one of those employees who…, the relative pronoun who refers to employees. Hence, are is correct.
57. She is the only one of the employees who _____ the required qualification.
A. has B. have C. are having D. were having
Correct Answer: A. has
Explanation: The expression the only one identifies a single person. The relative clause therefore refers to that individual.
58. It is I who _____ responsible for this decision.
A. is B. am C. are D. was
Correct Answer: B. am
Explanation: In formal grammar, the verb in a relative clause agrees with its antecedent. Here, who refers to I, so am is correct.
59. There _____ several reasons for the delay.
A. is B. has C. are D. was
Correct Answer: C. are
Explanation: In an existential construction beginning with there, the verb agrees with the following notional subject, several reasons.
60. There _____ a serious problem with the computer system.
A. are B. have C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: The notional subject a serious problem is singular, so the correct verb is is.
61. Where _____ the keys to the cabinet?
A. are B. is C. has D. was
Correct Answer: A. are
Explanation: The subject keys is plural. The position of the subject after the verb does not alter agreement.
62. Here _____ the documents you requested.
A. comes B. come C. is coming D. has come
Correct Answer: B. come
Explanation: In an introductory construction with here, the verb agrees with the following subject. Documents is plural.
63. The only thing we need _____ more time.
A. are B. have C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: The subject is the only thing, which is singular. The clause we need modifies thing.
64. More than half of the land _____ been sold.
A. have B. are C. were D. has
Correct Answer: D. has
Explanation: Land is an uncountable singular noun. Fractions and expressions such as half of generally agree with the noun that follows of.
65. More than half of the voters _____ supported the proposal.
A. have B. has C. is D. was
Correct Answer: A. have
Explanation: Voters is plural, so the present perfect requires have.
66. All the information _____ been checked carefully.
A. have B. has C. are D. were
Correct Answer: B. has
Explanation: Information is uncountable and singular. The correct form is has been checked.
67. All the books _____ been arranged alphabetically.
A. has B. is C. have D. was
Correct Answer: C. have
Explanation: Books is plural, so the present perfect auxiliary must be have.
68. None of the money _____ recovered so far.
A. have been B. are C. were D. has been
Correct Answer: D. has been
Explanation: Money is uncountable and singular. In this formal construction, none of the money has been is standard.
69. None of the applicants _____ qualified for the post.
A. have B. has C. is D. was
Correct Answer: A. have
Explanation: When none refers to no members of a plural group, a plural verb is acceptable and natural. In this sentence, have qualified is correct.
70. Every one of these machines _____ regularly maintained.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: Every one is singular and takes a singular verb.
Advanced agreement rules
71. The principal, not the teachers, _____ responsible for the final decision.
A. are B. have C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: The actual subject is principal. The interrupting phrase not the teachers does not change the subject's number.
72. Either of these books _____ suitable for beginners.
A. are B. have C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: Either is singular in this construction and takes is.
73. The poet's works, rather than his personal life, _____ the focus of the study.
A. are B. is C. has D. was
Correct Answer: A. are
Explanation: The subject works is plural. The phrase rather than his personal life does not determine the verb.
74. Fifty per cent of the population _____ below the poverty line.
A. live B. lives C. have lived D. are living
Correct Answer: B. lives
Explanation: Population is treated as a singular collective noun here. Percentage expressions agree with the noun following of.
75. Fifty per cent of the workers _____ on temporary contracts.
A. works B. is working C. work D. has worked
Correct Answer: C. work
Explanation: The noun workers is plural, so the plural verb work is required.
76. The United Arab Emirates _____ a federation of seven emirates.
A. are B. have C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: The United Arab Emirates is treated as one country and takes a singular verb.
77. The Philippines _____ an archipelago in Southeast Asia.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: The name of the country is treated as singular when referring to the nation as one entity.
78. The proceeds from the sale _____ donated to charity.
A. was B. were C. has been D. is
Correct Answer: B. were
Explanation: Proceeds, meaning money obtained from a transaction, is plural in standard usage and takes were.
79. The outskirts of the city _____ rapidly expanding.
A. is B. has C. are D. was
Correct Answer: C. are
Explanation: Outskirts is a plural-form noun and normally takes a plural verb.
80. The contents of the box _____ missing.
A. is B. has C. was D. are
Correct Answer: D. are
Explanation: Contents is plural in this sense and takes are.
81. Neither of the two brothers, who _____ abroad, wants to sell the house.
A. live B. lives C. has lived D. is living
Correct Answer: A. live
Explanation: The relative pronoun who refers to the plural noun brothers, so live is correct. The main clause has the singular subject neither and takes wants.
82. The manager is one of the officers who _____ been promoted this year.
A. has B. have C. is D. was
Correct Answer: B. have
Explanation: The relative clause refers to officers, so the plural auxiliary have is required.
83. The manager is the only one of the officers who _____ been promoted this year.
A. have B. are C. has D. were
Correct Answer: C. has
Explanation: The only one singles out the manager. Therefore, the relative clause takes the singular auxiliary has.
84. Not one of the participants _____ the correct answer.
A. know B. have known C. are knowing D. knows
Correct Answer: D. knows
Explanation: Not one is singular in standard examination grammar and takes a singular verb.
85. Every one of the proposals, including the revised version, _____ merit.
A. has B. have C. are having D. were having
Correct Answer: A. has
Explanation: The subject every one is singular. The parenthetical phrase does not affect agreement.
86. The majority of the committee _____ in favour of the motion.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: When the members of a collective body are emphasized individually, a plural verb is possible, especially in British English. If the majority is treated as one unified group, is may also be appropriate.
87. The majority of the work _____ already been completed.
A. have B. are C. has D. were
Correct Answer: C. has
Explanation: The noun work is uncountable and singular. Therefore, has been completed is correct.
88. The criteria for selection _____ clearly stated in the circular.
A. is B. has been C. was D. are
Correct Answer: D. are
Explanation: Criteria is the plural of criterion. It therefore takes a plural verb.
89. The criterion for selection _____ clearly stated in the circular.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: Criterion is singular, whereas criteria is plural.
90. The phenomenon _____ difficult to explain.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: Phenomenon is singular. Its plural form is phenomena.
91. These phenomena _____ further investigation.
A. requires B. has required C. require D. is requiring
Correct Answer: C. require
Explanation: Phenomena is plural and takes the base-form plural verb require.
92. The data _____ consistent with the hypothesis.
A. is B. has C. was D. are
Correct Answer: D. are
Explanation: In traditional formal scientific and examination usage, data is treated as the plural of datum. In general modern usage, singular data is also common.
93. This species of bird _____ endangered.
A. is B. are C. have D. were
Correct Answer: A. is
Explanation: Species has the same form in singular and plural. Here, this species is singular.
94. Several species of birds _____ found in this forest.
A. is B. are C. has D. was
Correct Answer: B. are
Explanation: The phrase several species refers to multiple kinds and takes a plural verb.
95. The wages paid to the workers _____ insufficient.
A. is B. has been C. are D. was
Correct Answer: C. are
Explanation: Wages referring to payments or earnings is generally plural in this construction.
96. The latest means of communication _____ available to the villagers.
A. are B. have C. were D. is
Correct Answer: D. is
Explanation: Means can be singular or plural. The phrase the latest means refers to one method here and takes a singular verb.
97. Several means of communication _____ available in the area.
A. are B. is C. has D. was
Correct Answer: A. are
Explanation: Several means refers to multiple methods and takes a plural verb.
98. What the employees need _____ better working conditions.
A. are B. is C. have been D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: A what-clause functioning as the subject is often treated as singular when it denotes one general requirement or idea. The complement better working conditions does not control the verb.
99. The only remaining issue _____ the shortage of funds.
A. are B. have been C. is D. were
Correct Answer: C. is
Explanation: The subject issue is singular. The noun phrase the shortage of funds is its complement, not the subject.
100. Which sentence is grammatically correct?
A. Each of the employees have a personal account. B. Neither the officers nor the manager are available. C. The quality of the goods have deteriorated. D. Every one of the applicants has received a confirmation email.
Correct Answer: D. Every one of the applicants has received a confirmation email.
Explanation: Every one is singular and requires has. The other sentences contain agreement errors: each requires has, manager is the nearer singular subject in B, and quality requires has in C.
Section B: Right Forms of Verbs (101–200)
Tenses and sequence of tenses
101. He usually _____ to the office by bus.
A. go B. goes C. going D. have gone
Correct Answer: B. goes
Explanation: The simple present describes a habitual action. With the third-person singular subject he, the verb takes -s.
102. Listen! Someone _____ at the door.
A. knocks B. knocked C. is knocking D. has knocked
Correct Answer: C. is knocking
Explanation: The present continuous describes an action happening at the moment of speaking. Listen! signals an ongoing event.
103. She _____ in Dhaka since 2020.
A. lives B. lived C. is living D. has lived
Correct Answer: D. has lived
Explanation: The present perfect is used with since to describe a state or situation that began in the past and continues into the present.
104. By the time we reached the station, the train _____.
A. had left B. has left C. leaves D. will leave
Correct Answer: A. had left
Explanation: The past perfect indicates that the train's departure occurred before another past event, our arrival.
105. If he _____ harder, he would have passed the examination.
A. studied B. had studied C. studies D. would study
Correct Answer: B. had studied
Explanation: The third conditional uses if + past perfect and would have + past participle to describe an unreal past condition.
106. I _____ him yesterday at the bank.
A. have met B. had met C. met D. meet
Correct Answer: C. met
Explanation: A completed action at a definite past time normally takes the simple past. Yesterday is incompatible with the usual present-perfect construction.
107. She _____ her assignment before the teacher entered the classroom.
A. completes B. has completed C. completed D. had completed
Correct Answer: D. had completed
Explanation: The past perfect marks an action completed before another past event.
108. By next December, he _____ here for ten years.
A. will have worked B. has worked C. had worked D. works
Correct Answer: A. will have worked
Explanation: The future perfect describes an action or state that will have continued for a specified period by a future point.
109. The earth _____ around the sun.
A. revolved B. revolves C. is revolving D. has revolved
Correct Answer: B. revolves
Explanation: The simple present expresses established scientific facts and universal truths.
110. I _____ for two hours when the electricity went out.
A. study B. have studied C. had been studying D. will study
Correct Answer: C. had been studying
Explanation: The past perfect continuous emphasizes the duration of an activity continuing up to a past event.
111. She said that she _____ tired.
A. is B. will be C. has been D. was
Correct Answer: D. was
Explanation: In traditional sequence-of-tenses rules, a present-tense verb often changes to the past in reported speech when the reporting verb is past.
112. The teacher said that the earth _____ around the sun.
A. revolves B. revolved C. had revolved D. will revolve
Correct Answer: A. revolves
Explanation: A universal truth normally remains in the simple present even in reported speech.
113. He told me that he _____ the work the previous day.
A. completes B. had completed C. has completed D. will complete
Correct Answer: B. had completed
Explanation: The past perfect indicates that the work was completed before the past reporting event.
114. When I arrived, they _____ dinner.
A. have B. had had C. were having D. will have
Correct Answer: C. were having
Explanation: The past continuous describes an activity in progress when another past event occurred.
115. No sooner had the meeting begun than the power _____ off.
A. goes B. has gone C. had gone D. went
Correct Answer: D. went
Explanation: The standard construction is no sooner…than. With no sooner had + subject + past participle, the following clause normally takes the simple past.
116. Hardly had he reached home when it _____ to rain.
A. began B. begins C. has begun D. had begun
Correct Answer: A. began
Explanation: The construction hardly had…when pairs the past perfect in the first clause with the simple past in the second.
117. I will call you as soon as I _____ home.
A. will reach B. reach C. reached D. would reach
Correct Answer: B. reach
Explanation: In a future-time clause introduced by as soon as, the simple present is normally used instead of will.
118. Wait here until the manager _____.
A. will arrive B. arrived C. arrives D. would arrive
Correct Answer: C. arrives
Explanation: Future-time clauses introduced by until generally take the simple present.
119. It is high time we _____ the matter seriously.
A. consider B. will consider C. have considered D. considered
Correct Answer: D. considered
Explanation: It is high time is traditionally followed by a past-tense form to express that an action is overdue. The meaning refers to the present or near future.
120. I wish I _____ more time to prepare for the examination.
A. had B. have C. will have D. am having
Correct Answer: A. had
Explanation: Wish + past simple expresses a present situation that is unreal or contrary to fact.
Infinitives, gerunds and participles
121. He enjoys _____ novels in his spare time.
A. to read B. reading C. read D. to reading
Correct Answer: B. reading
Explanation: The verb enjoy is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive. Correct: enjoys reading.
122. The officer decided _____ the report immediately.
A. submitting B. submit C. to submit D. submitted
Correct Answer: C. to submit
Explanation: Decide is followed by a to-infinitive when introducing the action chosen.
123. She avoided _____ about the confidential matter.
A. to talk B. talk C. talked D. talking
Correct Answer: D. talking
Explanation: Avoid is followed by a gerund. Therefore, avoided talking is correct.
124. The manager made the clerk _____ the document again.
A. rewrite B. to rewrite C. rewriting D. rewritten
Correct Answer: A. rewrite
Explanation: In the active voice, make + object is followed by the bare infinitive. In the passive, however, we say the clerk was made to rewrite the document.
125. We look forward to _____ you soon.
A. meet B. meeting C. have met D. be meeting
Correct Answer: B. meeting
Explanation: In look forward to, to is a preposition, so it is followed by a noun or gerund.
126. He is accustomed to _____ early.
A. get up B. have got up C. getting up D. got up
Correct Answer: C. getting up
Explanation: Be accustomed to uses to as a preposition and therefore takes a gerund.
127. The teacher let the students _____ early.
A. to leave B. leaving C. left D. leave
Correct Answer: D. leave
Explanation: Let + object takes the bare infinitive: let the students leave.
128. She stopped _____ when the phone rang.
A. writing B. to writing C. write D. written
Correct Answer: A. writing
Explanation: Stop + gerund means to cease an activity. Here, she ceased writing because the phone rang.
129. He stopped _____ some medicine on his way home.
A. buying B. to buy C. bought D. buy
Correct Answer: B. to buy
Explanation: Stop + to-infinitive means to pause one activity in order to do another. He stopped in order to buy medicine.
130. I remember _____ him at the conference last year.
A. to meet B. meet C. meeting D. met
Correct Answer: C. meeting
Explanation: Remember + gerund refers to recalling a past action. Remember to meet would mean not to forget a future obligation.
131. Remember _____ the application before the deadline.
A. submitting B. submitted C. submit D. to submit
Correct Answer: D. to submit
Explanation: Remember + to-infinitive means to remember to perform an action that is still required.
132. The house needs _____ before the rainy season.
A. repairing B. repair C. repaired D. to repairing
Correct Answer: A. repairing
Explanation: Need + gerund can have a passive meaning: the house needs repairing means the house needs to be repaired.
133. He is too weak _____ the heavy box.
A. lifting B. to lift C. lift D. lifted
Correct Answer: B. to lift
Explanation: The construction too + adjective + to-infinitive expresses that something prevents an action.
134. The question is difficult _____.
A. answering B. answer C. to answer D. answered
Correct Answer: C. to answer
Explanation: Adjectives such as difficult, easy, and hard can be followed by a to-infinitive.
135. _____ the instructions carefully, he completed the task.
A. Read B. Having readed C. Have read D. Having read
Correct Answer: D. Having read
Explanation: The perfect participle having + past participle shows that one action was completed before another. The past participle of read is spelled read.
136. The man _____ at the gate is our new accountant.
A. standing B. stood C. stands D. to stand
Correct Answer: A. standing
Explanation: The present participle standing modifies man and identifies which man is meant.
137. _____ by the sudden noise, the child began to cry.
A. Frightening B. Frightened C. Having frightening D. Frighten
Correct Answer: B. Frightened
Explanation: The past participle expresses the child's state of being frightened. A participle must logically describe the subject.
138. The report _____ yesterday contains several errors.
A. submitting B. submit C. submitted D. to submit
Correct Answer: C. submitted
Explanation: The past participle submitted modifies report and carries a passive meaning: the report that was submitted.
139. I would rather _____ at home than go out in the rain.
A. staying B. to stay C. stayed D. stay
Correct Answer: D. stay
Explanation: Would rather + subject is followed by the bare infinitive when the subject remains the same.
140. She had better _____ a doctor immediately.
A. consult B. to consult C. consulting D. consulted
Correct Answer: A. consult
Explanation: Had better is followed by the bare infinitive, even though had appears to be a past-tense form.
Modals, conditionals and subjunctive forms
141. You _____ obey the rules of the institution.
A. ought B. must C. may to D. can to
Correct Answer: B. must
Explanation: Must expresses obligation and is followed by the base form of the verb.
142. He _____ have missed the train; he left home very late.
A. should B. ought C. may D. must to
Correct Answer: C. may
Explanation: May have + past participle expresses a possibility about the past. Must have missed would express a stronger deduction.
143. You _____ have informed me earlier; now it is too late.
A. should B. can C. may D. will
Correct Answer: A. should
Explanation: Should have + past participle expresses an action that was advisable but was not performed.
144. If I _____ rich, I would help the poor.
A. am B. were C. had been D. will be
Correct Answer: B. were
Explanation: The second conditional uses a past-tense form in the if-clause and would + base verb in the result clause. Were is standard in formal hypothetical expressions.
145. If he had followed the advice, he _____ the loss.
A. avoids B. would avoid C. would have avoided D. will avoid
Correct Answer: C. would have avoided
Explanation: The third conditional describes an unreal past condition and its imagined result.
146. Unless you _____ hard, you will not succeed.
A. will work B. worked C. would work D. work
Correct Answer: D. work
Explanation: Unless means if…not. In future conditional sentences, its clause normally takes the simple present.
147. If water _____ 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure, it boils.
A. reaches B. reached C. will reach D. would reach
Correct Answer: A. reaches
Explanation: A zero conditional expresses a general truth, using the simple present in both clauses.
148. I suggest that he _____ the application without delay.
A. submits B. submit C. submitted D. will submit
Correct Answer: B. submit
Explanation: In formal English, verbs such as suggest, recommend, and insist can be followed by a mandative subjunctive with the base form.
149. It is essential that every candidate _____ present on time.
A. is B. was C. be D. has been
Correct Answer: C. be
Explanation: After expressions of necessity such as it is essential that, formal English uses the subjunctive base form be.
150. The doctor recommended that she _____ more rest.
A. takes B. took C. has taken D. take
Correct Answer: D. take
Explanation: The mandative subjunctive uses the base form after recommend that. Thus, she take is correct in this formal construction.
151. He behaves as if he _____ everything.
A. knew B. knows C. has known D. will know
Correct Answer: A. knew
Explanation: As if + past simple can express an unreal or hypothetical situation in the present.
152. She talks as though she _____ the owner of the company.
A. is B. were C. has been D. will be
Correct Answer: B. were
Explanation: As though + were is a formal hypothetical construction expressing a situation contrary to fact.
153. If he _____ earlier, he could catch the first bus.
A. arrived B. will arrive C. arrives D. had arrived
Correct Answer: C. arrives
Explanation: This is a real or open condition: the simple present in the if-clause can pair with a modal such as could in the main clause.
154. Had I known about the meeting, I _____ attended it.
A. will have B. would C. can have D. would have
Correct Answer: D. would have
Explanation: Inverted third conditionals omit if: Had I known means If I had known. The result clause takes would have + past participle.
155. Were I in your position, I _____ the offer.
A. would accept B. will accept C. accepted D. have accepted
Correct Answer: A. would accept
Explanation: Were I… is an inversion of If I were…, used in hypothetical situations. The result clause normally takes would + base verb.
Verb patterns and commonly tested expressions
156. The teacher asked the students _____ quiet.
A. keep B. to keep C. keeping D. kept
Correct Answer: B. to keep
Explanation: Ask + object + to-infinitive is the standard pattern: asked the students to keep quiet.
157. I would like _____ a complaint about the service.
A. making B. make C. to make D. made
Correct Answer: C. to make
Explanation: Would like is followed by a to-infinitive.
158. The officer prevented him from _____ the restricted area.
A. enter B. to enter C. entered D. entering
Correct Answer: D. entering
Explanation: Prevent + object + from is followed by a gerund.
159. She is capable of _____ the problem independently.
A. solving B. solve C. to solve D. solved
Correct Answer: A. solving
Explanation: A preposition such as of is followed by a noun or gerund, not a bare infinitive.
160. He denied _____ the confidential document.
A. to take B. taking C. take D. taken
Correct Answer: B. taking
Explanation: Deny is followed by a gerund when rejecting an allegation of having done something.
161. The suspect admitted _____ the money.
A. to steal B. steal C. stealing D. stolen
Correct Answer: C. stealing
Explanation: Admit is followed by a gerund when acknowledging an action.
162. They postponed _____ the final decision.
A. to make B. make C. made D. making
Correct Answer: D. making
Explanation: Postpone takes a gerund. The correct expression is postponed making the decision.
163. The officer insisted on _____ the original documents.
A. seeing B. see C. to see D. saw
Correct Answer: A. seeing
Explanation: The preposition on in insist on must be followed by a noun or gerund.
164. He failed _____ the required documents in time.
A. submitting B. to submit C. submit D. submitted
Correct Answer: B. to submit
Explanation: Fail meaning not to succeed in doing something is followed by a to-infinitive.
165. She appears _____ the instructions.
A. understand B. understanding C. to understand D. understood
Correct Answer: C. to understand
Explanation: Verbs such as appear, seem, and tend are commonly followed by a to-infinitive.
166. I cannot help _____ when I hear that joke.
A. to laugh B. laughed C. laugh D. laughing
Correct Answer: D. laughing
Explanation: Cannot help + gerund means that one cannot avoid doing something.
167. It is no use _____ over spilt milk.
A. crying B. to cry C. cry D. cried
Correct Answer: A. crying
Explanation: The expression it is no use is followed by a gerund.
168. He would sooner _____ than apologise.
A. resigning B. resign C. to resign D. resigned
Correct Answer: B. resign
Explanation: Would sooner + bare infinitive expresses a preference. The same pattern applies to would rather.
169. She persuaded him _____ the job offer.
A. accepting B. accept C. to accept D. accepted
Correct Answer: C. to accept
Explanation: Persuade + object + to-infinitive is the correct verb pattern.
170. The manager allowed the employees _____ early.
A. leaving B. leave C. left D. to leave
Correct Answer: D. to leave
Explanation: Allow + object takes a to-infinitive: allowed the employees to leave.
Error-sensitive verb forms
171. Choose the correct sentence.
A. He has gone to the bank. B. He has went to the bank. C. He have gone to the bank. D. He has go to the bank.
Correct Answer: A. He has gone to the bank.
Explanation: The present perfect requires has/have + past participle. The past participle of go is gone, not went.
172. Choose the correct sentence.
A. She did not went there. B. She did not go there. C. She did not gone there. D. She does not went there.
Correct Answer: B. She did not go there.
Explanation: After did not, use the base form of the verb. The past tense is already expressed by did.
173. Choose the correct sentence.
A. He is knowing the answer. B. He has knowing the answer. C. He knows the answer. D. He know the answer.
Correct Answer: C. He knows the answer.
Explanation: Know is normally a stative verb and is not ordinarily used in the continuous form when referring to possession of knowledge.
174. Choose the correct sentence.
A. I am living here since 2021. B. I live here since 2021. C. I lived here since 2021. D. I have been living here since 2021.
Correct Answer: D. I have been living here since 2021.
Explanation: The present perfect continuous is suitable for an activity that began in the past and continues into the present. Since identifies the starting point.
175. Choose the correct sentence.
A. The train had already left when we arrived. B. The train has already left when we arrived. C. The train already leaves when we arrived. D. The train had already leave when we arrived.
Correct Answer: A. The train had already left when we arrived.
Explanation: The past perfect marks the earlier past event. Its structure is had + past participle.
176. Choose the correct sentence.
A. If I will see him, I will tell him. B. If I see him, I will tell him. C. If I saw him, I will tell him. D. If I had seen him, I will tell him.
Correct Answer: B. If I see him, I will tell him.
Explanation: The first conditional normally uses the simple present in the if-clause and will + base verb in the main clause.
177. Choose the correct sentence.
A. He suggested me to apply. B. He suggested me applying. C. He suggested that I apply. D. He suggested that I applied tomorrow.
Correct Answer: C. He suggested that I apply.
Explanation: Suggest can be followed by a that-clause with the base-form subjunctive. It is not normally followed by object + to-infinitive in this meaning.
178. Choose the correct sentence.
A. I look forward to meet you. B. I look forward to have met you. C. I look forward to met you. D. I look forward to meeting you.
Correct Answer: D. I look forward to meeting you.
Explanation: In this expression, to is a preposition, so it takes the gerund meeting.
179. Choose the correct sentence.
A. He used to play football when he was young. B. He use to played football when he was young. C. He used playing football when he was young. D. He was use to play football when he was young.
Correct Answer: A. He used to play football when he was young.
Explanation: Used to + base verb describes a past habit or state that no longer holds. Do not confuse it with be used to + gerund.
180. Choose the correct sentence.
A. She is used to wake up early. B. She is used to waking up early. C. She used to waking up early. D. She is use to waking up early.
Correct Answer: B. She is used to waking up early.
Explanation: Be used to means be accustomed to. Since to is a preposition here, it is followed by the gerund waking.
Phrasal verbs and contextual usage
181. The meeting was _____ because the chairman was ill.
A. put off B. put out C. put up D. put away
Correct Answer: A. put off
Explanation: Put off means postpone. The meeting was rescheduled for a later time.
182. The police are trying to _____ the cause of the accident.
A. find over B. find out C. find away D. find through
Correct Answer: B. find out
Explanation: Find out means discover or obtain information about something.
183. You must _____ your promise.
A. carry out of B. carry over C. carry out D. carry away
Correct Answer: C. carry out
Explanation: Carry out means perform or fulfil a task, instruction, or plan. It can also mean execute a promise or order.
184. The new policy will _____ next month.
A. come over B. come across C. come out D. come into effect
Correct Answer: D. come into effect
Explanation: Come into effect means become operational or legally effective.
185. The company had to _____ several employees because of financial difficulties.
A. lay off B. lay out C. lay down D. lay in
Correct Answer: A. lay off
Explanation: Lay off means dismiss workers, usually because of economic or business conditions.
186. We must _____ the problem before it becomes worse.
A. deal up B. deal with C. deal over D. deal away
Correct Answer: B. deal with
Explanation: Deal with means handle, manage, or address a problem.
187. The manager asked me to _____ the figures carefully.
A. look after B. look into C. look over D. look down
Correct Answer: C. look over
Explanation: Look over means examine or review something. Look into usually means investigate.
188. The bank will _____ the application after verifying the documents.
A. turn up B. turn over C. turn into D. turn down
Correct Answer: D. turn down
Explanation: Turn down means reject or refuse an offer, request, or application.
189. The project was _____ owing to a lack of funds.
A. called off B. called on C. called up D. called for
Correct Answer: A. called off
Explanation: Call off means cancel an event, activity, or planned undertaking.
190. The officer asked the applicant to _____ the form in capital letters.
A. fill over B. fill in C. fill away D. fill off
Correct Answer: B. fill in
Explanation: Fill in means complete a form by supplying the required information. Fill out is also correct in many varieties of English.
Mixed applications and advanced constructions
191. The book, along with its appendices, _____ published last year.
A. were B. have been C. was D. are
Correct Answer: C. was
Explanation: The head subject is book, which is singular. Along with its appendices does not make the subject plural.
192. Each of the reports _____ carefully before publication.
A. were checked B. have checked C. are checking D. was checked
Correct Answer: D. was checked
Explanation: Each is singular, so the passive verb must be was checked.
193. The candidates, having _____ their answers, left the examination hall.
A. completed B. complete C. completing D. completes
Correct Answer: A. completed
Explanation: The perfect participle construction is having + past participle. The past participle of complete is completed.
194. Had she _____ the instructions, she would not have made the mistake.
A. follows B. followed C. follow D. following
Correct Answer: B. followed
Explanation: In an inverted third conditional, Had she means If she had. It must be followed by the past participle.
195. The sooner you start, the sooner you _____ the task.
A. finished B. have finished C. will finish D. finishing
Correct Answer: C. will finish
Explanation: In a future-oriented the sooner…the sooner construction, the comparative clause can use the simple present, while the result clause uses will.
196. I would rather you _____ the matter confidential.
A. keep B. will keep C. have kept D. kept
Correct Answer: D. kept
Explanation: Would rather + subject is followed by a past-tense form to express a present or future preference. The meaning is not necessarily past.
197. It is time the authorities _____ effective measures.
A. took B. take C. will take D. have taken
Correct Answer: A. took
Explanation: It is time + subject is traditionally followed by the past simple when the speaker means that an action should happen now.
198. The teacher, along with the students, _____ going to the museum tomorrow.
A. are B. is C. have D. were
Correct Answer: B. is
Explanation: The singular subject teacher controls the verb. The phrase along with the students is supplementary.
199. By the time the new branch opens, the bank _____ all the necessary staff.
A. recruits B. recruited C. will have recruited D. has recruited
Correct Answer: C. will have recruited
Explanation: The future perfect describes an action that will be completed before a specified future event.
200. Choose the grammatically correct sentence.
A. Neither the manager nor the officers was present. B. Each of the applicants have submitted their forms. C. If he would have studied, he would have passed. D. No sooner had the bell rung than the students rushed out.
Correct Answer: D. No sooner had the bell rung than the students rushed out.
Explanation: No sooner is followed by inversion using had + subject + past participle, and the next clause begins with than. In standard exam grammar, A requires were, B requires has, and C should use if he had studied.
