Section A: Synonyms (Questions 1–50)
1. Choose the synonym of “Abate.”
A. Intensify B. Diminish C. Accelerate D. Accumulate
Correct Answer: B. Diminish Explanation: Abate means to become less intense or severe. Diminish means to reduce in size, strength, or importance.
2. Choose the synonym of “Abhor.”
A. Admire B. Tolerate C. Detest D. Appreciate
Correct Answer: C. Detest Explanation: Abhor means to hate or loathe intensely. Detest expresses strong dislike or hatred.
3. Choose the synonym of “Abundant.”
A. Plentiful B. Scarce C. Insufficient D. Limited
Correct Answer: A. Plentiful Explanation: Abundant means available in large quantities. Plentiful is a close synonym, particularly when describing resources or supplies.
4. Choose the synonym of “Acquiesce.”
A. Protest B. Refuse C. Challenge D. Consent
Correct Answer: D. Consent Explanation: Acquiesce means to accept or comply with something reluctantly or without protest. Consent means to agree or give permission.
5. Choose the synonym of “Acrimony.”
A. Affection B. Bitterness C. Harmony D. Courtesy
Correct Answer: B. Bitterness Explanation: Acrimony refers to bitterness or hostility in speech, behaviour, or relationships. It commonly describes heated disputes.
6. Choose the synonym of “Adversity.”
A. Prosperity B. Advantage C. Hardship D. Comfort
Correct Answer: C. Hardship Explanation: Adversity means a difficult or unfavourable situation. Hardship refers to suffering or severe difficulty.
7. Choose the synonym of “Alleviate.”
A. Relieve B. Aggravate C. Intensify D. Prolong
Correct Answer: A. Relieve Explanation: Alleviate means to make pain, distress, or a problem less severe. For example, effective policies may alleviate poverty.
8. Choose the synonym of “Ambiguous.”
A. Precise B. Obvious C. Definite D. Equivocal
Correct Answer: D. Equivocal Explanation: Ambiguous means open to more than one interpretation. Equivocal describes something unclear or deliberately open to multiple meanings.
9. Choose the synonym of “Amiable.”
A. Hostile B. Cordial C. Arrogant D. Irritable
Correct Answer: B. Cordial Explanation: Amiable means friendly and pleasant. Cordial conveys warmth, friendliness, and goodwill.
10. Choose the synonym of “Anomaly.”
A. Regularity B. Similarity C. Irregularity D. Uniformity
Correct Answer: C. Irregularity Explanation: An anomaly is something that deviates from what is normal or expected. An irregularity is a departure from the usual pattern.
11. Choose the synonym of “Apathy.”
A. Indifference B. Enthusiasm C. Compassion D. Anxiety
Correct Answer: A. Indifference Explanation: Apathy means a lack of interest, enthusiasm, or concern. Indifference similarly indicates little or no emotional engagement.
12. Choose the synonym of “Arduous.”
A. Effortless B. Pleasant C. Convenient D. Laborious
Correct Answer: D. Laborious Explanation: Arduous means requiring great effort or endurance. Laborious describes work that demands considerable time and effort.
13. Choose the synonym of “Astute.”
A. Naive B. Shrewd C. Careless D. Gullible
Correct Answer: B. Shrewd Explanation: Astute means perceptive and capable of making sound judgments. Shrewd describes someone who understands situations quickly and judges them intelligently.
14. Choose the synonym of “Austere.”
A. Luxurious B. Decorative C. Stern D. Extravagant
Correct Answer: C. Stern Explanation: Austere can mean severe, strict, or lacking luxury. Stern is appropriate when the word refers to a strict manner or attitude.
15. Choose the synonym of “Benevolent.”
A. Charitable B. Malevolent C. Selfish D. Vindictive
Correct Answer: A. Charitable Explanation: Benevolent means kind, generous, and concerned about others' welfare. Charitable emphasises generosity and willingness to help those in need.
16. Choose the synonym of “Bolster.”
A. Weaken B. Undermine C. Oppose D. Strengthen
Correct Answer: D. Strengthen Explanation: Bolster means to support or strengthen something. For example, investment can bolster economic growth.
17. Choose the synonym of “Candid.”
A. Evasive B. Frank C. Deceptive D. Reserved
Correct Answer: B. Frank Explanation: Candid means honest, direct, and open. Frank describes speech that expresses one's views without concealment.
18. Choose the synonym of “Capricious.”
A. Consistent B. Predictable C. Fickle D. Dependable
Correct Answer: C. Fickle Explanation: Capricious means changing suddenly and unpredictably. Fickle describes someone whose opinions or preferences change frequently.
19. Choose the synonym of “Censure.”
A. Condemn B. Commend C. Endorse D. Applaud
Correct Answer: A. Condemn Explanation: Censure means to express strong disapproval of someone or something. It is often used in formal or institutional contexts.
20. Choose the synonym of “Coerce.”
A. Persuade B. Encourage C. Entreat D. Compel
Correct Answer: D. Compel Explanation: Coerce means to force someone to act through threats, pressure, or intimidation. Compel means to force or oblige someone to do something.
21. Choose the synonym of “Concise.”
A. Lengthy B. Succinct C. Repetitive D. Elaborate
Correct Answer: B. Succinct Explanation: Concise means expressing an idea clearly in few words. Succinct similarly emphasises brevity without unnecessary detail.
22. Choose the synonym of “Concur.”
A. Disagree B. Object C. Agree D. Contradict
Correct Answer: C. Agree Explanation: Concur means to agree with an opinion, decision, or conclusion. In formal writing, one may concur with another person's assessment.
23. Choose the synonym of “Conspicuous.”
A. Noticeable B. Hidden C. Obscure D. Unremarkable
Correct Answer: A. Noticeable Explanation: Conspicuous means easily seen or noticed. A conspicuous error is one that attracts immediate attention.
24. Choose the synonym of “Cordial.”
A. Antagonistic B. Indifferent C. Resentful D. Warm
Correct Answer: D. Warm Explanation: Cordial means friendly, sincere, and pleasant. It often describes greetings, relations, or conversations.
25. Choose the synonym of “Credible.”
A. Implausible B. Believable C. Dubious D. Absurd
Correct Answer: B. Believable Explanation: Credible means capable of being believed or trusted. A credible explanation is one that appears reasonable and convincing.
26. Choose the synonym of “Cursory.”
A. Thorough B. Exhaustive C. Superficial D. Meticulous
Correct Answer: C. Superficial Explanation: Cursory means quick and not thorough. A cursory examination may overlook important details.
27. Choose the synonym of “Debilitate.”
A. Weaken B. Reinforce C. Fortify D. Revitalise
Correct Answer: A. Weaken Explanation: Debilitate means to make someone or something weak. A prolonged illness may debilitate a person.
28. Choose the synonym of “Deference.”
A. Defiance B. Contempt C. Disregard D. Respect
Correct Answer: D. Respect Explanation: Deference means respectful submission to another person's judgment, authority, or wishes. It often implies acknowledging someone's seniority or status.
29. Choose the synonym of “Delineate.”
A. Conceal B. Describe C. Distort D. Omit
Correct Answer: B. Describe Explanation: Delineate means to describe, outline, or portray something precisely. Reports often delineate responsibilities or procedures.
30. Choose the synonym of “Diligent.”
A. Negligent B. Indolent C. Industrious D. Careless
Correct Answer: C. Industrious Explanation: Diligent means careful and persistent in one's work. Industrious emphasises being hardworking and productive.
31. Choose the synonym of “Disparage.”
A. Belittle B. Applaud C. Celebrate D. Exalt
Correct Answer: A. Belittle Explanation: Disparage means to speak about someone or something in a way that shows disrespect. Belittle means to make someone or something seem less important.
32. Choose the synonym of “Disseminate.”
A. Suppress B. Withhold C. Conceal D. Circulate
Correct Answer: D. Circulate Explanation: Disseminate means to spread information, knowledge, or ideas widely. Governments disseminate public information through official channels.
33. Choose the synonym of “Eclectic.”
A. Narrow B. Diverse C. Uniform D. Restricted
Correct Answer: B. Diverse Explanation: Eclectic describes a style or approach drawing ideas from a wide range of sources. Diverse captures the variety involved, although it is not identical in every context.
34. Choose the synonym of “Eloquent.”
A. Inarticulate B. Hesitant C. Articulate D. Taciturn
Correct Answer: C. Articulate Explanation: Eloquent means fluent, persuasive, and effective in expressing ideas. An eloquent speaker communicates thoughts with clarity and force.
35. Choose the synonym of “Ephemeral.”
A. Fleeting B. Enduring C. Permanent D. Eternal
Correct Answer: A. Fleeting Explanation: Ephemeral refers to something that lasts for a very short time. Fleeting is a close synonym, especially for experiences, feelings, or opportunities.
36. Choose the synonym of “Equanimity.”
A. Agitation B. Panic C. Irritation D. Composure
Correct Answer: D. Composure Explanation: Equanimity means mental calmness and emotional stability, particularly under pressure. A composed person remains calm in difficult circumstances.
37. Choose the synonym of “Eradicate.”
A. Cultivate B. Eliminate C. Preserve D. Generate
Correct Answer: B. Eliminate Explanation: Eradicate means to destroy or remove something completely. Public health programmes may aim to eradicate a disease.
38. Choose the synonym of “Exacerbate.”
A. Mitigate B. Relieve C. Aggravate D. Alleviate
Correct Answer: C. Aggravate Explanation: Exacerbate means to make a problem or negative situation worse. Poor planning can exacerbate an existing crisis.
39. Choose the synonym of “Exonerate.”
A. Acquit B. Convict C. Accuse D. Incriminate
Correct Answer: A. Acquit Explanation: Exonerate means to clear someone of blame or responsibility. Acquit means to find a defendant not guilty in a legal proceeding; their meanings overlap when discussing legal blame.
40. Choose the synonym of “Fastidious.”
A. Sloppy B. Indifferent C. Careless D. Meticulous
Correct Answer: D. Meticulous Explanation: Fastidious means very attentive to detail and demanding about standards. Meticulous describes work done with extreme care and precision.
41. Choose the synonym of “Frugal.”
A. Extravagant B. Thrifty C. Wasteful D. Lavish
Correct Answer: B. Thrifty Explanation: Frugal means economical in the use of money or resources. A thrifty person avoids unnecessary expenditure.
42. Choose the synonym of “Gregarious.”
A. Solitary B. Reserved C. Sociable D. Reclusive
Correct Answer: C. Sociable Explanation: Gregarious describes someone who enjoys the company of others. Sociable is a close synonym in describing personality.
43. Choose the synonym of “Impeccable.”
A. Flawless B. Defective C. Faulty D. Imperfect
Correct Answer: A. Flawless Explanation: Impeccable means free from faults or mistakes. Impeccable conduct or service meets an exceptionally high standard.
44. Choose the synonym of “Impetuous.”
A. Cautious B. Deliberate C. Prudent D. Impulsive
Correct Answer: D. Impulsive Explanation: Impetuous means acting suddenly without sufficient thought. Impulsive similarly describes actions undertaken without careful consideration.
45. Choose the synonym of “Ineffable.”
A. Ordinary B. Indescribable C. Expressible D. Mundane
Correct Answer: B. Indescribable Explanation: Ineffable describes something too great, extraordinary, or intense to be adequately expressed in words. Indescribable is its closest common synonym.
46. Choose the synonym of “Inexorable.”
A. Flexible B. Lenient C. Relentless D. Merciful
Correct Answer: C. Relentless Explanation: Inexorable means impossible to stop, prevent, or persuade to change. It often describes forces, demands, or processes that cannot be resisted.
47. Choose the synonym of “Laconic.”
A. Terse B. Verbose C. Lengthy D. Rambling
Correct Answer: A. Terse Explanation: Laconic means using very few words. Terse also means brief, though it can suggest abruptness or a lack of warmth.
48. Choose the synonym of “Lucid.”
A. Confusing B. Obscure C. Vague D. Clear
Correct Answer: D. Clear Explanation: Lucid means clear and easy to understand. A lucid explanation presents complex information in an understandable way.
49. Choose the synonym of “Magnanimous.”
A. Petty B. Generous C. Vindictive D. Resentful
Correct Answer: B. Generous Explanation: Magnanimous means generous and forgiving, especially toward a rival or someone who has wronged you. It suggests nobility of character rather than generosity alone.
50. Choose the synonym of “Mitigate.”
A. Intensify B. Aggravate C. Alleviate D. Exacerbate
Correct Answer: C. Alleviate Explanation: Mitigate means to make something less severe, harmful, or painful. For example, safeguards can mitigate financial risks.
Section B: Synonyms (Questions 51–100)
51. Choose the synonym of “Obdurate.”
A. Yielding B. Compassionate C. Flexible D. Stubborn
Correct Answer: D. Stubborn Explanation: Obdurate means refusing to change one's opinion or course of action despite persuasion. It often implies unreasonable resistance.
52. Choose the synonym of “Obfuscate.”
A. Clarify B. Confuse C. Simplify D. Illuminate
Correct Answer: B. Confuse Explanation: Obfuscate means to make something unclear or difficult to understand. It is often used when someone deliberately obscures facts.
53. Choose the synonym of “Obsolete.”
A. Modern B. Current C. Outdated D. Innovative
Correct Answer: C. Outdated Explanation: Obsolete means no longer used or useful, usually because something newer has replaced it. An obsolete system may no longer meet current requirements.
54. Choose the synonym of “Ominous.”
A. Menacing B. Reassuring C. Promising D. Cheerful
Correct Answer: A. Menacing Explanation: Ominous suggests that something bad or threatening is likely to happen. Menacing emphasises the sense of danger or threat.
55. Choose the synonym of “Ostensible.”
A. Genuine B. Hidden C. Undisputed D. Apparent
Correct Answer: D. Apparent Explanation: Ostensible means stated or appearing to be true, although it may not reflect the actual situation. For example, the ostensible reason for a decision may differ from the real motive.
56. Choose the synonym of “Perfunctory.”
A. Thorough B. Superficial C. Enthusiastic D. Meticulous
Correct Answer: B. Superficial Explanation: Perfunctory describes an action performed routinely with little care or interest. A perfunctory inspection may fail to identify serious defects.
57. Choose the synonym of “Perilous.”
A. Secure B. Harmless C. Hazardous D. Comfortable
Correct Answer: C. Hazardous Explanation: Perilous means full of danger or risk. Hazardous is commonly used for dangerous conditions, substances, or activities.
58. Choose the synonym of “Pernicious.”
A. Harmful B. Beneficial C. Harmless D. Constructive
Correct Answer: A. Harmful Explanation: Pernicious means causing serious harm, often gradually or subtly. Corruption can have pernicious effects on public institutions.
59. Choose the synonym of “Placate.”
A. Provoke B. Irritate C. Antagonise D. Appease
Correct Answer: D. Appease Explanation: Placate means to calm an angry or dissatisfied person. Appease means to satisfy or pacify someone, often by making concessions.
60. Choose the synonym of “Pragmatic.”
A. Idealistic B. Practical C. Impractical D. Theoretical
Correct Answer: B. Practical Explanation: Pragmatic means dealing with problems in a realistic and practical way. A pragmatic policy prioritises workable solutions over abstract ideals.
61. Choose the synonym of “Precarious.”
A. Stable B. Secure C. Uncertain D. Dependable
Correct Answer: C. Uncertain Explanation: Precarious means unstable, insecure, or dependent on uncertain conditions. A precarious financial position may deteriorate quickly.
62. Choose the synonym of “Preclude.”
A. Prevent B. Permit C. Encourage D. Facilitate
Correct Answer: A. Prevent Explanation: Preclude means to prevent something from happening or make it impossible. Proper planning can preclude avoidable errors.
63. Choose the synonym of “Proclivity.”
A. Aversion B. Objection C. Resistance D. Inclination
Correct Answer: D. Inclination Explanation: Proclivity means a natural tendency or preference. A proclivity for reading indicates a habitual inclination towards reading.
64. Choose the synonym of “Prolific.”
A. Unproductive B. Productive C. Sterile D. Inactive
Correct Answer: B. Productive Explanation: Prolific means producing many works, results, or offspring. A prolific writer publishes a large number of works.
65. Choose the synonym of “Prudent.”
A. Reckless B. Impulsive C. Cautious D. Rash
Correct Answer: C. Cautious Explanation: Prudent means showing good judgment and careful consideration of risks. A prudent investor evaluates potential losses before investing.
66. Choose the synonym of “Quell.”
A. Suppress B. Incite C. Aggravate D. Intensify
Correct Answer: A. Suppress Explanation: Quell means to put an end to a disturbance, rebellion, or strong emotion. Authorities may attempt to quell unrest.
67. Choose the synonym of “Reticent.”
A. Talkative B. Outspoken C. Loquacious D. Reserved
Correct Answer: D. Reserved Explanation: Reticent means reluctant to speak freely about one's thoughts or feelings. It describes a reserved or restrained manner of communication.
68. Choose the synonym of “Resilient.”
A. Fragile B. Adaptable C. Vulnerable D. Brittle
Correct Answer: B. Adaptable Explanation: Resilient means able to recover quickly from difficulty or withstand adversity. Adaptable overlaps with this meaning when the emphasis is on adjusting successfully to changing conditions.
69. Choose the synonym of “Scrutinise.”
A. Ignore B. Overlook C. Examine D. Neglect
Correct Answer: C. Examine Explanation: Scrutinise means to inspect something carefully and critically. Auditors scrutinise financial records to identify discrepancies.
70. Choose the synonym of “Sporadic.”
A. Occasional B. Continuous C. Regular D. Constant
Correct Answer: A. Occasional Explanation: Sporadic means occurring irregularly or at scattered intervals. Sporadic rainfall does not occur consistently.
71. Choose the synonym of “Stringent.”
A. Lenient B. Flexible C. Permissive D. Rigorous
Correct Answer: D. Rigorous Explanation: Stringent means strict, demanding, or tightly controlled. Stringent regulations impose firm requirements.
72. Choose the synonym of “Substantiate.”
A. Refute B. Corroborate C. Contradict D. Disprove
Correct Answer: B. Corroborate Explanation: Substantiate means to support a claim with evidence. Corroborate means to confirm or strengthen a statement using additional evidence.
73. Choose the synonym of “Superfluous.”
A. Essential B. Necessary C. Redundant D. Indispensable
Correct Answer: C. Redundant Explanation: Superfluous means unnecessary or more than what is needed. Redundant information repeats what is already available or adds no useful value.
74. Choose the synonym of “Taciturn.”
A. Uncommunicative B. Talkative C. Expressive D. Garrulous
Correct Answer: A. Uncommunicative Explanation: Taciturn describes a person who speaks very little. It refers to a tendency towards silence rather than simply being quiet on one occasion.
75. Choose the synonym of “Tenacious.”
A. Weak B. Indifferent C. Inconsistent D. Persistent
Correct Answer: D. Persistent Explanation: Tenacious means holding firmly to something or refusing to give up easily. A tenacious candidate continues working towards a difficult goal.
76. Choose the synonym of “Transient.”
A. Permanent B. Temporary C. Lasting D. Enduring
Correct Answer: B. Temporary Explanation: Transient means lasting only for a short period. Temporary economic fluctuations are not necessarily permanent changes.
77. Choose the synonym of “Ubiquitous.”
A. Rare B. Isolated C. Pervasive D. Uncommon
Correct Answer: C. Pervasive Explanation: Ubiquitous means present or found almost everywhere. Pervasive describes something that spreads throughout an area, system, or society.
78. Choose the synonym of “Vacillate.”
A. Waver B. Persist C. Decide D. Commit
Correct Answer: A. Waver Explanation: Vacillate means to keep changing one's mind or opinions. A person who vacillates struggles to reach a firm decision.
79. Choose the synonym of “Venerate.”
A. Despise B. Ridicule C. Disrespect D. Revere
Correct Answer: D. Revere Explanation: Venerate means to regard someone with deep respect or reverence. People may venerate leaders for their exceptional service.
80. Choose the synonym of “Verbose.”
A. Concise B. Wordy C. Terse D. Succinct
Correct Answer: B. Wordy Explanation: Verbose means using more words than necessary. A verbose report may obscure its main message through excessive detail.
81. Choose the synonym of “Viable.”
A. Impracticable B. Unworkable C. Feasible D. Impossible
Correct Answer: C. Feasible Explanation: Viable means capable of working successfully or being sustained. A viable business plan is practical enough to have a reasonable chance of success.
82. Choose the synonym of “Vindicate.”
A. Justify B. Condemn C. Accuse D. Discredit
Correct Answer: A. Justify Explanation: Vindicate means to clear someone of blame or show that a person, decision, or claim was justified. New evidence may vindicate an accused individual.
83. Choose the synonym of “Voracious.”
A. Satisfied B. Moderate C. Restrained D. Insatiable
Correct Answer: D. Insatiable Explanation: Voracious means having an exceptionally great appetite or desire. A voracious reader has an intense appetite for reading.
84. Choose the synonym of “Wary.”
A. Carefree B. Cautious C. Trusting D. Reckless
Correct Answer: B. Cautious Explanation: Wary means alert to possible danger or problems. A wary investor carefully considers the risks of an opportunity.
85. Choose the synonym of “Zealous.”
A. Apathetic B. Indifferent C. Enthusiastic D. Reluctant
Correct Answer: C. Enthusiastic Explanation: Zealous means showing great energy, commitment, or enthusiasm for a cause. A zealous employee works with strong dedication.
86. Choose the synonym of “Alacrity.”
A. Eagerness B. Reluctance C. Hesitation D. Apathy
Correct Answer: A. Eagerness Explanation: Alacrity means cheerful readiness or prompt willingness to act. Accepting an assignment with alacrity suggests enthusiasm and speed.
87. Choose the synonym of “Assiduous.”
A. Idle B. Negligent C. Inattentive D. Industrious
Correct Answer: D. Industrious Explanation: Assiduous means showing great care, attention, and persistent effort. Assiduous preparation improves performance in competitive examinations.
88. Choose the synonym of “Brevity.”
A. Lengthiness B. Conciseness C. Verbosity D. Repetition
Correct Answer: B. Conciseness Explanation: Brevity means the quality of being brief. Conciseness similarly describes expressing information in few words without unnecessary material.
89. Choose the synonym of “Clandestine.”
A. Public B. Open C. Secret D. Transparent
Correct Answer: C. Secret Explanation: Clandestine means kept secret, particularly because an activity is unauthorised or intended to avoid notice. A clandestine meeting is conducted secretly.
90. Choose the synonym of “Dogmatic.”
A. Opinionated B. Open-minded C. Uncertain D. Flexible
Correct Answer: A. Opinionated Explanation: Dogmatic describes someone who asserts opinions as unquestionably correct and is unwilling to consider alternatives. Opinionated overlaps with this meaning, although it does not always imply the same rigidity.
91. Choose the synonym of “Enervate.”
A. Invigorate B. Strengthen C. Energise D. Weaken
Correct Answer: D. Weaken Explanation: Enervate means to deprive someone or something of energy or vitality. Despite its sound, it means the opposite of energise.
92. Choose the synonym of “Futile.”
A. Effective B. Fruitless C. Productive D. Successful
Correct Answer: B. Fruitless Explanation: Futile means incapable of producing a useful result. A futile attempt is unlikely to achieve its intended purpose.
93. Choose the synonym of “Garrulous.”
A. Silent B. Reticent C. Talkative D. Taciturn
Correct Answer: C. Talkative Explanation: Garrulous means excessively talkative, especially about trivial matters. It generally carries a negative connotation.
94. Choose the synonym of “Insipid.”
A. Dull B. Fascinating C. Stimulating D. Exciting
Correct Answer: A. Dull Explanation: Insipid means lacking flavour, interest, or distinctive character. An insipid speech fails to engage its audience.
95. Choose the synonym of “Loquacious.”
A. Reserved B. Quiet C. Taciturn D. Talkative
Correct Answer: D. Talkative Explanation: Loquacious means tending to talk a great deal. It is a useful formal synonym for talkative.
96. Choose the synonym of “Munificent.”
A. Miserly B. Generous C. Stingy D. Frugal
Correct Answer: B. Generous Explanation: Munificent means extremely generous, especially in giving money or gifts. A munificent donation is exceptionally large or generous.
97. Choose the synonym of “Nascent.”
A. Mature B. Established C. Emerging D. Declining
Correct Answer: C. Emerging Explanation: Nascent means beginning to exist or develop. A nascent industry is one that is still in its early stages.
98. Choose the synonym of “Palliate.”
A. Relieve B. Aggravate C. Intensify D. Worsen
Correct Answer: A. Relieve Explanation: Palliate means to lessen the severity of pain, symptoms, or a problem without necessarily removing its underlying cause. It is often used in medical and formal contexts.
99. Choose the synonym of “Recalcitrant.”
A. Obedient B. Compliant C. Cooperative D. Defiant
Correct Answer: D. Defiant Explanation: Recalcitrant describes someone who resists authority or refuses to obey instructions. Defiant emphasises open resistance.
100. Choose the synonym of “Sagacious.”
A. Foolish B. Wise C. Naive D. Impulsive
Correct Answer: B. Wise Explanation: Sagacious means having keen judgment and practical wisdom. A sagacious decision reflects sound understanding and foresight.
Section C: Antonyms (Questions 101–150)
101. Choose the antonym of “Abundant.”
A. Plentiful B. Ample C. Scarce D. Copious
Correct Answer: C. Scarce Explanation: Abundant means available in large quantities, whereas scarce means insufficient or difficult to find. These words express opposite levels of availability.
102. Choose the antonym of “Benevolent.”
A. Malevolent B. Charitable C. Kind D. Generous
Correct Answer: A. Malevolent Explanation: Benevolent means well-intentioned and kind, while malevolent means wishing to cause harm. The prefixes bene- and male- are useful clues to their contrasting meanings.
103. Choose the antonym of “Candid.”
A. Frank B. Honest C. Sincere D. Evasive
Correct Answer: D. Evasive Explanation: Candid means open and honest. Evasive describes someone who avoids giving a direct or straightforward answer.
104. Choose the antonym of “Diligent.”
A. Industrious B. Indolent C. Assiduous D. Hardworking
Correct Answer: B. Indolent Explanation: Diligent means careful and hardworking, whereas indolent means habitually lazy. The two words contrast effort with idleness.
105. Choose the antonym of “Ephemeral.”
A. Fleeting B. Transient C. Enduring D. Temporary
Correct Answer: C. Enduring Explanation: Ephemeral refers to something short-lived. Enduring means continuing over a long period or lasting despite difficulties.
106. Choose the antonym of “Frugal.”
A. Extravagant B. Thrifty C. Economical D. Sparing
Correct Answer: A. Extravagant Explanation: Frugal means economical and careful with resources. Extravagant describes excessive spending or the use of more resources than necessary.
107. Choose the antonym of “Gregarious.”
A. Sociable B. Friendly C. Outgoing D. Reclusive
Correct Answer: D. Reclusive Explanation: Gregarious describes someone who enjoys social interaction. Reclusive describes someone who avoids the company of others.
108. Choose the antonym of “Hostile.”
A. Antagonistic B. Amicable C. Aggressive D. Unfriendly
Correct Answer: B. Amicable Explanation: Hostile means unfriendly or antagonistic, whereas amicable means friendly and peaceful. Amicable negotiations aim to reach an agreement without hostility.
109. Choose the antonym of “Impeccable.”
A. Flawless B. Perfect C. Defective D. Faultless
Correct Answer: C. Defective Explanation: Impeccable means free from faults, while defective means having a fault or imperfection. The contrast concerns the presence or absence of flaws.
110. Choose the antonym of “Laconic.”
A. Verbose B. Terse C. Brief D. Concise
Correct Answer: A. Verbose Explanation: Laconic means using few words, whereas verbose means using more words than necessary. These are common opposites in vocabulary examinations.
111. Choose the antonym of “Mitigate.”
A. Relieve B. Reduce C. Moderate D. Exacerbate
Correct Answer: D. Exacerbate Explanation: Mitigate means to make something less severe. Exacerbate means to make it worse or more intense.
112. Choose the antonym of “Obdurate.”
A. Stubborn B. Compliant C. Inflexible D. Obstinate
Correct Answer: B. Compliant Explanation: Obdurate means stubbornly refusing to change or yield. Compliant means willing to follow instructions or conform to requirements.
113. Choose the antonym of “Optimistic.”
A. Hopeful B. Confident C. Pessimistic D. Positive
Correct Answer: C. Pessimistic Explanation: Optimistic means expecting favourable outcomes. Pessimistic means expecting unfavourable outcomes or focusing on negative possibilities.
114. Choose the antonym of “Prudent.”
A. Reckless B. Cautious C. Sensible D. Judicious
Correct Answer: A. Reckless Explanation: Prudent means careful and wise in making decisions. Reckless describes acting without sufficient regard for danger or consequences.
115. Choose the antonym of “Resilient.”
A. Adaptable B. Tough C. Hardy D. Fragile
Correct Answer: D. Fragile Explanation: Resilient describes the ability to recover from difficulty or withstand pressure. Fragile means easily damaged or broken.
116. Choose the antonym of “Stringent.”
A. Rigorous B. Lenient C. Severe D. Strict
Correct Answer: B. Lenient Explanation: Stringent means strict and demanding. Lenient means permissive or not harsh in applying rules or penalties.
