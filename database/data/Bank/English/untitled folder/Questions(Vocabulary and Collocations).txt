Section A: Vocabulary in Context (1–25)
1. Choose the word closest in meaning to “Abate.”
A. Intensify B. Diminish C. Prolong D. Accelerate
Correct Answer: B. Diminish Explanation: Abate means to become less intense or severe. Example: The storm began to abate after midnight.
2. The word “Prudent” means—
A. Careless B. Impulsive C. Wise and cautious D. Extravagant
Correct Answer: C. Wise and cautious Explanation: Prudent describes someone who makes sensible decisions after considering possible consequences. A prudent investor evaluates risks before investing.
3. Choose the antonym of “Obsolete.”
A. Modern B. Outdated C. Ancient D. Archaic
Correct Answer: A. Modern Explanation: Obsolete means no longer used because something newer exists. Modern expresses the opposite idea.
4. “Mitigate” most nearly means—
A. Magnify B. Prevent completely C. Ignore D. Alleviate
Correct Answer: D. Alleviate Explanation: Mitigate means to make something less severe or harmful. Example: Effective policies can mitigate economic hardship.
5. A person who is “meticulous” is—
A. Reckless B. Extremely careful about details C. Easily distracted D. Unwilling to work
Correct Answer: B. Extremely careful about details Explanation: Meticulous describes great attention to accuracy and detail. A meticulous accountant checks every entry carefully.
6. Choose the closest synonym of “Candid.”
A. Secretive B. Dishonest C. Frank D. Hesitant
Correct Answer: C. Frank Explanation: Candid means honest, direct, and open in expressing opinions. A candid discussion addresses problems without concealing the truth.
7. “Ubiquitous” means—
A. Found everywhere B. Extremely rare C. Difficult to understand D. Completely useless
Correct Answer: A. Found everywhere Explanation: Ubiquitous describes something present or encountered almost everywhere. Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern society.
8. Choose the antonym of “Benevolent.”
A. Generous B. Compassionate C. Charitable D. Malevolent
Correct Answer: D. Malevolent Explanation: Benevolent means kind and well-intentioned, whereas malevolent means wishing harm to others.
9. The word “Ambiguous” means—
A. Precise B. Having more than one possible interpretation C. Obviously false D. Easily measurable
Correct Answer: B. Having more than one possible interpretation Explanation: An ambiguous statement can be understood in different ways. In examinations, distinguish ambiguity from vagueness and outright falsehood.
10. Choose the synonym of “Resilient.”
A. Fragile B. Passive C. Able to recover quickly D. Permanently damaged
Correct Answer: C. Able to recover quickly Explanation: Resilient describes the ability to recover from difficulties or adapt to adversity. A resilient economy can recover from a financial shock.
11. “Incessant” is closest in meaning to—
A. Continuous B. Occasional C. Temporary D. Uncertain
Correct Answer: A. Continuous Explanation: Incessant means continuing without interruption or relief. Incessant noise can make concentration difficult.
12. Choose the antonym of “Hostile.”
A. Aggressive B. Unfriendly C. Antagonistic D. Amicable
Correct Answer: D. Amicable Explanation: Hostile means unfriendly or antagonistic. Amicable means friendly and peaceful, especially when resolving disagreements.
13. “Pragmatic” most nearly means—
A. Theoretical only B. Practical C. Emotional D. Imaginative
Correct Answer: B. Practical Explanation: A pragmatic person focuses on practical results rather than abstract ideals alone. A pragmatic solution is workable under real conditions.
14. Choose the closest meaning of “Eloquent.”
A. Confused B. Taciturn C. Fluent and persuasive D. Inarticulate
Correct Answer: C. Fluent and persuasive Explanation: Eloquent means expressing ideas effectively and persuasively. An eloquent speaker communicates complex arguments clearly.
15. “Ephemeral” means—
A. Lasting for a short time B. Permanent C. Ancient D. Repetitive
Correct Answer: A. Lasting for a short time Explanation: Ephemeral refers to something brief or short-lived. Fame can be ephemeral when public attention quickly shifts elsewhere.
16. Choose the antonym of “Frugal.”
A. Economical B. Thrifty C. Sparing D. Extravagant
Correct Answer: D. Extravagant Explanation: Frugal means careful with money and resources. Extravagant describes excessive or wasteful spending.
17. “Complacent” means—
A. Deeply anxious B. Self-satisfied and insufficiently concerned C. Highly ambitious D. Critically observant
Correct Answer: B. Self-satisfied and insufficiently concerned Explanation: A complacent person may feel too satisfied with the present situation to recognize risks. Complacency can prevent necessary improvement.
18. Choose the synonym of “Diligent.”
A. Negligent B. Indifferent C. Industrious D. Careless
Correct Answer: C. Industrious Explanation: Diligent means hardworking, careful, and persistent. A diligent candidate follows a consistent study routine.
19. “Cumbersome” means—
A. Awkward and difficult to handle B. Compact and convenient C. Swift and efficient D. Light and portable
Correct Answer: A. Awkward and difficult to handle Explanation: Cumbersome describes something unwieldy or inconvenient. A cumbersome administrative process consumes unnecessary time.
20. Choose the antonym of “Scarce.”
A. Limited B. Rare C. Insufficient D. Abundant
Correct Answer: D. Abundant Explanation: Scarce means insufficient or difficult to obtain. Abundant means available in large quantities.
21. “Voracious” most nearly means—
A. Reluctant B. Having an enormous appetite C. Easily satisfied D. Indifferent
Correct Answer: B. Having an enormous appetite Explanation: Voracious commonly describes an extremely strong appetite or an insatiable desire for something. A voracious reader reads extensively.
22. Choose the closest meaning of “Austere.”
A. Luxurious B. Cheerful C. Strict or severely simple D. Elaborately decorated
Correct Answer: C. Strict or severely simple Explanation: Austere can describe a strict manner or a plain, unadorned lifestyle. An austere office may contain only essential furniture.
23. “Plausible” means—
A. Seemingly reasonable or believable B. Definitely proven C. Obviously impossible D. Entirely irrelevant
Correct Answer: A. Seemingly reasonable or believable Explanation: A plausible explanation appears reasonable, but it is not necessarily true. Plausibility should not be confused with proof.
24. Choose the antonym of “Transient.”
A. Brief B. Fleeting C. Temporary D. Permanent
Correct Answer: D. Permanent Explanation: Transient means lasting only briefly. Permanent describes something intended to last indefinitely.
25. “Pristine” most nearly means—
A. Polluted B. Spotlessly clean or in original condition C. Worn out D. Artificially modified
Correct Answer: B. Spotlessly clean or in original condition Explanation: Pristine describes something clean, fresh, or preserved in its original state. A pristine environment has suffered little or no damage.
Section B: Synonyms and Antonyms (26–50)
26. Choose the synonym of “Arduous.”
A. Effortless B. Pleasant C. Laborious D. Superficial
Correct Answer: C. Laborious Explanation: Arduous means requiring considerable effort or endurance. Preparing for a competitive examination can be arduous.
27. Choose the antonym of “Lucid.”
A. Obscure B. Clear C. Intelligible D. Coherent
Correct Answer: A. Obscure Explanation: Lucid means clear and easy to understand. Obscure means unclear or difficult to comprehend.
28. “Alleviate” is synonymous with—
A. Aggravate B. Intensify C. Complicate D. Relieve
Correct Answer: D. Relieve Explanation: Alleviate means to reduce pain, hardship, or severity. Financial assistance may alleviate poverty.
29. Choose the antonym of “Concur.”
A. Agree B. Disagree C. Approve D. Consent
Correct Answer: B. Disagree Explanation: Concur means to agree with an opinion or decision. Its opposite is disagree or dissent.
30. “Inevitable” means—
A. Avoidable B. Uncertain C. Unavoidable D. Improbable
Correct Answer: C. Unavoidable Explanation: Something inevitable cannot be avoided or prevented. If all conditions remain unchanged, a particular outcome may become inevitable.
31. Choose the synonym of “Ameliorate.”
A. Improve B. Deteriorate C. Abandon D. Conceal
Correct Answer: A. Improve Explanation: Ameliorate means to make something better or more satisfactory. Reforms can ameliorate working conditions.
32. Choose the antonym of “Prolific.”
A. Productive B. Fertile C. Abundant D. Unproductive
Correct Answer: D. Unproductive Explanation: Prolific means producing many works, results, or offspring. An unproductive period yields few results.
33. “Scrutinize” means—
A. Overlook B. Examine closely C. Approve automatically D. Summarize briefly
Correct Answer: B. Examine closely Explanation: Scrutinize means to inspect something carefully and critically. Auditors scrutinize financial statements for irregularities.
34. Choose the antonym of “Brittle.”
A. Fragile B. Breakable C. Tough D. Delicate
Correct Answer: C. Tough Explanation: Brittle describes something that breaks easily under stress. Tough suggests strength and resistance to damage.
35. “Ostensible” most nearly means—
A. Apparent or purported B. Secretly planned C. Completely impossible D. Scientifically verified
Correct Answer: A. Apparent or purported Explanation: Ostensible describes what appears to be true or is stated as the reason, though the reality may differ. The ostensible purpose of a meeting may not reveal every underlying motive.
36. Choose the antonym of “Exonerate.”
A. Acquit B. Absolve C. Vindicate D. Incriminate
Correct Answer: D. Incriminate Explanation: Exonerate means to clear someone of blame or responsibility. Incriminate means to make someone appear guilty of an offence.
37. “Capricious” means—
A. Predictable B. Unpredictable and changeable C. Consistently fair D. Carefully planned
Correct Answer: B. Unpredictable and changeable Explanation: A capricious person acts according to sudden or unpredictable changes in mood or desire. Capricious decisions can create uncertainty.
38. Choose the synonym of “Brevity.”
A. Lengthiness B. Complexity C. Conciseness D. Repetition
Correct Answer: C. Conciseness Explanation: Brevity means shortness of expression or duration. In formal writing, brevity is valuable when it preserves clarity.
39. “Dogmatic” most nearly means—
A. Stubbornly asserting beliefs as unquestionably true B. Open to every suggestion C. Unable to form opinions D. Indifferent to principles
Correct Answer: A. Stubbornly asserting beliefs as unquestionably true Explanation: A dogmatic person insists on particular beliefs without sufficient willingness to consider alternatives. The word often carries a negative connotation.
40. Choose the antonym of “Impartial.”
A. Fair B. Neutral C. Unbiased D. Biased
Correct Answer: D. Biased Explanation: Impartial means fair and not favouring one side. Biased means prejudiced in favour of or against someone or something.
41. “Rescind” means—
A. Reinforce B. Revoke C. Establish D. Extend
Correct Answer: B. Revoke Explanation: Rescind means to officially cancel a law, order, agreement, or decision. An authority may rescind a previously issued directive.
42. Choose the synonym of “Tenuous.”
A. Robust B. Substantial C. Weak or slight D. Conclusive
Correct Answer: C. Weak or slight Explanation: Tenuous means thin, weak, or poorly supported. A tenuous argument lacks strong evidence.
43. “Recalcitrant” describes someone who is—
A. Uncooperative and resistant to authority B. Eager to obey C. Easily persuaded D. Generous to others
Correct Answer: A. Uncooperative and resistant to authority Explanation: A recalcitrant person stubbornly resists control or instructions. The word often appears in formal contexts involving discipline or compliance.
44. Choose the antonym of “Ephemeral.”
A. Fleeting B. Momentary C. Short-lived D. Enduring
Correct Answer: D. Enduring Explanation: Ephemeral means short-lived, whereas enduring means lasting for a long time. These are useful opposites in vocabulary questions.
45. “Prerogative” means—
A. Legal punishment B. A special right or privilege C. A public obligation D. An ordinary request
Correct Answer: B. A special right or privilege Explanation: A prerogative is a right or power belonging to a particular person or authority. It is often used in official and constitutional contexts.
46. Choose the synonym of “Gregarious.”
A. Solitary B. Reserved C. Sociable D. Hostile
Correct Answer: C. Sociable Explanation: Gregarious describes someone who enjoys the company of others. A gregarious person generally prefers social interaction to isolation.
47. “Obdurate” means—
A. Stubborn and unwilling to change B. Easily influenced C. Deeply apologetic D. Generously forgiving
Correct Answer: A. Stubborn and unwilling to change Explanation: Obdurate means stubbornly refusing to change one's opinion or behaviour. It is close in meaning to obstinate.
48. Choose the antonym of “Enhance.”
A. Improve B. Strengthen C. Enrich D. Diminish
Correct Answer: D. Diminish Explanation: Enhance means to improve or increase the quality or value of something. Diminish means to reduce it.
49. “Equivocal” means—
A. Unmistakably clear B. Ambiguous or open to different interpretations C. Entirely irrelevant D. Absolutely certain
Correct Answer: B. Ambiguous or open to different interpretations Explanation: An equivocal statement permits more than one interpretation. Clear communication aims to avoid equivocal wording.
50. Choose the synonym of “Vindicate.”
A. Accuse B. Condemn C. Clear from blame D. Conceal evidence
Correct Answer: C. Clear from blame Explanation: Vindicate means to show that someone was right or is free from blame. New evidence may vindicate a wrongly accused person.
Section C: Commonly Tested Vocabulary and Usage (51–75)
51. Choose the correct meaning of “Disseminate.”
A. Spread information widely B. Suppress information C. Collect confidential records D. Distort a message
Correct Answer: A. Spread information widely Explanation: Disseminate means to distribute or spread information, ideas, or knowledge. Government agencies disseminate public notices through official channels.
52. “Exacerbate” means—
A. Resolve completely B. Make less serious C. Examine carefully D. Make worse
Correct Answer: D. Make worse Explanation: Exacerbate means to intensify an existing problem or unpleasant situation. Poor communication can exacerbate a dispute.
53. Choose the correct meaning of “Apathy.”
A. Strong enthusiasm B. Lack of interest or concern C. Sudden anger D. Excessive curiosity
Correct Answer: B. Lack of interest or concern Explanation: Apathy is the absence of interest, enthusiasm, or concern. Public apathy may reduce participation in civic activities.
54. “Insidious” most nearly means—
A. Openly beneficial B. Immediately harmless C. Gradually harmful in a subtle way D. Completely accidental
Correct Answer: C. Gradually harmful in a subtle way Explanation: Insidious describes something harmful that develops or operates gradually and often unnoticed. Insidious corruption can weaken institutions over time.
55. Choose the correct meaning of “Perfunctory.”
A. Done with minimal care or effort B. Completed with exceptional precision C. Conducted with deep enthusiasm D. Deliberately postponed
Correct Answer: A. Done with minimal care or effort Explanation: A perfunctory action is performed routinely without genuine attention or interest. A perfunctory inspection may fail to detect serious defects.
56. “Quandary” means—
A. A definite solution B. A financial reward C. A public celebration D. A difficult situation requiring a choice
Correct Answer: D. A difficult situation requiring a choice Explanation: A quandary is a state of uncertainty or perplexity, especially when choosing between alternatives. The manager faced a quandary over how to allocate limited funds.
57. Choose the correct meaning of “Anomaly.”
A. A common occurrence B. Something that deviates from what is normal C. A universally accepted rule D. A predictable outcome
Correct Answer: B. Something that deviates from what is normal Explanation: An anomaly is an irregularity or exception to an expected pattern. An unusual transaction may be flagged as a financial anomaly.
58. “Impeccable” means—
A. Doubtful B. Incomplete C. Flawless D. Inconsistent
Correct Answer: C. Flawless Explanation: Impeccable means without faults or mistakes. Impeccable documentation helps maintain professional credibility.
59. Choose the correct meaning of “Reticent.”
A. Reserved or reluctant to speak B. Talkative and outgoing C. Aggressive in debate D. Unable to listen
Correct Answer: A. Reserved or reluctant to speak Explanation: A reticent person does not readily reveal thoughts or speak freely. Reticence is not necessarily the same as dishonesty.
60. “Intransigent” means—
A. Flexible in negotiations B. Eager to compromise C. Uncertain about a decision D. Unwilling to compromise
Correct Answer: D. Unwilling to compromise Explanation: An intransigent person refuses to change a position or negotiate. Intransigent attitudes can obstruct dispute resolution.
61. Choose the correct meaning of “Sporadic.”
A. Constant and uninterrupted B. Occurring irregularly or occasionally C. Happening every hour D. Carefully scheduled
Correct Answer: B. Occurring irregularly or occasionally Explanation: Sporadic refers to events that occur at irregular intervals. Sporadic power outages are unpredictable rather than continuous.
62. “Mundane” means—
A. Extraordinary B. Mysterious C. Ordinary and routine D. Highly dangerous
Correct Answer: C. Ordinary and routine Explanation: Mundane describes ordinary, everyday matters. The mundane tasks of administration may still be essential to an organisation.
63. Choose the correct meaning of “Superfluous.”
A. Unnecessary or excessive B. Essential C. Scarce D. Inadequate
Correct Answer: A. Unnecessary or excessive Explanation: Superfluous means more than is needed. Remove superfluous words to make an answer concise.
64. “Pernicious” means—
A. Harmless B. Beneficial C. Temporary D. Highly destructive or harmful
Correct Answer: D. Highly destructive or harmful Explanation: Pernicious describes something that causes serious harm, often gradually. Pernicious rumours can damage public trust.
65. Choose the correct meaning of “Coerce.”
A. Persuade through reason alone B. Force someone through threats or pressure C. Reward voluntary participation D. Permit free choice
Correct Answer: B. Force someone through threats or pressure Explanation: Coerce means to compel someone to act against their will through force, threats, or pressure. It differs from voluntary persuasion.
66. “Altruistic” means—
A. Self-centred B. Greedy C. Concerned with others' welfare D. Indifferent to suffering
Correct Answer: C. Concerned with others' welfare Explanation: Altruistic describes selfless concern for the well-being of others. An altruistic volunteer helps people without expecting personal gain.
67. Choose the correct meaning of “Dogged.”
A. Persistent and determined B. Careless and distracted C. Easily discouraged D. Naturally suspicious
Correct Answer: A. Persistent and determined Explanation: Dogged means showing tenacious determination. Dogged efforts can eventually overcome difficult obstacles.
68. “Laconic” means—
A. Verbose B. Dramatic C. Ambiguous D. Using very few words
Correct Answer: D. Using very few words Explanation: Laconic describes brief expression that uses few words. A laconic reply may be clear but extremely short.
69. Choose the correct meaning of “Magnanimous.”
A. Petty and vindictive B. Generous and forgiving C. Suspicious and cautious D. Proud and arrogant
Correct Answer: B. Generous and forgiving Explanation: A magnanimous person shows generosity of spirit, especially towards a rival or someone who has caused offence. Magnanimity is associated with noble-mindedness.
70. “Precarious” means—
A. Secure B. Predictable C. Unstable or uncertain D. Permanent
Correct Answer: C. Unstable or uncertain Explanation: Precarious describes a situation that is insecure or likely to become dangerous. A precarious financial position leaves little room for unexpected losses.
71. Choose the correct meaning of “Cursory.”
A. Hasty and not thorough B. Comprehensive C. Meticulous D. Prolonged and detailed
Correct Answer: A. Hasty and not thorough Explanation: A cursory review gives only brief attention to a subject. A cursory check is insufficient when accuracy is critical.
72. “Infallible” means—
A. Often mistaken B. Easily deceived C. Uncertain D. Incapable of making mistakes
Correct Answer: D. Incapable of making mistakes Explanation: Infallible means incapable of error or failure. In practice, no human decision-maker should automatically be assumed infallible.
73. Choose the correct meaning of “Conspicuous.”
A. Hidden B. Easily noticeable C. Unimportant D. Difficult to detect
Correct Answer: B. Easily noticeable Explanation: Conspicuous describes something that stands out or attracts attention. A conspicuous error is readily visible.
74. “Pugnacious” means—
A. Peace-loving B. Timid C. Eager to fight or argue D. Cooperative
Correct Answer: C. Eager to fight or argue Explanation: Pugnacious describes a combative or quarrelsome disposition. A pugnacious debater often seeks confrontation.
75. Choose the correct meaning of “Pristine.”
A. Unspoiled and in excellent original condition B. Damaged beyond repair C. Artificially coloured D. Old-fashioned and useless
Correct Answer: A. Unspoiled and in excellent original condition Explanation: Pristine refers to something remarkably clean or untouched. The word can describe natural environments as well as objects.
Section D: Collocations—Natural Word Combinations (76–110)
76. Choose the correct collocation.
The committee will ______ a decision tomorrow.
A. Do B. Make up C. Create out D. Make
Correct Answer: D. Make Explanation: The standard collocation is make a decision. English uses make with many decisions, arrangements, and judgments.
77. Choose the correct word.
The bank must ______ an investigation into the suspicious transaction.
A. Do B. Conduct C. Build D. Draw
Correct Answer: B. Conduct Explanation: Conduct an investigation is the standard formal collocation. Carry out an investigation is also correct.
78. Choose the correct collocation.
The government aims to ______ poverty.
A. Commit poverty B. Perform poverty C. Alleviate poverty D. Execute poverty
Correct Answer: C. Alleviate poverty Explanation: Alleviate poverty means to reduce the hardship associated with poverty. Reduce poverty and combat poverty are also natural combinations.
79. Choose the correct collocation.
All employees must ______ attention to the new instructions.
A. Pay B. Give out C. Take D. Draw
Correct Answer: A. Pay Explanation: The established expression is pay attention to. For example, candidates should pay attention to qualifying words in MCQs.
80. Choose the correct word.
The company hopes to ______ a profit this year.
A. Win B. Earn out C. Take D. Make
Correct Answer: D. Make Explanation: Make a profit is the standard collocation. Earn a profit is also possible, but make a profit is the usual exam answer.
81. Choose the correct collocation.
The manager will ______ responsibility for the mistake.
A. Do B. Take C. Build D. Put
Correct Answer: B. Take Explanation: Take responsibility for means to accept accountability. The related expression assume responsibility is also correct.
82. Choose the correct word.
The central bank seeks to ______ financial stability.
A. Do B. Perform C. Maintain D. Execute
Correct Answer: C. Maintain Explanation: Maintain financial stability is a standard institutional collocation. Preserve financial stability is another acceptable expression.
83. Choose the correct collocation.
The officer must ______ a report on the incident.
A. Submit B. Commit C. Insist D. Persist
Correct Answer: A. Submit Explanation: Submit a report means to present it formally to the relevant authority. Prepare a report is correct when the emphasis is on writing it.
84. Choose the correct word.
The new policy may have a significant ______ on employment.
A. Affect B. Effort C. Resulting D. Impact
Correct Answer: D. Impact Explanation: The collocation is have an impact on something. Impact is a noun here; affect is commonly used as a verb.
85. Choose the correct collocation.
The witness gave ______ evidence in court.
A. Powerful B. Compelling C. Heavy D. Strongly
Correct Answer: B. Compelling Explanation: Compelling evidence is evidence that strongly persuades or supports a conclusion. Strong evidence is also correct, but compelling evidence is especially precise here.
86. Choose the correct word.
The two parties finally reached a ______ agreement.
A. Heavy B. Solidly C. Mutual D. Deep
Correct Answer: C. Mutual Explanation: Mutual agreement means an agreement shared by the parties concerned. Reach an agreement is the standard verb–noun collocation.
87. Choose the correct collocation.
The authority should ______ strict measures against corruption.
A. Take B. Do C. Make D. Perform
Correct Answer: A. Take Explanation: Take measures is a standard collocation. Implement measures and introduce measures are also acceptable in appropriate contexts.
88. Choose the correct word.
The country experienced ______ economic growth.
A. Deep B. Tall C. Wide D. Rapid
Correct Answer: D. Rapid Explanation: Rapid economic growth is a common collocation. Strong, sustained, and robust economic growth are also widely used.
89. Choose the correct collocation.
The new rules will ______ effect next month.
A. Take to B. Take C. Make D. Bring on
Correct Answer: B. Take Explanation: Take effect means to become operative or legally applicable. Example: The new regulations will take effect on 1 November.
90. Choose the correct word.
The company must ______ with all relevant regulations.
A. Agree B. Fulfil to C. Comply D. Obey to
Correct Answer: C. Comply Explanation: The correct construction is comply with regulations. Although obey regulations is correct, obey to regulations is not.
91. Choose the correct collocation.
The bank decided to ______ a loan to the applicant.
A. Grant B. Commit C. Deliver out D. Set
Correct Answer: A. Grant Explanation: Grant a loan means to approve or provide a loan. Banks may also extend or issue credit, depending on context.
92. Choose the correct word.
The auditor discovered a ______ error in the accounts.
A. Broad B. Spacious C. Lengthy D. Significant
Correct Answer: D. Significant Explanation: Significant error is a natural collocation for an error of considerable importance. In auditing, material misstatement is a more specialised technical term.
93. Choose the correct collocation.
The minister promised to ______ a thorough review of the policy.
A. Make B. Undertake C. Commit D. Build
Correct Answer: B. Undertake Explanation: Undertake a review means to begin and carry out a systematic examination. Conduct a review is also correct.
94. Choose the correct word.
The two countries signed a ______ treaty.
A. Hard B. Heavy C. Bilateral D. Strongly
Correct Answer: C. Bilateral Explanation: A bilateral treaty is an agreement between two parties, commonly two states. A multilateral treaty involves more than two parties.
95. Choose the correct collocation.
The bank is committed to ______ customer satisfaction.
A. Ensuring B. Doing C. Making D. Executing
Correct Answer: A. Ensuring Explanation: Ensure customer satisfaction means to make certain that customers are satisfied. The construction committed to ensuring uses a gerund after the preposition to.
96. Choose the correct word.
The board will ______ a final decision after reviewing the evidence.
A. Do B. Set C. Create out D. Reach
Correct Answer: D. Reach Explanation: Reach a decision is a standard collocation, similar to make a decision. Both are grammatically correct.
97. Choose the correct collocation.
The organisation aims to ______ awareness of cybercrime.
A. Do B. Raise C. Lift up D. Grow
Correct Answer: B. Raise Explanation: Raise awareness means to increase public knowledge or understanding of an issue. Awareness campaigns commonly use this expression.
98. Choose the correct word.
The bank needs to ______ a balance between risk and profitability.
A. Make B. Create out C. Strike D. Hit on
Correct Answer: C. Strike Explanation: Strike a balance between A and B means to achieve a reasonable compromise between competing objectives. Banks balance profitability against risk.
99. Choose the correct collocation.
The authority must ______ action against fraudulent activities.
A. Take B. Do C. Make D. Set
Correct Answer: A. Take Explanation: Take action against means to act to stop or address wrongdoing. Take action on a problem is also possible in other contexts.
100. Choose the correct word.
The company suffered a ______ loss during the financial crisis.
A. Tall B. Deeply C. Broad D. Heavy
Correct Answer: D. Heavy Explanation: Suffer a heavy loss is a conventional collocation. Suffer substantial losses is another common formal expression.
101. Choose the correct collocation.
The committee will ______ an inquiry into the allegations.
A. Make B. Hold C. Do out D. Build
Correct Answer: B. Hold Explanation: Hold an inquiry means to conduct an official investigation or examination. Conduct an inquiry is also acceptable.
102. Choose the correct word.
The manager gave ______ instructions to the staff.
A. Hard B. Heavy C. Clear D. Deep
Correct Answer: C. Clear Explanation: Clear instructions are easy to understand and follow. Clear instructions reduce mistakes in the workplace.
103. Choose the correct collocation.
The organisation must ______ its objectives within the given deadline.
A. Achieve B. Do C. Make D. Perform out
Correct Answer: A. Achieve Explanation: Achieve objectives means to accomplish intended goals. Meet objectives and fulfil objectives are also possible.
104. Choose the correct word.
The government has introduced measures to ______ inflation.
A. Commit B. Conduct C. Fulfil D. Curb
Correct Answer: D. Curb Explanation: Curb inflation means to restrain or reduce the rate at which prices rise. Central banks may use monetary policy to curb inflationary pressure.
105. Choose the correct collocation.
The employee was dismissed for ______ misconduct.
A. Doing B. Committing C. Performing D. Making
Correct Answer: B. Committing Explanation: Commit misconduct is possible in formal usage, particularly as commit an act of misconduct. More commonly, English says engage in misconduct or be guilty of misconduct; the best option among those given is B.
106. Choose the correct word.
The new system will ______ efficiency in processing applications.
A. Make B. Do C. Improve D. Commit
Correct Answer: C. Improve Explanation: Improve efficiency means to make a process more effective or less wasteful. The phrase is common in business and administrative English.
107. Choose the correct collocation.
The bank must ______ a close eye on suspicious transactions.
A. Keep B. Hold C. Make D. Put
Correct Answer: A. Keep Explanation: Keep a close eye on means to monitor something carefully. Banks monitor suspicious transactions to identify potential fraud.
108. Choose the correct word.
The proposal received ______ support from the board.
A. Tall B. Heavy C. Deeply D. Widespread
Correct Answer: D. Widespread Explanation: Widespread support means support from many people or groups. The adjective widespread naturally modifies abstract nouns such as support, concern, and opposition.
109. Choose the correct collocation.
The officer was asked to ______ an explanation for the discrepancy.
A. Say B. Provide C. Tell out D. Speak
Correct Answer: B. Provide Explanation: Provide an explanation is a standard formal collocation. Give an explanation is equally natural in many contexts.
110. Choose the correct word.
The company must ______ its commitment to ethical practices.
A. Do B. Make C. Honour D. Perform
Correct Answer: C. Honour Explanation: Honour a commitment means to fulfil a promise or obligation. In American English, honor is the usual spelling.
Section E: Advanced Collocations and Word Partnerships (111–135)
111. Choose the correct collocation.
The central bank is responsible for ______ monetary policy.
A. Formulating B. Doing out C. Building up D. Saying
Correct Answer: A. Formulating Explanation: Formulate monetary policy means to develop and specify policy measures. Central banks formulate policy to pursue objectives such as price stability.

136. Choose the correct word.
The new measures may ______ the quality of public services.

137. Choose the correct word.
The new law will come into ______ next month.
A. Affect B. Effect C. Effective D. Affection
Correct Answer: B. Effect Explanation: Come into effect means to become operative. Remember: affect is usually a verb, while effect is usually a noun.
138. Choose the correct word.
The manager tried to ______ the employees that the change was necessary.
A. Assure B. Ensure C. Persuade D. Insure
Correct Answer: C. Persuade Explanation: Persuade someone that something is true or necessary is correct. Assure means to tell someone confidently, ensure means to make certain, and insure relates to insurance.
139. Choose the correct word.
The bank will ______ that all documents are complete.
A. Ensure B. Assure C. Insure D. Reassure
Correct Answer: A. Ensure Explanation: Ensure means to make certain that something happens or is the case. Here, the bank must make certain that all documents are complete.
140. Choose the correct word.
The auditor examined the ______ records of the company.

141. Choose the correct word.
The candidate gave a ______ account of the incident.

142. Choose the correct word.
The officer's explanation was ______ with the evidence.

143. Choose the correct word.

144. Choose the correct word.
The board decided to ______ a new code of conduct.

145. Choose the correct word.
The manager's remarks were ______ to the issue under discussion.

146. Choose the correct word.
The bank must ______ with the new reporting requirements.

147. Choose the correct word.
The witness was asked to ______ the accuracy of the statement.

148. Choose the correct word.

156. What does “look into” mean?

158. What does “come across” mean in “I came across an old document”?

159. What does “put off” mean?

161. What does “rule out” mean?

162. What does “back up” mean in a discussion of evidence?

163. What does “fall through” mean?

Correct Answer: A. Fail to happen as planned Explanation: A plan or agreement falls through when it fails to materialise. The proposed merger fell through because the parties could not agree on the terms.

165. What does “cut down on” mean?

167. What does “look down on” mean?

Correct Answer: A. Regard as inferior Explanation: Look down on means to consider someone inferior or less worthy. Professional respect requires avoiding attitudes that look down on others.
168. What does “make up for” mean?

169. What does “turn down” mean in “The bank turned down the application”?

170. What does “set aside” mean in a financial context?

171. What does “brush up on” mean?

172. What does “phase out” mean?

173. What does “zero in on” mean?

174. What does “in the long run” mean?

175. What does “at stake” mean?
