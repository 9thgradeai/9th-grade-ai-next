Section 1: Fundamentals and Identification (1–25)
1. Identify the gerund in the sentence: “Swimming is an excellent exercise.”
A. Is
B. Excellent
C. Swimming
D. Exercise
Correct Answer: C. Swimming
Explanation: A gerund is an -ing form of a verb that functions as a noun. Here, “Swimming” is the subject of the sentence.
2. Which sentence contains a gerund?
A. The crying baby needs attention.
B. She enjoys reading novels.
C. The running water is cold.
D. The sleeping child is my brother.
Correct Answer: B. She enjoys reading novels.
Explanation: “Reading” functions as the object of the verb “enjoys,” so it is a gerund. In the other sentences, the -ing forms function as modifiers.
3. Identify the present participle in “The girl standing near the gate is my sister.”
A. Girl
B. Standing
C. Gate
D. Sister
Correct Answer: B. Standing
Explanation: “Standing” is a present participle modifying “girl.” It describes which girl is being referred to rather than functioning as a noun.
4. Which sentence uses an -ing form as a gerund?
A. The barking dog frightened the child.
B. He is barking at the stranger.
C. Barking loudly, the dog ran away.
D. Barking annoys the neighbours.
Correct Answer: D. Barking annoys the neighbours.
Explanation: “Barking” functions as the subject of the sentence and is therefore a gerund. In A and C, it describes the dog; in B, it forms part of the progressive verb phrase.
5. Identify the past participle in “The broken window was repaired yesterday.”
A. Broken
B. Window
C. Repaired
D. Yesterday
Correct Answer: A. Broken
Explanation: “Broken” is the past participle of “break” and modifies “window.” “Repaired” is also a past participle, used here in a passive construction.
6. Which of the following is a gerund phrase?
A. The tall building
B. Walking along the river
C. Very quickly
D. Has been working
Correct Answer: B. Walking along the river
Explanation: A gerund phrase consists of a gerund and its modifiers or complements. The entire phrase can function as a noun, as in “Walking along the river relaxes me.”
7. Choose the sentence in which the participle is used adjectivally.
A. Reading improves vocabulary.
B. She enjoys dancing.
C. The exhausted workers went home.
D. He avoided arguing.
Correct Answer: C. The exhausted workers went home.
Explanation: “Exhausted” is a past participle describing the noun “workers.” The other highlighted verb forms in A, B and D function as gerunds.
8. Which sentence contains a perfect participle?
A. Having finished the assignment, he went out.
B. Finishing the assignment is difficult.
C. He is finishing the assignment.
D. The finished assignment was submitted.
Correct Answer: A. Having finished the assignment, he went out.
Explanation: The perfect participle is formed with “having + past participle.” It indicates that one action was completed before another action occurred.
9. In which sentence does the -ing form function as the object of a preposition?
A. Running is healthy.
B. He is running fast.
C. He is interested in learning English.
D. The running train is late.
Correct Answer: C. He is interested in learning English.
Explanation: “Learning” is a gerund governed by the preposition “in.” A gerund, rather than a bare infinitive, normally follows a preposition when a verb is used as its complement.
10. Choose the correct statement about gerunds.
A. They always function as adjectives.
B. They always express completed actions.
C. They are used only after auxiliary verbs.
D. They are -ing forms that function as nouns.
Correct Answer: D. They are -ing forms that function as nouns.
Explanation: A gerund is a verb form with a noun-like function, although it can retain verbal properties such as taking an object. Its grammatical role, not its spelling alone, distinguishes it from a participle.
11. Identify the gerund phrase in “His constant complaining annoyed everyone.”
A. His constant complaining
B. Annoyed everyone
C. Constant
D. Everyone
Correct Answer: A. His constant complaining
Explanation: The phrase “His constant complaining” acts as the subject of the sentence. “Complaining” is the gerund, while “his” and “constant” modify it.
12. Which sentence contains a past participle used as part of a passive verb phrase?
A. The damaged car
B. The car was damaged in the accident.
C. Damaged by the storm, the roof collapsed.
D. The damage was severe.
Correct Answer: B. The car was damaged in the accident.
Explanation: “Was damaged” forms a passive verb phrase. In A and C, “damaged” functions adjectivally or participially rather than as the main verb of a finite passive clause.
13. Choose the correct sentence.
A. He is fond of to swim.
B. He is fond to swimming.
C. He is fond of swimming.
D. He is fond swim.
Correct Answer: C. He is fond of swimming.
Explanation: The adjective phrase “fond of” is followed by a noun or gerund. Therefore, “swimming” is correct, not “to swim.”
14. Which of the following contains a dangling participle?
A. Walking down the road, I met an old friend.
B. Having completed the work, she took a rest.
C. Exhausted by the journey, the travellers slept.
D. Walking down the road, the trees looked beautiful.
Correct Answer: D. Walking down the road, the trees looked beautiful.
Explanation: The sentence incorrectly suggests that the trees were walking down the road. A participial phrase should normally modify the subject performing the action: “Walking down the road, I thought the trees looked beautiful.”
15. Identify the gerund in “They discussed building a new bridge.”
A. Discussed
B. Building
C. New
D. Bridge
Correct Answer: B. Building
Explanation: “Building” is the object of the verb “discussed” and functions as a gerund. The noun phrase “a new bridge” is the object of the gerund.
16. Which sentence contains a participial phrase?
A. To learn English is useful.
B. Learning English is useful.
C. The man wearing a blue shirt is our teacher.
D. His learning of English impressed us.
Correct Answer: C. The man wearing a blue shirt is our teacher.
Explanation: “Wearing a blue shirt” is a participial phrase modifying “the man.” In B, “Learning English” functions as a gerund phrase and serves as the subject.
17. Which word is the past participle of “write”?
A. Wrote
B. Writing
C. Writes
D. Written
Correct Answer: D. Written
Explanation: The principal forms are “write–wrote–written.” The past participle is used in perfect tenses, passive constructions and participial modifiers.
18. Which sentence uses a gerund as the subject?
A. Travelling broadens the mind.
B. We are travelling tomorrow.
C. The travelling salesman arrived.
D. They travelled by train.
Correct Answer: A. Travelling broadens the mind.
Explanation: “Travelling” is the subject of the sentence and functions as a gerund. In B, it is part of a progressive-like arrangement with a future time reference.
19. Identify the present participle in “The audience watched the magician performing tricks.”
A. Audience
B. Watched
C. Performing
D. Tricks
Correct Answer: C. Performing
Explanation: “Performing tricks” describes the action observed by the audience and is a participial complement of “watched.” Here, “performing” is not the subject or object noun of the sentence.
20. Choose the correct sentence.
A. I look forward to meet you.
B. I look forward to meeting you.
C. I look forward meeting you.
D. I look forward to met you.
Correct Answer: B. I look forward to meeting you.
Explanation: In “look forward to,” the word “to” is a preposition, not an infinitive marker. It must therefore be followed by a noun or gerund: “meeting.”
21. Which sentence uses a gerund as the complement of a preposition?
A. The child is crying.
B. The crying child needs help.
C. Crying loudly, he left the room.
D. She succeeded by working hard.
Correct Answer: D. She succeeded by working hard.
Explanation: “Working” follows the preposition “by” and functions as a gerund. The phrase explains the means by which she succeeded.
22. Choose the correct past participle of “lie” in the sense of reclining.
A. Lain
B. Lied
C. Lay
D. Lying
Correct Answer: A. Lain
Explanation: The forms are “lie–lay–lain” when “lie” means to recline. “Lay” is the past tense, while “lied” is associated with telling an untruth.
23. Which sentence contains a gerund used as the subject complement?
A. She is swimming in the pool.
B. Her hobby is painting.
C. The painting is beautiful.
D. She painted the wall.
Correct Answer: B. Her hobby is painting.
Explanation: “Painting” identifies the hobby and functions as a subject complement after the linking verb “is.” It names an activity rather than describing the subject's temporary action.
24. Identify the participle in “The frightened child clung to his mother.”
A. Child
B. Clung
C. Frightened
D. Mother
Correct Answer: C. Frightened
Explanation: “Frightened” is a past participle used as an adjective modifying “child.” It describes the child's emotional state.
25. Which sentence contains both a gerund and a participle?
A. Swimming is enjoyable.
B. The broken chair was discarded.
C. She enjoys singing while cooking.
D. He decided to leave early.
Correct Answer: C. She enjoys singing while cooking.
Explanation: “Singing” is a gerund functioning as the object of “enjoys,” while “cooking” is a present participle describing the simultaneous action. Both forms end in -ing, but their grammatical functions differ.
Section 2: Gerunds After Verbs and Expressions (26–50)
26. Choose the correct option: “The officer avoided _____ the confidential information.”
A. to disclose
B. disclosing
C. disclose
D. disclosed
Correct Answer: B. disclosing
Explanation: “Avoid” is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive. Remember common verb patterns: avoid doing, enjoy doing, consider doing, and deny doing.
27. She admitted _____ the document without permission.
A. to copy
B. copy
C. copying
D. copied
Correct Answer: C. copying
Explanation: “Admit” is followed by a gerund when acknowledging an action. Example: “He admitted making a mistake.”
28. The manager suggested _____ the meeting until Monday.
A. postponing
B. to postpone
C. postponed
D. postpone
Correct Answer: A. postponing
Explanation: “Suggest” can be followed by a gerund or a that-clause, but not normally by a to-infinitive. Correct alternatives include “suggested postponing” and “suggested that we postpone.”
29. He denied _____ the money from the drawer.
A. to take
B. took
C. take
D. taking
Correct Answer: D. taking
Explanation: “Deny” takes a gerund as its complement. Use “denied taking,” not “denied to take.”
30. I cannot help _____ at his ridiculous explanation.
A. to laugh
B. laughing
C. laughed
D. laugh
Correct Answer: B. laughing
Explanation: “Cannot help” meaning “cannot avoid” is followed by a gerund. The expression “cannot help laughing” means that laughing is irresistible.
31. He postponed _____ the final decision.
A. to make
B. make
C. making
D. made
Correct Answer: C. making
Explanation: “Postpone” takes a gerund. The same pattern applies to “delay,” “put off,” and “defer.”
32. The student practised _____ English every day.
A. speaking
B. to speak
C. spoke
D. speak
Correct Answer: A. speaking
Explanation: When “practise” means to perform an activity repeatedly to improve a skill, it is followed by a gerund. Example: “She practises writing essays.”
33. They are considering _____ a new branch in Dhaka.
A. to open
B. opened
C. open
D. opening
Correct Answer: D. opening
Explanation: “Consider” meaning “think about” is followed by a gerund. “Consider opening” is the correct construction.
34. The witness kept _____ the same story.
A. to repeat
B. repeating
C. repeated
D. repeat
Correct Answer: B. repeating
Explanation: “Keep” meaning “continue” is followed by a gerund. “Kept repeating” indicates that the witness repeatedly told the same story.
35. Would you mind _____ the window?
A. open
B. to open
C. opening
D. opened
Correct Answer: C. opening
Explanation: “Would you mind” is followed by a gerund when making a polite request. The usual expression is “Would you mind opening the window?”
36. He narrowly escaped _____ in the accident.
A. being injured
B. to be injured
C. injured
D. to have injured
Correct Answer: A. being injured
Explanation: “Escape” is followed by a gerund. Because the subject would receive the injury, the passive gerund “being injured” is appropriate.
37. She resented _____ unfairly by her colleagues.
A. to treat
B. to be treated
C. treating
D. being treated
Correct Answer: D. being treated
Explanation: “Resent” takes a gerund, and the passive form is needed because the subject receives the action. Thus, “resented being treated unfairly” is correct.
38. He is accustomed to _____ early in the morning.
A. get up
B. getting up
C. have got up
D. got up
Correct Answer: B. getting up
Explanation: In “be accustomed to,” to is a preposition, so it takes a noun or gerund. Do not confuse this construction with “used to + base verb,” which refers to a past habit.
39. The government is committed to _____ public services.
A. improve
B. improved
C. improving
D. have improved
Correct Answer: C. improving
Explanation: “Committed to” is followed by a noun or gerund because to is a preposition here. The correct form is “committed to improving.”
40. He could not resist _____ the tempting offer.
A. accepting
B. to accept
C. accepted
D. accept
Correct Answer: A. accepting
Explanation: “Resist” is followed by a gerund. The phrase “could not resist accepting” means he was unable to refuse the offer.
41. She dislikes _____ in public.
A. to criticize
B. criticize
C. criticized
D. being criticized
Correct Answer: D. being criticized
Explanation: “Dislike” can take a gerund. The passive gerund “being criticized” is appropriate because the subject receives the criticism.
42. The company risked _____ its reputation.
A. to damage
B. damaging
C. damaged
D. damage
Correct Answer: B. damaging
Explanation: “Risk” is followed by a gerund, not a to-infinitive. Use “risk losing,” “risk making,” and “risk damaging.”
43. He confessed to _____ the rules.
A. break
B. to break
C. breaking
D. broke
Correct Answer: C. breaking
Explanation: In “confess to,” to is a preposition, so a gerund follows it. “Confessed to breaking the rules” means he admitted that he had broken them.
44. I distinctly remember _____ him at the conference last year.
A. meeting
B. to meet
C. meet
D. met
Correct Answer: A. meeting
Explanation: “Remember + gerund” refers to recalling a past event. Compare “remember to meet him,” which means remembering an obligation to meet him.
45. Please remember _____ the application before the deadline.
A. submitting
B. submitted
C. submit
D. to submit
Correct Answer: D. to submit
Explanation: “Remember + to-infinitive” means not to forget a future or intended action. This differs from “remember submitting,” which refers to recalling an action already performed.
46. I regret _____ you that your application has been rejected.
A. informing
B. to inform
C. informed
D. inform
Correct Answer: B. to inform
Explanation: “Regret + to-infinitive” is used when announcing unwelcome information. “Regret informing” would refer to feeling sorry about having informed someone.
47. He stopped _____ because the doctor advised him to do so.
A. to smoke
B. smoke
C. smoking
D. smoked
Correct Answer: C. smoking
Explanation: “Stop + gerund” means to cease an activity. “Stop to smoke” means to stop another activity in order to smoke.
48. He stopped _____ a cup of tea on his way to work.
A. to have
B. having
C. had
D. have
Correct Answer: A. to have
Explanation: “Stop + to-infinitive” expresses the purpose for stopping another activity. Here, he stopped his journey in order to have tea.
49. She went on _____ about her achievements for an hour.
A. to speak
B. speak
C. spoke
D. speaking
Correct Answer: D. speaking
Explanation: “Go on + gerund” means to continue the same activity. By contrast, “go on to speak” can mean proceeding to another action or topic.
50. After discussing the proposal, the committee proceeded _____ the final report.
A. preparing
B. to prepare
C. prepare
D. prepared
Correct Answer: B. to prepare
Explanation: “Proceed” is normally followed by a to-infinitive when it means to move on to the next action. Here, preparing the report is the next step.
Section 3: Gerunds After Prepositions and Fixed Expressions (51–75)
51. He succeeded _____ the examination despite several difficulties.
A. to pass
B. pass
C. in passing
D. for passing
Correct Answer: C. in passing
Explanation: The standard construction is “succeed in + gerund.” Example: “She succeeded in securing the position.”
52. She insisted _____ the bill herself.
A. on paying
B. to pay
C. pay
D. for paying
Correct Answer: A. on paying
Explanation: “Insist on” is followed by a noun or gerund. Use “insisted on paying,” not “insisted to pay.”
53. He apologized _____ the meeting late.
A. to attend
B. attend
C. for attend
D. for attending
Correct Answer: D. for attending
Explanation: “Apologize for” is followed by a noun or gerund when explaining the reason for an apology. “Apologized for attending late” refers to apologizing for the act of arriving late, though “apologized for arriving late” is more natural.
54. She is capable _____ complex financial problems.
A. to solve
B. of solving
C. solve
D. for solving
Correct Answer: B. of solving
Explanation: “Capable of” is followed by a noun or gerund. The correct structure is “capable of solving.”
55. There is no point _____ over a mistake that cannot be changed.
A. to worry
B. worry
C. worrying
D. worried
Correct Answer: C. worrying
Explanation: The expression “there is no point” is followed by a gerund. It means that an action is useless or serves no practical purpose.
56. It is worth _____ this proposal carefully.
A. considering
B. to consider
C. consider
D. considered
Correct Answer: A. considering
Explanation: “Worth” is followed by a gerund in this construction. Say “worth considering,” not “worth to consider.”
57. He left without _____ goodbye to anyone.
A. say
B. to say
C. said
D. saying
Correct Answer: D. saying
Explanation: “Without” is a preposition and therefore takes a noun or gerund. “Without saying goodbye” is the correct phrase.
58. Instead of _____ the problem, he blamed others.
A. to solve
B. solving
C. solved
D. solve
Correct Answer: B. solving
Explanation: “Instead of” is a prepositional expression followed by a noun or gerund. It introduces an alternative action.
59. He was accused _____ confidential documents.
A. to leak
B. leak
C. of leaking
D. for leaking
Correct Answer: C. of leaking
Explanation: The standard construction is “accuse someone of doing something.” Because the person receives the accusation, the passive form is “was accused of leaking.”
60. She devoted her life _____ the poor.
A. to helping
B. help
C. for help
D. to help
Correct Answer: A. to helping
Explanation: “Devote oneself or one's life to” takes a noun or gerund. Here, to is a preposition, so “helping” is required.
61. He objected _____ the proposal without proper discussion.
A. to reject
B. reject
C. for rejecting
D. to rejecting
Correct Answer: D. to rejecting
Explanation: “Object to” takes a noun or gerund. The to is a preposition, making “rejecting” correct.
62. She is looking forward to _____ from the bank.
A. hear
B. hearing
C. heard
D. have heard
Correct Answer: B. hearing
Explanation: “Look forward to” is followed by a gerund. The phrase expresses anticipation, not an action already completed.
63. The candidate was prevented _____ the examination.
A. to take
B. for taking
C. from taking
D. to taking
Correct Answer: C. from taking
Explanation: The correct passive construction is “prevented from + gerund.” For example, “He was prevented from entering the room.”
64. He has difficulty _____ technical instructions.
A. understanding
B. to understand
C. understand
D. understood
Correct Answer: A. understanding
Explanation: “Have difficulty” is commonly followed by a gerund. The phrase describes an action that is difficult for someone to perform.
65. She is busy _____ the annual report.
A. to prepare
B. prepare
C. prepared
D. preparing
Correct Answer: D. preparing
Explanation: “Busy” can be followed by a gerund to describe the activity occupying someone. “She is busy preparing the annual report” is the natural construction.
66. It is no use _____ with someone who refuses to listen.
A. to argue
B. arguing
C. argued
D. argue
Correct Answer: B. arguing
Explanation: “It is no use” is followed by a gerund. It means that the action is unlikely to produce a useful result.
67. He was punished for _____ the examination rules.
A. to violate
B. violate
C. violating
D. violated
Correct Answer: C. violating
Explanation: The preposition “for” takes a gerund here, explaining the reason for the punishment. The correct phrase is “punished for violating.”
68. She thanked me for _____ her with the application.
A. helping
B. to help
C. help
D. helped
Correct Answer: A. helping
Explanation: After the preposition “for,” use the gerund “helping.” The expression “thank someone for doing something” identifies the reason for gratitude.
69. He is tired of _____ the same complaint every day.
A. hear
B. to hear
C. heard
D. hearing
Correct Answer: D. hearing
Explanation: “Tired of” is followed by a noun or gerund. Here, “hearing” refers to the repeated experience that annoys him.
70. The officer was charged _____ accepting a bribe.
A. for
B. with
C. of
D. by
Correct Answer: B. with
Explanation: The standard expression is “charged with + noun or gerund.” Therefore, “charged with accepting a bribe” is correct.
71. He abstained _____ alcohol during the treatment.
A. to consume
B. consuming
C. from consuming
D. for consuming
Correct Answer: C. from consuming
Explanation: “Abstain from” means to deliberately avoid something. The preposition “from” is followed by the gerund “consuming.”
72. She is used to _____ under pressure.
A. working
B. work
C. worked
D. have worked
Correct Answer: A. working
Explanation: “Be used to” means to be accustomed to and is followed by a noun or gerund. Compare “used to work,” which describes a past habit.
73. He prevented the child _____ into the street.
A. to run
B. run
C. for running
D. from running
Correct Answer: D. from running
Explanation: The usual structure is “prevent someone from doing something.” The preposition “from” takes the gerund “running.”
74. There is no harm _____ the matter with the supervisor.
A. to discuss
B. in discussing
C. discuss
D. discussed
Correct Answer: B. in discussing
Explanation: “There is no harm in doing something” is a standard expression. The preposition “in” is followed by the gerund “discussing.”
75. He was on the verge of _____ his temper.
A. lose
B. to lose
C. losing
D. lost
Correct Answer: C. losing
Explanation: “On the verge of” means very close to doing something and is followed by a noun or gerund. Therefore, “on the verge of losing” is correct.
Section 4: Gerund or Infinitive? (76–100)
76. He decided _____ a new job.
A. seeking
B. to seek
C. seek
D. sought
Correct Answer: B. to seek
Explanation: “Decide” is normally followed by a to-infinitive. Remember “decide to do,” but “consider doing.”
77. She enjoys _____ classical music in the evening.
A. to listen
B. listened
C. listening to
D. listen
Correct Answer: C. listening to
Explanation: “Enjoy” takes a gerund. The verb “listen” requires the preposition “to” before the thing being heard.
78. I cannot afford _____ a new car at present.
A. to buy
B. buying
C. bought
D. buy
Correct Answer: A. to buy
Explanation: “Afford” is normally followed by a to-infinitive. The construction is “afford to do something.”
79. They agreed _____ the terms of the contract.
A. accepting
B. accept
C. accepted
D. to accept
Correct Answer: D. to accept
Explanation: “Agree” is followed by a to-infinitive when expressing consent or willingness. Thus, “agreed to accept” is correct.
80. He gave up _____ after several unsuccessful attempts.
A. to try
B. trying
C. tried
D. try
Correct Answer: B. trying
Explanation: “Give up” is a phrasal verb followed by a gerund. It means to stop attempting something.
81. The teacher encouraged us _____ independently.
A. thinking
B. think
C. to think
D. thought
Correct Answer: C. to think
Explanation: “Encourage someone to do something” requires an object followed by a to-infinitive. The correct pattern is “encouraged us to think.”
82. She avoided _____ a direct answer.
A. giving
B. to give
C. gave
D. give
Correct Answer: A. giving
Explanation: “Avoid” takes a gerund. It means deliberately keeping away from an action or situation.
83. He promised _____ the payment by Friday.
A. making
B. make
C. made
D. to make
Correct Answer: D. to make
Explanation: “Promise” is followed by a to-infinitive when expressing a commitment. “Promised to make the payment” is correct.
84. I would prefer _____ at home tonight rather than go out.
A. staying
B. to stay
C. stayed
D. stay
Correct Answer: B. to stay
Explanation: “Would prefer” commonly takes a to-infinitive. The construction “prefer to stay rather than go out” expresses a choice between alternatives.
85. She suggested that we _____ early.
A. leaving
B. to leave
C. leave
D. left
Correct Answer: C. leave
Explanation: In a that-clause after “suggest” meaning recommend, the base form is commonly used in formal English. “Suggested that we leave” is correct; “suggested us to leave” is not the standard construction.
86. He is considering _____ abroad for higher studies.
A. studying
B. to study
C. study
D. studied
Correct Answer: A. studying
Explanation: “Consider” takes a gerund when it means to think about a possibility. The correct phrase is “considering studying abroad.”
87. The officer refused _____ the confidential file.
A. opening
B. open
C. opened
D. to open
Correct Answer: D. to open
Explanation: “Refuse” takes a to-infinitive. It means to decline to do something.
88. He stopped _____ when the manager entered the room.
A. to talk
B. talking
C. talked
D. talk
Correct Answer: B. talking
Explanation: “Stop + gerund” means to cease the activity. Here, he ceased talking when the manager entered.
89. He stopped _____ to the receptionist on his way out.
A. talking
B. talked
C. to talk
D. talk
Correct Answer: C. to talk
Explanation: “Stop + to-infinitive” means to interrupt another activity for a particular purpose. He stopped what he was doing in order to speak to the receptionist.
90. She regrets _____ the confidential information to the press.
A. revealing
B. to reveal
C. reveal
D. revealed
Correct Answer: A. revealing
Explanation: “Regret + gerund” expresses sorrow about an action already performed. “Regret to inform” is used when announcing unwelcome news.
91. I regret _____ you that the position has been filled.
A. informing
B. informed
C. inform
D. to inform
Correct Answer: D. to inform
Explanation: “Regret + to-infinitive” is used for an unpleasant announcement. The speaker regrets having to give the information.
92. He attempted _____ the problem without assistance.
A. solving
B. to solve
C. solve
D. solved
Correct Answer: B. to solve
Explanation: “Attempt” is normally followed by a to-infinitive. “Attempt to solve” means to try to find a solution.
93. The child denied _____ the vase.
A. to break
B. break
C. breaking
D. broke
Correct Answer: C. breaking
Explanation: “Deny” is followed by a gerund when rejecting an allegation. “Denied breaking the vase” is the correct construction.
94. She learned _____ a computer at an early age.
A. to operate
B. operating
C. operate
D. operated
Correct Answer: A. to operate
Explanation: “Learn” is commonly followed by a to-infinitive to describe acquiring a skill. The sentence means that she acquired the ability to operate a computer.
95. He postponed _____ his resignation until the matter was settled.
A. to submit
B. submit
C. submitted
D. submitting
Correct Answer: D. submitting
Explanation: “Postpone” requires a gerund. Use “postpone doing something,” not “postpone to do something.”
96. She appears _____ the answer already.
A. knowing
B. to know
C. know
D. known
Correct Answer: B. to know
Explanation: “Appear” can be followed by a to-infinitive to express an impression or apparent fact. “Appears to know” is correct.

B. pay
C. to pay
D. paid
Correct Answer: A. paying
Explanation: In “object to,” to is a preposition and takes a gerund. Therefore, “objected to paying” is correct.
191. Which sentence contains a gerund as the object of a preposition?
A. Playing football is enjoyable.
B. The playing children were noisy.
C. She is interested in learning French.
D. She is learning French.
Correct Answer: C. She is interested in learning French.
Explanation: “Learning” is the object of the preposition “in.” In A, “playing” is the subject of the sentence, while in B it modifies “children.”
192. Choose the correct participle: “The _____ report revealed serious irregularities.”
A. investigating
B. investigated
C. investigate
D. investigative
Correct Answer: D. investigative
Explanation: “Investigative” is an adjective meaning concerned with investigation. The question tests the distinction between participles and related adjective forms; “the investigative report” is the natural expression.
193. Which sentence uses the -ing form as part of a continuous tense?
A. The crying child needs attention.
B. They are preparing for the examination.
C. Preparing for the examination requires discipline.
D. She enjoys preparing reports.
Correct Answer: B. They are preparing for the examination.
Explanation: “Are preparing” is the present progressive verb phrase, formed with “be + present participle.” In C and D, “preparing” is a gerund.
194. Choose the correct sentence.
A. I am looking forward to hear from you.
B. I am looking forward hear from you.
C. I am looking forward to hearing from you.
D. I am looking forward to heard from you.
Correct Answer: C. I am looking forward to hearing from you.
Explanation: “Look forward to” requires a noun or gerund because to is a preposition. “Hearing” is the correct form.
195. Identify the function of “to travel” in “To travel alone requires courage.”
A. Infinitive phrase functioning as the subject
B. Gerund functioning as the object
C. Present participle modifying a noun
D. Past participle forming a passive verb
Correct Answer: A. Infinitive phrase functioning as the subject
Explanation: “To travel alone” is a to-infinitive phrase functioning as the subject. Although it names an activity, it is not a gerund because it uses the infinitive form.
196. Choose the correct completion: “The witness claimed _____ the suspect near the bank.”
A. seeing
B. saw
C. seen
D. to have seen
Correct Answer: D. to have seen
Explanation: “Claim” can be followed by a perfect infinitive to refer to an earlier event. “Claimed to have seen” indicates that the alleged sighting occurred before the claim was made.
197. Which sentence contains a gerund with a direct object?
A. The sleeping child was peaceful.
B. She enjoys reading historical novels.
C. The child is sleeping peacefully.
D. The broken glass was removed.
Correct Answer: B. She enjoys reading historical novels.
Explanation: “Reading” is a gerund, and “historical novels” is its direct object. Gerunds retain the ability of verbs to take objects.
198. Choose the correct sentence.
A. Having been complete the training, he received a certificate.
B. Having completing the training, he received a certificate.
C. Having completed the training, he received a certificate.
D. Having been completing the training, he received a certificate.
Correct Answer: C. Having completed the training, he received a certificate.
Explanation: “Having completed” is the perfect participle, showing that the training ended before the certificate was received. The passive form is unnecessary because the subject completed the training.
199. Identify the grammatically correct sentence.
A. He is accustomed to working under pressure.
B. He is accustomed to work under pressure.
C. He is accustomed working under pressure.
D. He accustomed to working under pressure.
Correct Answer: A. He is accustomed to working under pressure.
Explanation: “Be accustomed to” takes a noun or gerund, and the verb “be” is required in this construction. The correct sentence is “He is accustomed to working under pressure.”
200. Choose the correct sentence containing both a gerund and a participle.
A. Reading books improves vocabulary.
B. The broken window needs repair.
C. She enjoys reading while travelling by train.
D. To read regularly is beneficial.
Correct Answer: C. She enjoys reading while travelling by train.
Explanation: “Reading” is a gerund serving as the object of “enjoys,” while “travelling” is a present participle indicating the accompanying circumstance. The sentence tests the distinction between grammatical function and the shared -ing ending.
