Directions: Identify the part containing the grammatical error. If the sentence is correct, choose D (No error).

B. have submitted
C. the application form before the deadline
D. No error
Correct Answer: B. have submitted
Explanation: “Each” takes a singular verb. Correct: “Each of the candidates has submitted the application form.”
