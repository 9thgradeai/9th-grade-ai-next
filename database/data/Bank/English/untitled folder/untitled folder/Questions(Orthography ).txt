Topic: Orthography — Correct Spelling Identification
Total MCQs: 250
Target: Bangladesh Bank and similar bank recruitment exams
This question bank focuses on identifying the single correctly spelled word among four highly similar alternatives, covering advanced vocabulary, commonly confused spellings, doubled consonants, vowel sequences, suffixes, prefixes, and frequently tested competitive-exam words. The difficulty ranges from intermediate to challenging.
