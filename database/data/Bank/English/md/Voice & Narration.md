# Bank English · Voice & Narration

Q: *Change the voice:* The central bank must have adjusted the repo rate before the inflation spiked.
A) The repo rate must have been adjusted by the central bank before the inflation spiked.
B) The repo rate must be adjusted by the central bank before the inflation spiked.
C) The repo rate had to be adjusted by the central bank before the inflation spiked.
D) The repo rate must have had adjusted by the central bank before the inflation spiked.
Ans: A
Exp: **Speed Shortcut:** Modal + have + V3 (Active) ➔ Modal + have + been + V3 (Passive). Only A uses "must have been adjusted".

Q: *Change the voice:* Who authorized this uncollateralized loan?
A) By whom was this uncollateralized loan authorized?
B) By whom this uncollateralized loan was authorized?
C) Who was this uncollateralized loan authorized by?
D) Both A and C
Ans: D
Exp: **Speed Shortcut:** Who ➔ By whom + Aux + Subject + V3 OR Who + Aux + Subject + V3 + by. Both A and C follow standard passive interrogative structures. B lacks verb inversion.

Q: *Change the narration:* The auditor said to the manager, "Why didn't you report the discrepancy yesterday?"
A) The auditor asked the manager why he hadn't reported the discrepancy the previous day.
B) The auditor asked the manager why didn't he report the discrepancy the previous day.
C) The auditor asked the manager why he hasn't reported the discrepancy the day before.
D) The auditor asked the manager that why he hadn't reported the discrepancy yesterday.
Ans: A
Exp: **Speed Shortcut:** Didn't (Past Indefinite) ➔ Hadn't (Past Perfect). Interrogative structures become assertive (why he hadn't). "Yesterday" ➔ "the previous day".

Q: *Change the voice:* People expect that the stock market will rebound in the next quarter.
A) It is expected that the stock market will rebound in the next quarter.
B) The stock market is expected to rebound in the next quarter.
C) The stock market was expected to rebound in the next quarter.
D) Both A and B
Ans: D
Exp: **Speed Shortcut:** Complex sentences with "People expect/say" have two passive forms: It is expected that... OR Subject + is expected to + V1.

Q: *Change the narration:* The client said to the teller, "I must withdraw the funds today."

Q: *Change the voice:* The board made him the Chief Financial Officer.

B) The Chief Financial Officer was made him by the board.

Exp: **Speed Shortcut:** "Chief Financial Officer" is a factitive object (complement). Factitive objects cannot become the passive subject. Only "him" (he) can be the subject. Drop "as" (Option D trap).
