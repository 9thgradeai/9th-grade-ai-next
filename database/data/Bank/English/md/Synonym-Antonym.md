# Bank English · Synonym-Antonym

Q: Choose the word that is most nearly **SIMILAR** in meaning to the word **AMELIORATE**:
A) Worsen
B) Improve
C) Mitigate
D) Complicate
Ans: B
Exp: *Ameliorate* means to make something bad better. "Improve" is its direct synonym (Latin *melior* = better). Option C (Mitigate) means to lessen severity, but "improve" is the direct equivalent.

Q: Choose the word that is most nearly **OPPOSITE** in meaning to the word **EPHEMERAL**:
A) Transient
B) Fleeting
C) Perennial
D) Momentary
Ans: C
Exp: *Ephemeral* means lasting a very short time (fleeting, transient). Its antonym must mean lasting a long time or forever; *perennial* (lasting through the years) fits perfectly.

Q: Choose the word that is most nearly **SIMILAR** in meaning to the word **OBFUSCATE**:
A) Clarify
B) Confuse
C) Illuminate
D) Simplify
Ans: B
Exp: *Obfuscate* means to render obscure, unclear, or incomprehensible. Think of "obscure/fogs". It matches "confuse".

Q: Choose the word that is most nearly **OPPOSITE** in meaning to the word **RECALCITRANT**:
