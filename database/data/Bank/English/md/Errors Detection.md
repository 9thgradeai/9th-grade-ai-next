# Bank English · Errors Detection

Q: Identify the segment containing an error in the following sentence: "The managing director, as well as the department heads, are going to review the new fiscal policies tomorrow."
A) The managing director, as well as
B) the department heads,
C) are going to review
D) the new fiscal policies tomorrow.
Ans: C
Exp: Connectors like *as well as* do not make the subject plural. The verb agrees with the first subject ("director" = singular). Change *are* to *is*.

Q: Identify the segment containing an error in the following sentence: "The newly appointed auditor is not only highly experienced but also has a lot of dedication to corporate transparency."
A) The newly appointed auditor
B) is not only highly experienced
C) but also has a lot of dedication
D) to corporate transparency.
Ans: C
Exp: *Not only... but also* must connect parallel parts of speech. "Highly experienced" is an adjective phrase; "has a lot of dedication" is a verb phrase. Change to *but also highly dedicated*.

Q: Identify the segment containing an error in the following sentence: "The yield on government bonds in the current quarter is significantly higher than corporate bonds."

B) in the current quarter
C) is significantly higher than
D) corporate bonds.
Ans: D
Exp: Faulty Comparison. You cannot compare "yield" directly to "corporate bonds". It must be *higher than THAT OF corporate bonds*.
