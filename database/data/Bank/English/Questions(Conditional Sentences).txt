Section 1: Fundamentals and Types of Conditionals (1–25)
1. If water reaches 100°C at standard atmospheric pressure, it _____.
A. boiled
B. boils
C. will boil
D. would boil
Correct Answer: B. boils
Explanation: The zero conditional expresses general truths and scientific facts. Its usual structure is if + present simple, present simple. Example: If ice melts, it becomes water.
2. If it rains tomorrow, we _____ the match.
A. cancel
B. cancelled
C. will cancel
D. would cancel
Correct Answer: C. will cancel
Explanation: The first conditional describes a realistic future possibility. It normally follows if + present simple, will + base verb. Do not usually use will in the if-clause.
3. If I were rich, I _____ a hospital for the poor.
A. build
B. will build
C. would build
D. had built
Correct Answer: C. would build
Explanation: The second conditional describes an unreal or unlikely present or future situation. Its standard structure is if + past simple, would + base verb.
4. If she had studied harder, she _____ the examination.
A. would pass
B. will pass
C. would have passed
D. passed
Correct Answer: C. would have passed
Explanation: The third conditional describes an unreal past situation and its imagined result. The structure is if + past perfect, would have + past participle.
5. If you heat ice, it _____.
A. melted
B. melts
C. would melt
D. will have melted
Correct Answer: B. melts
Explanation: This is a zero conditional because it expresses a general scientific fact. Both clauses use the present simple.
6. If he hurries, he _____ the train.
A. catches
B. would catch
C. caught
D. will catch
Correct Answer: D. will catch
Explanation: The sentence describes a possible future result. The present simple hurries appears in the if-clause, while will catch expresses the expected result.
7. If I _____ you, I would accept the offer.
A. am
B. was
C. were
D. had been
Correct Answer: C. were
Explanation: In formal hypothetical statements, were is conventionally used with all subjects in the second conditional. The expression If I were you is especially common in advice.
8. If they had left earlier, they _____ the flight.
A. would not miss
B. would not have missed
C. will not miss
D. did not miss
Correct Answer: B. would not have missed
Explanation: The third conditional imagines a different past and its consequence. The negative result takes would not have + past participle.
9. If you mix blue and yellow, you _____ green.
A. get
B. got
C. would get
D. will have got
Correct Answer: A. get
Explanation: A zero conditional is used for predictable facts and general results. Both the condition and result are expressed in the present simple.
10. If he does not work hard, he _____ the competition.
A. would lose
B. lost
C. will lose
D. would have lost
Correct Answer: C. will lose
Explanation: This is a first conditional expressing a possible future consequence. The negative present simple appears in the if-clause, followed by will + base verb.
11. If I had enough money, I _____ a new laptop.
A. will buy
B. would buy
C. would have bought
D. bought
Correct Answer: B. would buy
Explanation: The second conditional describes a hypothetical present situation. Had means “possessed” here and is the past simple form of have.
12. If she had taken the medicine, she _____ better now.
A. would feel
B. will feel
C. would have felt
D. felt
Correct Answer: A. would feel
Explanation: This is a mixed conditional: an unreal past condition has a present result. The if-clause uses the past perfect, while the main clause uses would + base verb.
13. If I knew his address, I _____ him yesterday.
A. would visit
B. will visit
C. would have visited
D. visited
Correct Answer: C. would have visited
Explanation: This is a mixed conditional with a present condition and an unreal past result. Not knowing his address now explains why the visit did not happen yesterday.
14. If the temperature falls below zero, water _____ into ice.
A. turned
B. would turn
C. turns
D. will have turned
Correct Answer: C. turns
Explanation: The zero conditional expresses a general scientific relationship. The present simple is appropriate in both clauses.
15. If you invite her, she _____ to the party.
A. came
B. would come
C. will come
D. had come
Correct Answer: C. will come
Explanation: This is a first conditional because the invitation and attendance are realistic future possibilities. Use will + base verb in the result clause.
16. If he _____ more careful, he would not make so many mistakes.
A. is
B. were
C. had been
D. will be
Correct Answer: B. were
Explanation: The second conditional expresses an unreal or unlikely present situation. The hypothetical condition takes the past simple; were is preferred in formal English.
17. If they had informed me, I _____ them.
A. help
B. will help
C. would help
D. would have helped
Correct Answer: D. would have helped
Explanation: The third conditional concerns an unreal past condition and result. Had informed is past perfect, and would have helped is the correct result construction.
18. If you press this button, the machine _____.
A. starts
B. started
C. would start
D. would have started

Explanation: This is a zero conditional describing how a machine normally operates. The present simple is used for the condition and its predictable result.
19. If it were not raining, we _____ for a walk.

B. will go
C. would go
D. would have gone

Explanation: The sentence describes an unreal present situation: it is raining, so the walk is not happening. The second conditional uses were not and would + base verb.
20. If he had followed the instructions, the device _____ properly.

B. would work

Explanation: The sentence imagines a different past outcome. The third conditional requires would have + past participle in the result clause.
21. If I were more confident, I _____ for the post.

B. will apply
C. would apply
D. would have applied
Correct Answer: C. would apply
Explanation: The second conditional is used for an imagined present characteristic and a possible future consequence. The past form were does not indicate past time here.
22. If the alarm rings, everyone _____ the building.
A. evacuated
B. would evacuate
C. will evacuate
D. had evacuated
Correct Answer: C. will evacuate
Explanation: The first conditional expresses a possible future event and its expected result. The if-clause uses the present simple rings.
23. If he had not wasted his time, he _____ the work by now.
A. would finish
B. will finish
C. would have finished
D. finished
Correct Answer: C. would have finished
Explanation: This is a mixed-time interpretation: a past failure to use time wisely affects completion by the present. Would have finished expresses the unreal result, with by now marking the present deadline.
24. If people exercise regularly, they generally _____ healthier.
A. became
B. become
C. would become
D. will have become
Correct Answer: B. become
Explanation: The sentence states a general tendency rather than one specific future event. The zero conditional commonly uses the present simple in both clauses.
25. If you had told me about the problem, I _____ you.
A. would help
B. helped
C. will help
D. would have helped
Correct Answer: D. would have helped
Explanation: This is a third conditional because the opportunity to tell and help belongs to the past. The result clause takes would have + past participle.

C. will submit
