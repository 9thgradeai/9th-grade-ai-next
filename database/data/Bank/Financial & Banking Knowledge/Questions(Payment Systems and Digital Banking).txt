Questions 1–25: BACPS, BEFTN, NPSB and BD-RTGS
1. What is the primary function of the Bangladesh Automated Cheque Processing System (BACPS)?
A. Processing international SWIFT messages
B. Automating the clearing of paper cheques using cheque images and data
C. Issuing mobile financial service accounts
D. Managing government securities auctions
Correct Answer: B. Automating the clearing of paper cheques using cheque images and data
Explanation: BACPS uses cheque imaging and truncation technology to process paper-based cheques electronically, reducing the need for physical cheque transportation.
2. Which payment system is primarily associated with electronic credit transfers and direct debits in Bangladesh?
A. BACPS
B. NPSB
C. BEFTN
D. SWIFT
Correct Answer: C. BEFTN
Explanation: The Bangladesh Electronic Funds Transfer Network (BEFTN) facilitates eligible electronic credit and debit transfers, including payroll, remittances and bill payments.
3. What is the principal purpose of the National Payment Switch Bangladesh (NPSB)?
A. Facilitating interoperability among participating banks' electronic payment channels
B. Printing negotiable instruments
C. Regulating insurance premiums
D. Replacing all bank deposit accounts with mobile wallets
Correct Answer: A. Facilitating interoperability among participating banks' electronic payment channels
Explanation: NPSB connects participating institutions for supported electronic transactions, including ATM, POS and internet banking fund transfers.
4. Which system is designed primarily for high-value, time-critical interbank payments settled individually in real time?
A. BACPS
B. BEFTN
C. NPSB
D. BD-RTGS
Correct Answer: D. BD-RTGS
Explanation: Bangladesh Real-Time Gross Settlement (BD-RTGS) processes eligible payment instructions individually in real time rather than waiting for net settlement at the end of a processing cycle.
5. In the context of payment systems, what does RTGS stand for?
A. Retail Transaction Guarantee Service
B. Real-Time Gross Settlement
C. Regional Transfer and Guarantee Scheme
D. Real-Time General Switching
Correct Answer: B. Real-Time Gross Settlement
Explanation: RTGS stands for Real-Time Gross Settlement. “Real-time” refers to processing payments individually as they are received, while “gross” means that transactions are settled individually rather than offset against one another.
6. Which statement best describes gross settlement?
A. Payments are settled individually rather than accumulated into a net amount for settlement
B. All payments are settled only at the end of the month
C. Only cheque payments can be processed
D. Every payment is settled through a physical cash transfer
Correct Answer: A. Payments are settled individually rather than accumulated into a net amount for settlement
Explanation: Gross settlement handles each transaction individually. In contrast, net settlement offsets eligible incoming and outgoing obligations before settling the resulting net amount.
7. A bank receives many payment instructions throughout the day. Under net settlement, the settlement obligation is generally calculated by:
A. Adding only the bank's incoming payments
B. Settling every instruction separately in central bank money
C. Offsetting eligible incoming and outgoing obligations
D. Converting all transactions into foreign currency
Correct Answer: C. Offsetting eligible incoming and outgoing obligations
Explanation: Net settlement calculates the difference between eligible incoming and outgoing payment obligations. This can reduce liquidity requirements compared with settling every transaction individually.
8. Which of the following is the most appropriate example of a retail electronic funds transfer?
A. A central bank's foreign-exchange reserve valuation
B. A bank's capital adequacy calculation
C. A securities exchange's annual report
D. A company electronically paying employees' salaries into their bank accounts
Correct Answer: D. A company electronically paying employees' salaries into their bank accounts
Explanation: Electronic salary payments are a common application of electronic funds transfer (EFT), allowing employers to transfer money directly into employees' accounts without distributing physical cash.
9. Which statement best distinguishes clearing from settlement?
A. Clearing determines or processes payment obligations; settlement discharges those obligations by transferring funds
B. Clearing always means cash withdrawal, whereas settlement means depositing cash
C. Clearing is exclusively international, whereas settlement is exclusively domestic
D. The two terms always mean exactly the same thing
Correct Answer: A. Clearing determines or processes payment obligations; settlement discharges those obligations by transferring funds
Explanation: Clearing involves processing payment instructions and determining obligations. Settlement is the discharge of those obligations through the transfer of funds.
10. Which institution is principally responsible for overseeing Bangladesh's national payment systems?
A. Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission
B. Bangladesh Bank
C. Department of Immigration and Passports
D. Insurance Development and Regulatory Authority
Correct Answer: B. Bangladesh Bank
Explanation: Bangladesh Bank plays a central role in payment-system regulation and oversight in Bangladesh, including the development and supervision of relevant payment infrastructure.
11. A business needs to transfer a large payment urgently between two participating banks. Which system is conceptually the best fit, subject to applicable rules and access?
A. A paper-based cheque collection process only
B. A monthly salary-processing batch
C. BD-RTGS
D. A card's contactless payment feature
Correct Answer: C. BD-RTGS
Explanation: BD-RTGS is designed for eligible payments requiring real-time gross settlement. Actual availability depends on the system's operating rules, participating institutions and applicable transaction requirements.
12. What is the principal benefit of automating cheque processing through image-based clearing?
A. It eliminates the need for bank accounts
B. It guarantees that every cheque has sufficient funds
C. It removes every possibility of cheque fraud
D. It reduces physical cheque movement and can speed up processing
Correct Answer: D. It reduces physical cheque movement and can speed up processing
Explanation: Image-based cheque clearing reduces the need to transport physical cheques between banks. However, it does not guarantee that a cheque is genuine or that sufficient funds are available.
13. BEFTN is best described as a system supporting:
A. Only international securities trading
B. Electronic fund transfers processed under applicable system rules
C. The printing of debit cards
D. The physical delivery of banknotes between branches
Correct Answer: B. Electronic fund transfers processed under applicable system rules
Explanation: BEFTN supports eligible electronic credit and debit transfers. It is not a securities-trading platform or a physical cash-delivery service.
14. Which situation most clearly illustrates interoperability in digital payments?
A. A customer can use a supported payment channel across different participating institutions
B. Every bank uses a separate, incompatible payment network
C. A bank refuses transactions initiated by other participating institutions
D. A customer can transact only at the branch where an account was opened
Correct Answer: A. A customer can use a supported payment channel across different participating institutions
Explanation: Interoperability enables compatible payment systems operated by different participating institutions to communicate and process supported transactions.
15. Which statement about BD-RTGS is most accurate?
A. It is principally a cheque-image capture system
B. It is a telecommunications protocol for exchanging bank messages
C. It supports real-time gross settlement of eligible payment instructions
D. It is exclusively a mobile wallet application
Correct Answer: C. It supports real-time gross settlement of eligible payment instructions
Explanation: BD-RTGS is a settlement system for eligible payments. It is distinct from cheque-image processing, financial messaging and mobile wallet applications.
16. Why are settlement finality and clear settlement rules important?
A. They allow banks to ignore failed transactions
B. They eliminate the need for liquidity management
C. They guarantee that no participant will ever default
D. They establish when payment obligations are considered irrevocably discharged under the applicable rules
Correct Answer: D. They establish when payment obligations are considered irrevocably discharged under the applicable rules
Explanation: Settlement-finality rules provide certainty about when obligations are discharged. This reduces legal uncertainty and helps participants manage settlement risk.
17. Which is a major risk reduced by timely, reliable interbank settlement arrangements?
A. All market and credit risks in the economy
B. The possibility that a payment obligation remains unsettled and creates further obligations or losses
C. The risk of every customer forgetting a PIN
D. All risks associated with lending to businesses
Correct Answer: B. The possibility that a payment obligation remains unsettled and creates further obligations or losses
Explanation: Reliable settlement arrangements help limit the consequences of failed or delayed payments. They do not eliminate every financial or operational risk.
18. A payment instruction is accepted by a bank's front-end application but has not yet been settled. What should an examiner conclude?
A. Acceptance alone does not prove final settlement

C. The transaction must have been settled in cash
D. The payment can no longer fail under any circumstances

Explanation: Acceptance by a front-end application is a processing event, not proof of final interbank settlement or irrevocable beneficiary credit.

C. Processing eligible payment instructions, often in batches

Correct Answer: C. Processing eligible payment instructions, often in batches
Explanation: Automated clearing house arrangements commonly process eligible payment instructions in batches. The timing and settlement model depend on the particular system.
20. What is a settlement account used for in an interbank payment arrangement?

22. A bank has insufficient settlement funds to complete an otherwise valid urgent payment. Which risk is most directly implicated?
A. Liquidity risk
B. Spelling risk
C. Inventory obsolescence risk
D. Depreciation risk
Correct Answer: A. Liquidity risk
Explanation: Liquidity risk arises when a bank cannot meet its payment obligations when due because it lacks sufficient immediately available funds, even if it remains solvent overall.
23. Why may a retail payment system use deferred net settlement instead of settling every instruction individually in real time?
A. To ensure that all payments remain anonymous
B. To eliminate transaction records
C. To aggregate eligible obligations and settle net positions, potentially reducing liquidity needs
D. To convert domestic transfers into international remittances
Correct Answer: C. To aggregate eligible obligations and settle net positions, potentially reducing liquidity needs
Explanation: Deferred net settlement offsets eligible payment obligations before settlement. This can reduce liquidity requirements, although the system must manage the risks associated with delayed settlement.

25. A beneficiary's bank account has been credited provisionally while a payment remains subject to applicable processing or reversal rules. Which conclusion is safest?

Correct Answer: B. The customer-facing credit and interbank settlement finality must be assessed separately
Explanation: A provisional customer-account credit does not necessarily establish final interbank settlement. The transaction's status must be assessed under the applicable payment-system and account rules.
Questions 26–45: Mobile Financial Services and Agent Banking

C. To eliminate all bank accounts
D. To restrict financial services to urban customers
Correct Answer: B. To provide convenient, accessible financial services through mobile-based channels
Explanation: MFS uses mobile-based channels to facilitate financial transactions and improve access to services, particularly for underserved populations.

C. An individual paying a merchant for goods

Correct Answer: C. An individual paying a merchant for goods
Explanation: P2B refers to payments made by individuals to businesses, such as paying a shopkeeper or merchant using a mobile financial service.

A. An employer transferring salaries to employees' mobile accounts
B. A customer paying a shopkeeper
C. An individual transferring money to a friend
D. A customer paying personal income tax
Correct Answer: A. An employer transferring salaries to employees' mobile accounts
Explanation: B2P payments involve a business paying an individual. Salary disbursement to employees is a common example.
29. Under the Bangladesh Mobile Financial Services Regulations, 2022, which institution has a central regulatory role in MFS?
A. Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission
B. Dhaka Stock Exchange
C. Bangladesh Insurance Development and Regulatory Authority
D. Bangladesh Bank
Correct Answer: D. Bangladesh Bank
Explanation: Bangladesh Bank issued the Mobile Financial Services Regulations, 2022, and has a central role in regulating MFS activities in Bangladesh.
30. Which practice is most important when opening an MFS account?
A. Allowing anonymous transactions without any restrictions
B. Complying with applicable customer identification and KYC requirements
C. Sharing the customer's PIN with the agent
D. Accepting fabricated identity information
Correct Answer: B. Complying with applicable customer identification and KYC requirements
Explanation: Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures help establish customer identity, reduce impersonation and support anti-money-laundering controls.
31. What is the primary function of an MFS agent?
A. To issue sovereign currency
B. To independently determine national monetary policy
C. To perform authorized services on behalf of an MFS provider
D. To guarantee every customer's investment return
Correct Answer: C. To perform authorized services on behalf of an MFS provider
Explanation: An MFS agent performs permitted services on behalf of an authorized provider. The agent cannot independently exercise the powers of a central bank or guarantee investment returns.
32. Which statement best distinguishes agent banking from MFS?

Correct Answer: A. Agent banking enables authorized agents to provide specified banking services on behalf of a bank, whereas MFS delivers financial services through a mobile-based account and service framework
Explanation: Agent banking extends permitted bank services through authorized agents. MFS uses a mobile-based service framework. The two models can overlap in cash-in and cash-out services but are not identical.
