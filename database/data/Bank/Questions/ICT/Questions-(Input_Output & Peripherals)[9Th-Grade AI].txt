1. A Bangladesh Bank clearinghouse processes automated cheque routing. Which specific technology enables the reading of routing numbers even if optical ink stamps overlap them?
A) OCR
B) MICR
C) OMR
D) RFID
Ans: B | Speed Solution: MICR (Magnetic Ink Character Recognition) reads magnetic properties of iron oxide ink, ignoring standard optical ink overlaps.

C) CMC-7
D) ISO-Magnetic
