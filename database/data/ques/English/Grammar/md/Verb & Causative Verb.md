# English Grammar · Verb & Causative Verb

Q: Choose the correct sentence.
A) To help others is a great deed.
B) To help
C) To helping
D) To have helped
Ans: A
Exp: An infinitive phrase ("To help") functions correctly as the subject of the sentence.
