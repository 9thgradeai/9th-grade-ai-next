# Product

## What is 9Th-Grade AI?

9Th-Grade AI is a free, open-source AI-powered exam preparation platform for Bangladeshi government job aspirants. It targets competitive recruitment exams including **BCS Preliminary**, **Bangladesh Bank**, **Assistant Director**, and other **9th-grade (pay-scale) government posts**.

There is no pricing, no paid tier, no premium gating — every feature is available to everyone.

## Target Users

- Bangladeshi students preparing for BCS Preliminary exams.
- Candidates preparing for Bangladesh Bank recruitment exams.
- Aspirants targeting Assistant Director and other 9th-grade pay-scale government posts.
- Users who need a unified platform combining syllabus, question bank, mock tests, flashcards, study planning, and AI tutoring.

## Non-Goals

- No paid tiers or premium gating.
- No mobile-native apps (responsive web only).
- No social features beyond leaderboards.
- No real-time multi-user collaboration.
